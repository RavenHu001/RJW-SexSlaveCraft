## English

**Develop pawns, choose their roles, manage their relationships, and preserve their personalities across bodies.**

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult **RimJobWorld** expansion built around long-term pawn progression. Training, bonds, specialization, body modification and Personality Gel connect individual development with colony life.

**Version support:** the **2.4.8** package supports **RimWorld 1.7 and 1.6**. The features below describe **1.7**; **1.6 retains frozen SSC 2.4.1 content**. Includes Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian text.

### Train, bond and progress

Assign trainers, issue manual orders or use eligible self-training to develop Corruption and body-part progress. **Binding Rituals** advance Chain stages and unlock further controls. Bonds also affect shared beds, mood and colony bonuses; the bond overview helps you locate members and manage trainer assignments.

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification. Personally completing Training and Rituals develops four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire**, with a Master panel for growth and damage sharing.

Eligible trainers can be explicitly assigned with Training work enabled in the same action, or enabled in place; new enabling uses priority 3 and preserves an already enabled priority.

### Choose from seven specializations

- **Public Use:** Trade and social benefits, plus additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Increased milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualified pawns can train others for their Master.
- **Pet Cat:** Mobility and melee evasion; final **Gentle Comfort** calms eligible mental states or grants mood/work benefits.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Animal Training** tames an eligible wild animal or completes an owned animal's training project.
- **Combatant:** Combat and survival bonuses, with final **Combat Overdrive**.
- **Broodmother:** Staged fertility/care/Beauty benefits, preconception settings and final **Conception** after a completed scene with an eligible adult target. New selection requires Chain stage 3 and completed uterine development.

Each direction retains its own progress. **Final forms require completed specialization development, Personality Gel processing and implantation.**

### Set personal rules and manage relationships

Configure activity permissions, romance behavior and partner categories per pawn. Chain stages unlock initiative controls, response controls and **one-time orders** for courtship, proposals and ending relationships. Optional acceptance guarantees default to off; prerequisites still apply, and interruption ends an order.

Romance events produce contextual mood, personal logs, history and task feedback. Separate **Restore Defaults** buttons reset behavior or romance preferences while preserving relationships and memories. Eligible adult bound slaves receive voluntary attraction support toward their actual Master; the Master's willingness and other interaction requirements remain in force.

Individual panels provide eligible controls for **Bestow Bliss, damage sharing, milk production, fertility, gel rendering and PNA production**.

### Develop the body and carry the personality forward

Research and surgery start **uterine and stomach development**, followed by eligible Training. Completed uterine development offers fertility control, increased fertility while enabled and shorter eligible new pregnancies; fertility starts off. Stomach development improves hunger and eating efficiency, ending in food-poisoning immunity during normal eating.

Optional **body sensitivity**, off by default, unlocks four levels through Chain stages: **×2 / ×4 / ×8 / ×16** frequency factors, with increased vulnerability and stage-dependent mood. Saved and effective levels are shown separately. The general **Orgasm Frequency Multiplier** also combines PNA factors and staged body-development offsets; switching off personal sensitivity leaves other valid sources active.

Further options include permanent lactation, limb modification and full gelatinization. Fully gelatinized pawns can adjust their own **color, opacity and rendering**.

Extract **Personality Gel**, process it and implant it into an eligible **Hollow**. Personality-based growth, Master qualification/progression and romance preferences continue with the personality. Uterine/stomach development, sensitivity, PNA production choices and gel appearance stay with the body.

Three-state personality imprints follow Seed/bond qualification and personality transfer; differentiated forms lock their corresponding role. Current Masters cannot undergo personality extraction. Preconception choices stay with the uterine body; the completed Hollow state disables six comfort/environment needs while retaining its other effects. Replicas are a separate deferred project.

### Produce PNA, equip pawns and shape colony life

Completed genital Training enables ordinary **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** production. Personal **pause/resume** preserves batch progress; the panel shows accumulation, disabled status and insufficient nutrition. Concentration research opens drug-lab processing and a less efficient campfire/stove route.

PNA acts as a drug on ordinary pawns and provides beneficial activation to eligible Chain-bound bodies; current Masters are immune to its drug effects. Optional **Bestow Bliss**, off by default, supplies PNA and limited development benefits after eligible paired interactions.

**Nine PNA weapons span medieval, industrial and spacer technology.** Coated weapons cause real wounds; spacer main attacks allow eligible Chain-bound bodies to absorb PNA harmlessly and repair injuries. Shields and cover still matter. Finished weapons need no separate ammunition or refills.

With Ideology, **Master-slave Order** adds **28 precepts**, cultural feedback, dedicated icons, naming presets and dynamic backgrounds. Binding Rituals can optionally award ideology development points under vanilla conditions; this defaults to off. **Cosmetic surgery and Reshaping Rituals** use PNA and medicine to improve vanilla Beauty one step per success, up to **+2**, with recovery between treatments.

### Requirements and compatibility

- Load matching **Harmony** and **RimJobWorld** before SSC.
- **Ideology** is required for Binding Rituals, Master-slave Order and Reshaping Rituals. **Royalty is optional**; when enabled, Training research also requires techprints.
- Training targets need SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.
- Supported **UAP** manual group requests work with SSC restrictions enabled. A separate manual permission override defaults to off; native matching/body conditions remain. Unfinished group sessions are canceled on load.

Back up saves, close the game and **replace the whole old mod folder**, retaining both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. Saves referencing the old 1.7 Basic PNA Launcher or related definitions removed in 2.4.3 have no migration path.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable; legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. Traditional Chinese, English and Russian romance translations are included, but their in-game display awaits separate confirmation; see the release notes for scope.

## 中文

**培养角色、选择特化分工、管理个人关系，让已经成长的人格在新的身体中延续。**

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是围绕长期角色成长展开的 **RimJobWorld 成人向扩展**。调教、绑定、特化、身体改造与人格凝胶，将个人发展与殖民地生活连接起来。

**版本支持：** **2.4.8**安装包支持 **RimWorld 1.7与1.6**；下述功能以 **1.7**为准，**1.6保留SSC 2.4.1冻结内容**。提供简中、繁中、英语和俄语文本。

### 从调教、绑定到持续成长

安排调教员、下达手动命令，或进行符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与部位开发。通过**契约仪式**推进锁链阶段，逐步开放进一步的控制。绑定也影响同床、心情与殖民地增益；关系概览帮助你定位成员、管理调教员指派。

**欲望之种**提供永久主人资格。亲自完成调教与仪式，可推进**欲望共生**四阶段，并在主人面板查看成长、管理伤害分担。

可显式“指定并启用调教工作”，也可为现指定者就地启用；新启用优先级3，已启用则保留原值。

### 为角色选择七种特化

- **公交车：** 贸易与社交收益，以及交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 符合资格后，代主人培养其他角色。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**贴心安抚**可平复合格精神状态，或提供心情／工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**驯兽**可驯服合格野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 战斗与生存增益，以及终极能力**战斗超频**。
- **孕母：** 阶段生育／照料／魅力收益、孕前预设及面向合格成年对象的终极**受孕**技能；新选需锁链阶段3与完整子宫开发。

各方向分别保留培养进度。**终极形态需要完成相应特化培养，再经人格凝胶加工与植入定型。**

### 设置个人规则，管理互动关系

为每名角色分别配置行为许可、浪漫行为与对象类别。锁链阶段逐步开放主动控制、被动回应，以及求爱、求婚、结束关系的**一次性点名命令**。可选接受保证默认关闭，互动仍须满足必要条件；命令被打断即结束。

浪漫事件会产生对应心情、本人视角日志、历史与任务反馈。性行为和浪漫各有个人级**恢复默认**按钮，重置偏好时保留关系与记忆。符合成年等条件的绑定性奴，会获得面对真实主人的自愿吸引力支持；主人侧意愿和其他互动条件仍然生效。

个人面板提供符合资格时可用的**欢愉恩赐、伤害分担、泌乳、生育、凝胶渲染和 PNA 生产**控制。

### 开发当前身体，延续已经成长的人格

通过研究与手术开启**子宫开发和胃部开发**，再经合格调教推进。子宫开发完成后可控制生育，开启时提高生育能力、缩短合格新妊娠的时长，生育开关默认关闭；胃部开发改善饥饿与进食效率，完成后获得正常进食口径的食物中毒免疫。

默认关闭的可选**身体敏感度**随锁链阶段开放四档，提供 **×2／×4／×8／×16** 频率系数，同时增加脆弱度，并按阶段影响持续心情。界面分别显示保存选择与实际档位；通用**高潮频率系数**也合成PNA倍率与部位阶段加值，关闭个人敏感度不关闭其他合法来源。

其他选择包括永久泌乳、局部肢体改造与全身凝胶化。完全凝胶化角色可独立调整**颜色、不透明度和渲染**。

提取**人格凝胶**、完成加工，再植入合格**空壳**。属于人格的成长、主人资格／进度和浪漫偏好随人格延续；子宫／胃部开发、敏感度、PNA 生产选择与凝胶外观留在身体上。

三态人格印记按种子／绑定资格分化并随人格流转，分化态锁定对应身份；当前主人禁止人格排泄。孕前选择保存在子宫身体；空壳完成态禁用六项舒适／环境类需求，保留其他效果。复制体独立立项并暂缓。

### 生产 PNA、配置武器与建设殖民地文化

生殖器调教完成后，可生产普通**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**。个人**暂停／恢复生产**保留当前批次进度，面板显示积累、关闭与营养不足状态。浓缩研究开放药物台加工，以及效率较低的篝火／灶台路线。

PNA 对普通角色产生药物效果，为符合资格的锁链身体提供正面的微械活化，当前主人免疫其药效。默认关闭的可选能力**欢愉恩赐**，在合格双人互动完成后提供 PNA 与有限成长收益。

**九把 PNA 武器贯穿中世纪、工业与太空三个科技阶段。** 涂覆武器会造成真实伤口；太空主攻击可让符合资格的锁链身体无伤吸收 PNA 并修复伤势。护盾和掩体仍有作用，成品无需独立弹药或补液。

安装 Ideology 后，**主奴秩序**提供 **28 条戒律**、文化反馈、专属图标、名称预设和动态背景。契约仪式可按原版条件获得文化演化点，该选项默认关闭。**医美手术与塑貌仪式**消耗 PNA 和药品，每次成功将原版美貌提升一档，最高 **+2**，恢复反应结束后才能再次施作。

### 依赖与兼容

- 使用与游戏匹配的 **Harmony、RimJobWorld**，加载在 SSC 之前。
- 契约仪式、主奴秩序和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）DLC**。**Royalty（皇权）为可选**，启用时调教研究还需蓝图。
- 受训目标需要设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**，原版奴隶身份不能替代。
- 已支持的 **UAP** 手动多人入口可在 SSC 限制开启时使用。独立的手动许可豁免默认关闭，原匹配与身体条件仍保留；未完成会话读档时取消。

备份存档、退出游戏，并**完整替换旧模组文件夹**，保留两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。存档若引用 2.4.3 已从 1.7 移除的旧简易 PNA 发射器或相关定义，不提供迁移支持。

宠物兔选择入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 对接暂停。繁中、英语、俄语浪漫译文已经提供，但实机显示仍待独立确认，具体验证范围见发布说明。
