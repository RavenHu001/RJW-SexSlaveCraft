## English

**Train pawns, give them specialized roles, manage their relationships, and carry their personalities into new bodies.**

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult **RimJobWorld** expansion built around long-term pawn development. Training, bonds, specialization, body modification and Personality Gel form a progression system that connects individual growth with colony life.

**Version support:** the 2.4.7 package supports RimWorld 1.7 and 1.6. The features below describe **1.7**; **1.6 retains frozen SSC 2.4.1 content**. Includes Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian text.

### Train, bond and develop

Assign trainers, give manual orders or use eligible self-training to develop Corruption and body-part progress. **Binding Rituals** advance Chain stages, opening further control and development options. Bonds also affect shared beds, mood and colony bonuses; the relationship overview helps you find bound pawns and manage trainer assignments.

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification. Personally completing Training and Rituals develops four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire**, with a Master panel for growth and damage sharing.

### Choose a role for each pawn

Six specializations support different colony roles:

- **Public Use:** Trade and social bonuses, plus additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Greater milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualified pawns can train others for their Master.
- **Pet Cat:** Mobility and melee evasion; final **Gentle Comfort** calms eligible mental states or grants mood/work benefits.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Animal Training** tames an eligible wild animal or completes an owned animal's training project.
- **Combatant:** Combat bonuses and resilience, with final **Combat Overdrive**.

Each direction keeps its own ordinary progress. **Final forms require completed specialization development, Personality Gel processing and implantation.**

### Set personal rules and see the consequences

Manage activity permissions, romance behavior and partner categories per pawn. Chain stages progressively unlock initiative controls, response controls and **one-time named commands** for courtship, proposals and ending relationships. Optional forced-acceptance settings default to off; interaction prerequisites still apply, and interruption ends a command.

Real romance events produce contextual mood, personal log backgrounds and task feedback, with history updated as relationships change. Separate **Restore Defaults** buttons reset personal behavior or romance preferences without erasing relationships or memories.

Personal panels also provide eligible controls for **Bestow Bliss, damage sharing, milk production, fertility, gel rendering and PNA production**.

Eligible adult bound slaves also gain directed voluntary attraction toward their actual Master, while body conditions and the Master's willingness remain required. Manual UAP groups can work with SSC restrictions enabled; an optional manual-request permission override defaults to off. Unfinished group sessions are canceled on load.

### Develop the body and preserve the personality

Research and surgery start **uterine and stomach development**, with further eligible Training advancing each path. Completed uterine development provides fertility control (off by default), increased fertility while enabled and shorter eligible new pregnancies. Stomach development improves hunger and eating efficiency, ending in food-poisoning immunity during normal eating.

Optional **body sensitivity** unlocks four levels through Chain stages, with frequency factors ×2/×4/×8/×16, vulnerability tradeoffs and stage-dependent ongoing mood. Saved and effective levels are shown separately. The general frequency stat also accepts valid external sources; automatic warm-up and fatigue slowdown are not included.

Other routes include permanent lactation, limb modification and full gelatinization. Fully gelatinized pawns can adjust their **color, opacity and rendering preference**, preserving vanilla eyes and supported Facial Animation features.

Extract **Personality Gel**, process it and implant it into an eligible **Hollow**. Personality-based progress, Master qualification/growth and romance preferences can continue in the new body. Organ development, sensitivity and gel appearance belong to the current body.

### Use PNA from the workshop to the battlefield

Completing genital Training unlocks ordinary **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** production. Concentration research opens drug-lab recipes and a less efficient campfire/stove route, with production guidance in item and research descriptions.

Personal **pause/resume** preserves the current production batch without catch-up. The body panel shows accumulation, disabled status and insufficient nutrition.

PNA has drug effects on ordinary pawns; valid Chain-bound pawns receive beneficial nanomachine activation, and current Masters are immune. Optional **Bestow Bliss**, off by default, supplies PNA and limited development benefits after eligible paired interactions.

**Nine PNA weapons span medieval, industrial and spacer technology.** Coated weapons still cause real wounds; spacer main attacks support harmless absorption and injury repair for eligible Chain-bound bodies. Shields and cover still matter. Finished weapons need no separate ammunition or refills.

### Shape the colony's culture

With Ideology, **Master-slave Order** offers **28 precepts** with mood, opinion, leadership and population feedback, alongside dedicated icons, naming presets and dynamic backgrounds. An optional setting lets Binding Rituals award ideology development points under vanilla conditions; it defaults to off.

**Cosmetic surgery and Reshaping Rituals** improve vanilla Beauty one step per success, up to **+2**, using PNA and medicine. Both have temporary recovery reactions that must pass before repeating treatment.

### Requirements and installation

- Load matching **Harmony** and **RimJobWorld** before SSC.
- **Ideology** is required for Binding Rituals, cultural features and Reshaping Rituals. **Royalty is optional**; when enabled, Training research also requires techprints.
- Training targets need SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

Back up saves, close the game and **replace the whole old mod folder**, keeping both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. **1.7 saves referencing the old Basic PNA Launcher or its related definitions removed in 2.4.3 have no migration path.**

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable; legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. Three-language romance display (Traditional Chinese, English and Russian) still awaits in-game confirmation; PNA production guidance has been accepted; see the release notes for verification scope.

## 中文

**培养角色、安排特化分工、管理个人关系，让成长起来的人格在新的身体中延续。**

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是围绕长期角色培养展开的 **RimJobWorld 成人向扩展**。调教、绑定、特化、身体改造与人格凝胶构成连续的成长路线，将个人发展与殖民地生活连接起来。

**版本支持：** 2.4.7安装包支持RimWorld 1.7与1.6。下述功能以 **1.7** 为准，**1.6保留SSC 2.4.1冻结内容**。提供简中、繁中、英语和俄语文本。

### 从培养到绑定，逐步建立成长路线

安排调教员、下达手动命令，或进行符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与部位开发。通过**契约仪式**推进锁链阶段，逐步开放进一步的控制和培养选项。绑定也影响同床、心情与殖民地增益；关系概览可帮助你定位绑定成员、管理调教员指派。

**欲望之种**提供永久主人资格；亲自完成调教与仪式，可推进**欲望共生**四阶段，并在主人面板查看成长、管理伤害分担。

### 为角色选择不同的殖民地分工

六种特化各有用途：

- **公交车：** 提供贸易与社交收益，以及交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 符合资格后，代主人培养其他角色。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**贴心安抚**可平复合格精神状态，或提供心情／工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**驯兽**可驯服合格野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 提供战斗与生存增益，并获得终极能力**战斗超频**。

各方向分别保留普通进度。**终极形态需要完成相应培养，再经人格凝胶加工与植入定型。**

### 设置个人规则，让互动留下反馈

为每名角色分别管理行为许可、浪漫行为与对象类别。锁链阶段逐步开放主动控制、被动回应，以及求爱、求婚、结束关系的**一次性点名命令**。可选强制接受默认关闭，互动仍须满足原有必要条件；命令被打断即结束。

真实浪漫事件会产生对应心情、本人视角的日志背景与任务结果，并随关系变化更新相关历史。性行为和浪漫分别提供个人级**恢复默认**按钮，恢复偏好不清空关系或记忆。

个人面板还提供符合资格时可用的**欢愉恩赐、伤害分担、泌乳、生育、凝胶渲染和PNA生产**控制。

符合成年等条件的绑定性奴还会获得面对真实主人的自愿吸引力支持，仍须满足身体条件和主人侧意愿。UAP手动多人可在SSC限制开启时使用；可选手动许可豁免默认关闭，未完成会话读档时取消。

### 改造当前身体，延续已经培养的人格

通过研究与手术开启**子宫开发和胃部开发**，再经合格培养逐步推进各自进度。子宫开发完成后可控制生育，默认关闭，开启时提高生育能力，并缩短合格新妊娠的时长；胃部开发改善饥饿与进食效率，完成后获得正常进食口径的食物中毒免疫。

可选**身体敏感度**随锁链阶段开放四档，提供×2／×4／×8／×16频率系数、脆弱度代价和阶段对应持续心情，保存选择与实际档位分别显示。通用频率属性也支持合法外部来源；自动升温与疲劳减速尚未实现。

其他路线包括永久泌乳、局部肢体改造与全身凝胶化。完全凝胶化角色可独立调整**颜色、不透明度和个人渲染**，保留原版眼部及已支持的 Facial Animation 五官表现。

提取**人格凝胶**、完成加工，再植入合格**空壳**。属于人格的成长、主人资格／进度和浪漫偏好可在新身体中延续；器官开发、敏感度与凝胶外观属于当前身体。

### 将 PNA 用于生产、培养与战斗

生殖器调教完成后可生产普通**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**。完成浓缩研究后，可使用药物台加工，或采用效率较低的篝火／灶台路线；物品与研究描述提供产出流程引导。

个人**暂停／恢复生产**保留当前批次进度，不追补暂停期间产出；身体栏显示积累、关闭与营养不足状态。

PNA 对普通角色和有效锁链对象具有不同效果，后者获得正面的微械活化，当前主人免疫其药效。默认关闭的可选能力**欢愉恩赐**，在合格双人互动完成后提供 PNA 与有限成长收益。

**九把 PNA 武器贯穿中世纪、工业与太空三个科技阶段。** 涂覆武器仍会造成真实伤口；太空主攻击可让合格锁链身体无伤吸收并修复伤势，护盾和掩体仍有作用。成品无需独立弹药或补液。

### 让殖民地形成自己的文化

安装 Ideology 后，**主奴秩序**提供 **28 条戒律**，带来心情、好感、领袖与人数反馈，配合专属图标、名称预设和动态背景。可选设置允许契约仪式按原版条件获得文化演化点，该选项默认关闭。

**医美手术与塑貌仪式**消耗 PNA 和药品，每次成功将原版美貌提升一档，最高 **+2**；两种途径均有暂时恢复反应，恢复后才能再次施作。

### 依赖与安装

- 使用与游戏匹配的 **Harmony、RimJobWorld**，加载在 SSC 之前。
- 契约仪式、文化功能与塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）DLC**。**Royalty（皇权）为可选**，启用时调教研究还需蓝图。
- 受训目标需要设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**，原版奴隶身份不能替代。

备份存档、退出游戏，并**完整替换旧模组文件夹**，保留两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。**1.7 存档若引用 2.4.3 已移除的旧简易 PNA 发射器或相关定义，不提供迁移支持。**

宠物兔入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 对接暂停。繁中、英语、俄语浪漫文案的实机显示仍待确认；PNA产出引导已获确认有效，具体验证范围见发布说明。
