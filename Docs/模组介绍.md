## English

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult expansion for RimJobWorld, focused on long-term pawn development, master–slave bonds, specialized colony roles, personality transfer, and body modification.

Train individual pawns, develop their abilities, and choose how they contribute to the colony—from trade and production to animal handling and combat.

**Training and bonds**

Arrange automatic Training, issue manual orders, or enable self-training for eligible pawns. Develop Corruption and six body-part progression tracks, then use Binding Rituals to advance Chain stages.

Bonds affect behavior permissions, shared beds, mood, and relationships. Masters also gain the Libidinal Resonance Field and can enable damage sharing with eligible bound pawns.

**Seed of Desire and Master growth**

The Seed of Desire grants permanent personal Master qualification. A one-time free quest provides initial access; later seeds come from rare quest and ruin rewards. Direct Master selection remains enabled by default and can be disabled in settings. With Royalty, seeds also supply missing Training techprints without completing the research.

Masters develop Symbiosis of Desire through their own daily Training and whole Binding Rituals. Four stages boost Consciousness, Sight, Hearing, Social Impact and Injury Healing. The Master panel shows growth and hosts damage sharing. Qualification and growth follow Personality Gel; clones do not inherit them or Master identity. Leaving the role suspends bonuses and hides growth; returning restores the saved stage and experience.

**Six specializations**

- **Public Use:** Trade and social abilities, with additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualify to train other pawns on the Master's behalf, with duty and trust feedback.
- **Pet Cat:** Movement and melee dodge. The final form unlocks **Soothing Resonance**, which calms a pawn's current mental state or grants temporary mood and work bonuses.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal taming and training. The final form unlocks **Directed Taming**, which directly tames an eligible wild animal or completes one training project for an owned animal.
- **Combatant:** Melee and ranged combat bonuses, damage reduction, and **Combat Overdrive** in the final form.

Ordinary training completion does **not** automatically grant a final specialization. Finalization requires Personality Gel processing and implantation.

**Personality transfer and body modification**

Extract a personality into **Personality Gel**, transfer it into an eligible Hollow, or process it into a final specialization. Personality development and physical modification can be planned separately.

Further development unlocks permanent lactation and PNA nanofluid production. Pursue partial limb modification or transform an eligible Hollow through full-body gelatinization for stronger physical capabilities, protection, and regeneration. Transformation carries risks, including loss of a limb or body.

**Requirements and version notes**

- **RimWorld 1.6**
- **Harmony and RimJobWorld**, loaded before SSC.
- **Ideology is needed for Binding Rituals and the associated stage-progression route.**
- Training targets must have SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

Version 2.4.0 adds the Seed of Desire, personal Master qualification and Master progression. Receiver identity requirements and Training Officer feedback from 2.3.9 are retained.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable. Legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. The mod contains adult themes of slavery, coercive relationships, and body modification.

## 中文

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是 RimJobWorld 的成人向扩展模组，围绕长期角色培养、主从绑定、殖民地分工、人格转移与身体改造展开。

你可以培养少量角色经营主从关系，也可以建立承担贸易、生产、训导、动物工作和战斗任务的培养体系。

**培养与绑定**

安排自动调教、下达手动任务，或为符合条件的角色开启自我调教。通过日常培养积累恶堕与六项部位开发，再用契约仪式推进锁链阶段。

绑定关系会影响行为许可、同床、心情与社交。主人还可获得**灵欲共振场**增益，并开启符合条件的绑定角色参与的伤害分担。

**欲望之种与主人成长**

欲望之种赋予永久个人主人资格，初次免费任务提供首枚，后续来自稀有任务与遗迹奖励。默认仍允许直接手动选择主人，也可在设置中关闭。有 Royalty 时，种子同时补齐调教研究蓝图，但不会直接完成研究。

主人亲自完成日常调教和整场契约仪式，推进欲望共生的四个阶段，强化意识、视觉、听觉、社交影响与伤口愈合。主人面板展示成长并提供伤害分担开关。资格与成长随人格凝胶迁移，克隆不继承它们或主人身份；退出身份时隐藏成长并停用加成，返回后恢复原阶段与经验。

**六种特化方向**

- **公交车：** 偏向贸易与社交，并包含交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶能力，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 取得资格后代主人培养其他角色，并获得职责与信任反馈。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极形态解锁**安抚共鸣**，平复角色当前精神状态，或提供短期心情与工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物驯服与训练；终极形态解锁**定向驯导**，直接驯服符合条件的野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 获得近远程战斗加成与减伤；终极形态解锁**战斗超频**。

**普通培养完成不等于终极化。** 终极特化需要进一步加工并植入人格凝胶。

**人格转移与身体改造**

将角色的人格提取为**人格凝胶**，转移到符合条件的空壳，或加工为终极特化。人格成长与身体改造可以分别规划。

进一步培养还能解锁永久泌乳与 PNA 纳米液生产。局部肢体改造，以及面向符合条件空壳的全身凝胶化，提供身体机能、防护与再生方面的强化，但改造存在失败风险，可能损失肢体或整个身体。

**依赖与版本说明**

- **RimWorld 1.6**
- **Harmony、RimJobWorld**，加载在 SSC 之前。
- **契约仪式及其阶段进阶路线需要 Ideology（文化）DLC。**
- 受训目标必须设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**；原版奴隶身份不能替代。
