## English

**Develop pawns, shape your colony's culture, and carry personalities into new bodies.** SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult RimJobWorld expansion combining long-term Training, bonds, six specializations, personality transfer and body modification.

**2.4.4 adds Master-slave Order, optional ritual development points, cosmetic surgery and Reshaping Rituals.** It also fixes a Personality Excretion interruption. The bond overview, low-tech progression, nine PNA weapons and individual gel appearance remain part of the experience.

**Version support:** the release includes both game-version folders. **RimWorld 1.7 receives the current features; 1.6 retains frozen SSC 2.4.1 content.** The game selects the matching runtime automatically.

**Training, bonds and colony roles**

Use automatic Training, manual orders or eligible self-training to develop Corruption and body-part progress. Binding Rituals advance Chain stages. Bonds influence permissions, shared beds, mood and colony bonuses; the overview provides portrait navigation, trainer assignment and group selection of available bound members on the current map.

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification. Masters advance through four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire** by personally completing Training and Rituals, with growth and damage-sharing controls on their panel.

Choose from six specializations:

- **Public Use:** Trade and social influence, with additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualified pawns train others on their Master's behalf.
- **Pet Cat:** Movement and melee dodge; final **Soothing Resonance** calms eligible mental states or grants mood/work bonuses.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Directed Taming** tames an eligible wild animal or completes an owned animal's training project.
- **Combatant:** Combat bonuses, damage reduction and final **Combat Overdrive**.

Each direction keeps its own ordinary progress. To obtain a final specialization, **complete Training, process the Personality Gel, then implant it**.

**Culture and reshaping — new in 2.4.4, 1.7 only**

**Master-slave Order** offers six groups with **28 precepts** covering attitudes toward sex slaves, Training Officers and Masters, Master/bound-pawn counts, and gender rules. Choices produce mood, opinion, leadership and population feedback. Dedicated icons, four-language text, naming presets and dynamic backgrounds give the ideology its own presentation. Cultural bond checks use actual Chains; precepts do not grant qualification or create bonds.

An optional global setting lets Binding Rituals award **ideology development points** under vanilla conditions and reform caps. It is **off by default**.

Improve vanilla **Beauty one step per success, up to +2**, through researched **cosmetic surgery** (Medical 8) or the Master-slave Order's **Reshaping Ritual** (Medical 6, no research prerequisite). Both consume normal PNA and medicine and cause temporary recovery reactions, stronger after failure. Wait for recovery before repeating. The ritual includes purple mist and dedicated name presets. Conflicting, gene-sourced or suppressed Beauty states block treatment; higher modded levels are preserved.

**PNA, weapons and low-tech progression — 1.7 weapon expansion**

PNA has five ordinary drug stages; valid Chain-bound pawns instead receive beneficial **nanomachine activation**. Current Masters are immune to its drug effects. Optional **Bestow Bliss**, off by default, supplies PNA and limited development/feedback after eligible paired interaction completions; its dose follows the Master's current body output capacity.

Nine weapons span three eras:

- **Medieval:** Short spear, blowpipe and light crossbow, crafted with normal PNA.
- **Industrial:** Short whip, repeating needle gun and precision needle rifle, crafted with concentrated PNA.
- **Spacer:** Silk whip, pulse gun and projector gun; main attacks allow eligible Chain-bound bodies to absorb PNA without wounds and repair an injury.

Medieval/industrial delivery requires fresh qualifying wounds, which remain and can be fatal. Shields and cover still intercept spacer attacks. Finished weapons require no separate ammunition or refills, and have dedicated art and purple hit/projectile feedback.

Lower-tech research opens more progression before industrial technology. After concentration research, campfires and compatible stoves offer **10 normal PNA → 1 concentrated PNA**; drug labs retain more efficient recipes.

**Personality transfer and body modification**

Extract **Personality Gel**, process it, and implant it into an eligible **Hollow**. Master qualification and growth follow the personality; PNA states, recovery reactions and gel appearance belong to the body.

Develop permanent lactation and PNA production, modify limbs, or fully gelatinize an eligible Hollow. On 1.7, fully gelatinized pawns can adjust their own color, opacity and rendering preference through **Gel Appearance** on the Training tab, preserving vanilla eyes and supported Facial Animation features. Failed transformations can cost a limb or body.

**Requirements and installation**

- Load matching **Harmony** and **RimJobWorld** before SSC.
- **Ideology** is required for Binding Rituals, cultural features and Reshaping Rituals. **Royalty is optional**; seeds can supply missing Training techprints, but research still needs completion.
- Training targets require SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

Back up your save, close the game and **replace the entire old mod folder**. Keep both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml` intact. **1.7 saves referencing the old Basic PNA Launcher, projectile, recipe or dedicated research removed in 2.4.3 have no migration path.** Frozen 1.6 retains its original content.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable; legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. Adult themes include slavery, coercive relationships and body modification.

## 中文

**培养角色、塑造殖民地文化，让人格在新的身体中延续。** SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是 RimJobWorld 的成人向扩展，将长期调教、主从绑定、六种特化、人格转移与身体改造连接起来。

**2.4.4 新增主奴秩序文化、可选仪式演化点、医美手术与塑貌仪式**，并修复人格排泄中断问题。关系概览、低科技路线、三代九把 PNA 武器与个体凝胶外观继续保留。

**版本支持：** 安装包包含两套游戏版本目录。**RimWorld 1.7 使用当前功能，1.6 保留 SSC 2.4.1 冻结内容**，游戏自动选择对应运行版本。

**培养、绑定与殖民地分工**

通过自动调教、手动命令或符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与部位开发；通过契约仪式推进锁链阶段。绑定影响行为许可、同床、心情及殖民地增益。关系概览支持头像定位、调教员指派和批选当前地图可用的绑定成员。

**欲望之种**赋予永久主人资格。主人亲自完成调教和整场仪式，可推进**欲望共生**四阶段，并在主人面板查看成长、设置伤害分担。

六种特化各有分工：

- **公交车：** 贸易、社交影响与交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 取得资格后，代主人培养其他角色。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**安抚共鸣**可平复合格精神状态，或提供心情／工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**定向驯导**可驯服合格野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 战斗加成、减伤及终极能力**战斗超频**。

各方向分别保留普通进度。获得终极特化需要**完成培养、加工人格凝胶，再经植入定型**。

**文化与塑貌——2.4.4 新增，仅限 1.7**

**主奴秩序**提供六组、共 **28 条戒律**，涵盖性奴态度、训导官、主人、主人数量、性奴数量和性别规范。不同选择带来心情、好感、领袖与人数反馈，配合专属图标、四语文案、名称预设及动态背景。文化依据实际锁链判断绑定；戒律不授予资格，也不自动建立契约。

全局设置可让契约仪式按原版发展条件和改革上限获得**文化演化点**，该选项**默认关闭**。

通过研究解锁的**医美手术**（医术 8），或主奴秩序的**塑貌仪式**（医术 6、无研究前置），每次成功将原版 **Beauty 提升一档，最高 +2**。两者消耗普通 PNA 与药品，产生暂时恢复反应，失败反应更重；恢复前不能重复施作。仪式带有紫雾和专属命名预设。冲突、基因来源或受抑制的美貌会拒绝处理，模组更高档保留。

**PNA、三代武器与低科技路线——武器拓展仅限 1.7**

PNA 对普通角色提供五阶段药效，有效锁链对象则获得正面的**微械活化**，当前主人免疫药效。默认关闭的可选能力**欢愉恩赐**，在合格双人互动完成后提供 PNA、有限成长及独立反馈；剂量依据主人当前身体的产液能力计算。

九把武器贯穿三个科技阶段：

- **中世纪：** 短矛、吹箭筒、轻弩，制造时使用普通原液。
- **工业：** 短鞭、速射针枪、精确针枪，制造时使用浓缩液。
- **太空：** 丝鞭、脉冲枪、投射枪；主攻击可让合格锁链身体无伤吸收 PNA，并修复伤势。

中世纪／工业武器须造成合格新伤口才能施药，伤势仍可能致死；护盾和掩体仍会拦截太空攻击。成品无需独立弹药或补液，并有专用美术与紫色命中／投射反馈。

低科技研究让工业前殖民地更早接入部分玩法。完成浓缩研究后，篝火和兼容灶台可用 **10 份普通原液制成 1 份浓缩液**；药物台保留更高效配方。

**人格转移与身体改造**

提取**人格凝胶**，加工后植入合格**空壳**。主人资格与成长随人格延续，PNA 状态、恢复反应和凝胶外观属于当前身体。

发展永久泌乳与 PNA 生产，改造局部肢体，或为空壳进行全身凝胶化。1.7 的完全凝胶化角色可通过“调教”页的**凝胶外观**，独立调整颜色、不透明度及个人渲染，保留原版眼部和已支持的 Facial Animation 五官表现。改造失败可能损失肢体或身体。

**依赖与安装**

- **Harmony、RimJobWorld** 使用与游戏匹配的版本，加载在 SSC 之前。
- 契约仪式、文化功能和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）DLC**。**Royalty（皇权）为可选**；欲望之种可补齐调教研究缺少的蓝图，之后仍需完成研究。
- 受训目标必须设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**，原版奴隶身份不能替代。

备份存档、退出游戏，并**完整替换旧模组文件夹**，保留两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。**1.7 存档若引用 2.4.3 已移除的旧简易 PNA 发射器、弹丸、配方或专属研究，不提供迁移支持。** 1.6 冻结版保留原内容。

宠物兔选择入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 对接暂停。本模组包含奴役、强制关系与身体改造等成人题材。
