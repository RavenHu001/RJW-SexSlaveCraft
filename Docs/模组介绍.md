## English

**SexSlaveCraft (SSC)** is an adult RimJobWorld expansion for long-term pawn development. Train pawns for specialized roles, develop Master–slave bonds through Binding Rituals, and use **Personality Gel** to carry learned progress into another eligible body.

[Download 2.5.0](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.0) · [Quick start](../QUICK_START_EN.md) · [Full player guide](../PLAYER_GUIDE_EN.md)

### New in 2.5.0

- **A six-page Training Management window.** Select one pawn and click the original **Training** tab. The movable window follows your selection on the map or colonist bar. Check identity, progress and cooldowns in **Overview**, then use the other pages for relationships, daily Training, specializations, body and appearance, and behavior rules.
- **Multiple trainers per trainee.** Use the handles next to portraits in **Relationships** to add or remove trainers individually. You can also locate each trainer or enable their Training work from the same page. Old single-trainer assignments migrate on load.
- **One daily Training job per trainee.** Automatic jobs and manual orders share the same reservation. In-progress jobs can resume on load. Binding Rituals and shared beds can also use eligible trainers from the roster.

[2.5.0 release notes](Releases/2.5.0/2.5.0发布说明.md#english)

### What you can do

- **Build bonds and develop nine specializations.** Advance Chain stages through Binding Rituals. Choose Public Use, Cow, Training Officer, Combatant, Broodmother, or one of the four pet paths for Cat, Dog, Rabbit and Fox. Finish training, then process and implant Personality Gel to unlock a final form.
- **Manage pawns individually.** Set behavior permissions and romance preferences, manage fertility and lactation, and customize gel-body appearance. Personality Gel carries progress and preferences tied to the personality. Body development stays with the original body.
- **Put PNA to work.** Produce Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA) for Chain activation, weapons and cosmetic procedures. Set automatic PNA upkeep for eligible pawns on maps and caravans. With Ideology, Master-slave Order adds its own precepts and rituals.

### Install and start

**RimWorld 1.7** receives the 2.5.0 features. The same package includes **SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6**. In-game text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Russian.

Load **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. Binding Rituals, SSC ideology and Reshaping Rituals require **Ideology**. **Royalty** is optional and adds a techprint requirement for Training research.

When upgrading, back up your saves and replace the entire old mod folder. Keep both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. To get started, research **Training**, set the trainee’s SSC identity to **Sex Slave**, and assign an eligible trainer.

### 2.5.0 新内容

- **六页调教管理窗口。** 单选角色后点击原「调教」标签，即可打开可拖动的独立窗口。窗口随地图或殖民者栏的单选切换。在「概况」查看身份、进度和冷却，其余页面分别管理关系、日常调教、特化、身体与外观及行为规则。
- **同一受训者可指派多名调教员。** 在「关系」页拖动头像旁的把手，逐人追加或撤销指派，也可就地定位或启用该角色的调教工作。旧档的单人指派会在读档时迁入名单。
- **每名受训者同时只接受一个日常调教任务。** 自动接单与点名命令共用预约，在途任务可在读档后恢复。绑定仪式和同床也可按各自条件使用名单中的调教员。

### 主要玩法

- **建立绑定，培养九种特化。** 通过绑定仪式推进锁链阶段。可选择公交车、奶牛、训导官、战斗员、孕母，以及猫、狗、兔、狐四种宠物特化。完成对应培养后，加工并植入人格凝胶即可获得终极形态。
- **管理每名角色。** 分别设置行为许可和浪漫偏好，管理生育与泌乳，或调整全身凝胶化外观。人格凝胶可携带归属人格的进度与偏好，身体开发则留在原身体上。
- **殖民地生产与文化。** 生产紫色纳米催情液（PNA），用于锁链活化、武器和医美。符合条件的角色可在地图或旅队中自动补充 PNA。安装 Ideology 后，「主奴秩序」提供专属戒律与仪式。

### 安装与入门

2.5.0 新内容面向 **RimWorld 1.7**。同一安装包为 **RimWorld 1.6** 提供 **SSC 2.4.1**。游戏内文本提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语。

按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 的顺序加载。绑定仪式、SSC 文化及塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）**。**Royalty（皇权）**可选，安装后调教研究需要蓝图。

升级时先备份存档，再完整替换旧模组文件夹，保留包内两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。初次游玩时，完成**调教**研究，将受训者的 SSC 身份设为**性奴**，再指派符合条件的调教员。
