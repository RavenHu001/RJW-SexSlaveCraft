## English

**SexSlaveCraft (SSC)** is an adult RimJobWorld expansion for long-term pawn progression. Train pawns for jobs your colony needs, develop their bonds with a Master, and use **Personality Gel** to carry their learned specializations into another body.

### New in 2.4.10

- **Pet Rabbit** develops through cleaning and cooking. Its final ability, **Careful Cooking**, speeds up cooking for four in-game hours and protects eligible meals finished during that time from preparation-related food poisoning.
- **Pet Fox** develops through bench research and tending other pawns. Its final ability, **Emergency Bandaging**, treats all eligible wounds on another humanlike member of your faction on the same map, including downed patients, without medicine.
- **Pet Cat** improves movement speed and hunting stealth. **Pet Dog** adds plant work speed alongside its animal-handling bonuses. Training progress is tracked separately for all four pet paths. Each pawn can hold one final pet specialization at a time.

### What you can do

- **Train and bind.** Assign trainers and advance Chain stages through Binding Rituals. A Seed of Desire grants permanent Master qualification. Masters gain experience by personally completing Training and Binding Rituals.
- **Develop nine specializations.** Choose **Public Use** for trade and social benefits, **Cow** for milk production, **Training Officer** to train others, **Combatant** for battle, **Broodmother** for fertility and childcare, or one of the **four pet paths**. Complete the chosen path's training, then process and implant its Personality Gel to unlock the final form.
- **Manage each pawn.** Set individual activity permissions and romance preferences. Develop the body through surgery, manage fertility and lactation, or customize full gelatinization. Personality Gel carries learned progress and preferences. Body development stays with its original body.
- **Use SSC in colony production.** Produce **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** for Chain activation, nine weapons and cosmetic procedures. Set automatic PNA upkeep for eligible pawns on maps and caravans. Three tiers of room emitters spread the RJW humpshroom effect, and **Depravity Gel** is a consumable that can boost Corruption gains. With Ideology, **Master-slave Order** adds its own precepts and rituals.

### Install and start

**RimWorld 1.7** gets the features above. The same download includes **SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6**. In-game text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Russian.

Load **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. Binding Rituals, SSC ideology and Reshaping Rituals require **Ideology**. **Royalty** is optional and adds a techprint requirement for Training.

When upgrading, back up your saves and replace the entire old mod folder. Keep both version folders and the included `LoadFolders.xml`.

To start playing, research **Training**, set up a trainer, and give the trainee the **Sex Slave** identity in SSC's Training tab.

## 中文

**SexSlaveCraft（SSC）** 是一款以长期角色培养为核心的 RimJobWorld 成人向扩展。为殖民地培养不同岗位的专精角色，发展主从关系，还能用**人格凝胶**把已习得的专精延续到另一具身体。

### 2.4.10 新内容

- **宠物兔**通过清洁和烹饪积累进度。终极技能**精心料理**在 4 游戏小时内提高烹饪速度，防止期间出炉的合格餐食在制作时染毒。
- **宠物狐**通过研究台科研和护理他人积累进度。终极技能**应急包扎**无需药品，即可一次处理同图另一名己方人形伤员所有当前可包扎的伤口，倒地伤员也可选。
- **宠物猫和宠物狗**的常驻加成有调整。猫提高移动速度与狩猎隐蔽，狗在动物工作加成之外获得种植速度。猫、狗、兔、狐各自保存培养进度，同一时间只可拥有一种宠物终极成果。

### 主要玩法

- **调教与绑定。** 指派调教员，通过绑定仪式提升锁链阶段。欲望之种可授予永久主人资格，主人亲自完成调教和绑定仪式也能积累经验。
- **九种特化。** 公交车偏贸易与社交，奶牛偏产奶，训导官可代为培养他人，战斗员专攻战斗，孕母侧重生育与育儿，另外还有**猫、狗、兔、狐四种宠物特化**。完成对应培养后，加工并植入人格凝胶，即可获得终极形态。
- **个人管理与身体改造。** 分别设置行为许可和浪漫偏好，通过手术进行身体开发，管理生育与泌乳，或调整全身凝胶化的外观。人格凝胶可携带培养进度与偏好，身体开发则随原身体保留。
- **殖民地生产与文化。** 生产**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**，用于锁链活化、九种武器和医美。符合条件的角色可在地图或旅队中自动补充 PNA。三档发烟设备可在房间内扩散 RJW 驼峰菇药效，**堕落凝胶**是可加快恶堕积累的消耗品。安装 Ideology 后，**主奴秩序**还提供专属戒律与仪式。

### 安装与入门

以上玩法面向 **RimWorld 1.7**。同一安装包为 **RimWorld 1.6** 提供 **SSC 2.4.1**。游戏内文本提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语。

按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 的顺序加载。绑定仪式、SSC 文化及塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）**。**Royalty（皇权）**可选，安装后调教研究需要蓝图。

升级时先备份存档，再完整替换旧模组文件夹，保留包内两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。

初次游玩时，完成**调教**研究并配置调教员，在 SSC 的「调教」页为受训角色设置**性奴**身份。
