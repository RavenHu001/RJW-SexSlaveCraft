## English

**SexSlaveCraft (SSC)** is an adult RimJobWorld expansion built around long-term pawn development. Masters and trainees grow through daily Training and Binding Rituals. As the bond develops, you can specialize pawns for different colony roles, set their individual behavior rules, and carry their progress into another body through Personality Gel.

**SSC 2.5.1** is for RimWorld 1.7. The same ZIP includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

### Masters, Training, and the Chain

Use a **Seed of Desire** to grant a pawn permanent Master qualification. Masters gain experience by personally completing daily Training and Binding Rituals. Daily Training develops the trainee’s Corruption and body-part experience. The Binding Ritual establishes a bond and advances the **Sex Slave Chain**, whose stage affects how far ordinary Training can raise Corruption.

Select a pawn and open **Training** to see their identity, progress, and cooldowns. In **Relationships**, assign several eligible trainers to the same trainee and manage them individually. Trainers can take jobs automatically or be ordered manually, with one active daily Training job per trainee. Eligible bound pawns also have their own self-training option, and Masters can configure damage sharing through their bond.

### Specializations and Personality Gel

SSC has nine paths. **Public Use**, **Cow**, **Training Officer**, **Combatant**, and **Broodmother** develop different roles in the colony. For example, Cows focus on lactation and PNA production, and qualified Training Officers can train other pawns. The **Cat**, **Dog**, **Rabbit**, and **Fox** paths are pet specializations with distinct benefits. Rabbits can develop through cooking and cleaning, while foxes can develop through research and tending.

**Personality Gel** can preserve a pawn’s specialization progress, Master qualification, and personality-linked settings. Extract a personality into gel, edit it at an art bench to finalize a completed specialization, and implant it into an eligible **Hollow**. Physical development such as body-part training stays with the original body.

### Body development and personal settings

Develop specific body parts, including the stomach and uterus, and manage fertility, lactation, and sensitivity for each eligible pawn. Semi- and Full Gelatinization are separate surgery paths. Fully gelatinized pawns can have their gel color and transparency adjusted. Individual behavior and romance settings let you control which interactions a pawn can initiate or accept.

### PNA, equipment, and Ideology

Produce **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** through lactation and refine it into PNA Plus. PNA is used for Chain activation, weapons, and cosmetic reshaping. Eligible bound pawns can maintain a target PNA stage on maps and in caravans. Weapons and emitters give the colony ways to apply PNA across an area. Eligible bodies can also produce **Depravity Gel**, which can be consumed for a temporary mood boost at the cost of slower work.

With **Ideology** installed, **Master-slave Order** adds its own precepts and mood responses. Ideology also enables SSC’s Binding Ritual and the Reshaping Ritual, which can improve a pawn’s Beauty.

### What changed in 2.5.1

- **PNA mist equipment.** Mortar shells and IEDs raise eligible pawns’ PNA load within a five-tile radius. A single-tile emitter builds PNA load over time, with separate controls for radius and output mode. [How to use the mist equipment](../QUICK_START_EN.md#ssc-pna-mist)
- **Relationship commands.** With WOWGAG command list reuse enabled, the relationship card no longer flickers. The **Select All** and **Select Master** buttons use vanilla command behavior.
- **Compatibility and diagnostics.** SSC skips patches that fail to register and continues loading the others. If a required hook is still missing, SSC blocks the affected Daily Training jobs to prevent a start-and-retry loop. **Diagnostics and compatibility** in settings shows which patches were skipped or fell back and why. [2.5.1 release notes](Releases/2.5.1/2.5.1发布说明.md#english)

### Requirements and first steps

Load **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. **Ideology** is required for Binding Rituals, the Master-slave Order meme, and Reshaping Rituals. **Royalty** is optional and adds a techprint requirement for Training research. In-game text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Russian.

When upgrading, back up your saves and replace the whole mod folder. Keep both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. To start training, research **Training**, set the trainee’s SSC identity to **Sex Slave**, and assign an eligible trainer. ## 中文

**SexSlaveCraft（SSC）** 是以长期角色培养为核心的 RimJobWorld 成人向扩展。主人与受训者通过日常调教和绑定仪式成长。随着主从关系发展，你可以按殖民地需要培养不同特化，为每名角色设置行为规则，也能通过人格凝胶把培养成果延续到另一具身体。

**当前版本**为面向 RimWorld 1.7 的 SSC 2.5.1。同一 ZIP 为 RimWorld 1.6 提供 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

### 主人、日常调教与锁链

「**欲望之种**」可为角色解锁永久主人资格。主人亲自完成日常调教或绑定仪式，会积累自己的成长经验。日常调教能提高受训者的恶堕率和部位经验。绑定仪式建立主从关系并推进**性奴锁链**，锁链阶段会影响日常调教能把恶堕率提高到什么程度。

单选角色并打开「**调教**」，即可查看身份、进度和冷却。在「关系」页可为同一受训者指派多名符合条件的调教员，并逐人管理。调教员可以自动接单，也可以手动点名，每名受训者同时只接受一项日常调教。符合条件的绑定角色还可自我调教，主人则可设置基于主从链接的伤害分担。

### 九种特化与人格凝胶

**公交车、奶牛、训导官、战斗员、孕母**各有培养方向。例如奶牛侧重泌乳与 PNA 生产，取得资格的训导官可以调教其他角色。**猫、狗、兔、狐**是四种独立的宠物特化，其中宠物兔可通过烹饪和清洁培养，宠物狐则可通过科研和护理成长。

**人格凝胶**可保存特化进度、主人资格和归属人格的设置。你可以把人格提取成凝胶，在艺术工作台编辑已培养完成的特化以解锁终极形态，再植入符合条件的**空壳**。部位培养等身体开发成果留在原身体。

### 身体开发与个人设置

角色可积累部位经验，并进一步开发胃部和子宫。符合条件的角色可以单独管理生育、泌乳和敏感度。手术还提供半凝胶化与全身凝胶化路线，完成全身凝胶化后可调整凝胶颜色和透明度。个人行为许可和浪漫设置可控制角色能主动发起或接受哪些互动。

### PNA、装备与文化

通过泌乳生产**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**，并进行浓缩。PNA 可用于锁链活化、武器和医美。符合条件的绑定角色可在地图或旅队中自动维持指定的 PNA 阶段。殖民地也可使用 PNA 武器和发烟器，让范围内的目标累积负荷。符合条件的身体还可产出**堕落凝胶**，摄取后可暂时提升心情，代价是工作速度下降。

安装 **Ideology（文化）**后，「**主奴秩序**」提供专属戒律和心情反馈。绑定仪式与可提升美貌的塑貌仪式也依赖该 DLC。

### 2.5.1 新内容

- **PNA 雾气装备。** 雾气迫击炮弹和 IED 提高半径 5 格内有效目标的 PNA 负荷。单格发烟器可持续累积负荷，半径与输出档位可分别调节。[雾气装备入门](../读我，玩法介绍.md#ssc-pna-mist)
- **主从关系命令栏。** 修复 WOWGAG 复用命令列表时的关系卡片闪烁。「选择所有性奴」和「选择主人」采用原版命令交互。
- **兼容与诊断。** SSC 会跳过注册失败的补丁并继续加载其余补丁。必要钩子仍缺失时，SSC 会停止派发受影响的日常调教任务，避免反复开始、退出和重派。可在设置的「诊断与兼容」查看补丁跳过或回退的原因。[2.5.1 版本说明](Releases/2.5.1/2.5.1发布说明.md#中文)
