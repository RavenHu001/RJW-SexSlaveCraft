## English

**Train pawns, develop specialized roles, and carry their personalities into new bodies.**

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult **RimJobWorld expansion** that connects long-term pawn progression with relationship management, body modification and colony life. Build bonds between Masters and trained pawns, choose how each character develops, and preserve their personality through **Personality Gel**.

**Development version: 2.4.9, unreleased. Public download: 2.4.8.** The development additions below are not in the public package. The package supports **RimWorld 1.7 and 1.6**. Features below describe **1.7**; **1.6 retains SSC 2.4.1 content**. Text is included in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Russian.

### A progression system for both sides of the bond

Assign trainers, give manual orders or allow eligible self-training. Training develops Corruption and body parts; **Binding Rituals** advance Chain stages and unlock more personal controls. The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification, while personal participation in Training and Rituals develops four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire**.

The Training panel and bond overview bring growth, relationships and trainer assignments together. In 2.4.8, you can **assign a trainer and enable Training work in one action**, or enable work for the current assignee.

### Seven specializations

| Specialization | Main role |
| --- | --- |
| **Public Use** | Trade and social benefits, with additional trader interactions. |
| **Cow** | Greater milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs. |
| **Training Officer** | Qualified pawns train others on their Master's behalf. |
| **Pet Cat** | Mobility and melee evasion; final **Gentle Comfort** offers support. |
| **Pet Dog** | Animal handling; final **Animal Training** helps tame or train animals. |
| **Combatant** | Combat and survival bonuses, with final **Combat Overdrive**. |
| **Broodmother — new in 2.4.8** | Fertility, pregnancy, childcare and Beauty benefits, with offspring presets and final **Conception**. |

Each direction retains its own progress. **Complete the relevant development, process its Personality Gel and implant it to obtain the final form.**

### New in 2.4.9 development

- **Three emitter tiers:** a non-electric censer, electric atomizer and spacer emitter cover different technology levels. They apply the final loaded RJW humpshroom effect within the same room and radius, with dedicated art and four-language text.
- **Personal PNA maintenance:** choose Off or one of five activation targets. Eligible pawns consume ordinary PNA before ordinary map work without interrupting current tasks; caravans use personal/shared stock including pack animals, pause during night rest and run after vanilla drug policies.
- **Dedicated Conception icon:** the Broodmother ability now has its own bud artwork. Device/art, map/caravan maintenance and icon acceptance are closed within their recorded scope. See [2.4.9 development notes](Releases/2.4.9/2.4.9发布说明.md#english).

### Introduced in 2.4.8

- **Broodmother progression:** eligible Training, teaching, baby play and live births build progress. New selection requires **Chain stage 3 and completed uterine development**.
- **Preconception choices:** progressively unlock offspring sex, species-source and initial-imprint presets. Choices apply when offspring are actually generated; pregnancy and labor lock editing. Native gene and litter-size rules remain in force.
- **Ultimate Broodmother's Conception:** choose an eligible adult, non-hostile humanlike target on the same map. The caster approaches and completes a shared RJW scene before pregnancy is established **in the caster**. Success starts a **seven-day cooldown**; interruption grants neither pregnancy nor success cooldown.
- **Three-state personality imprints:** an initial form and two differentiated forms follow Seed and new-binding rules. Differentiated forms lock their corresponding SSC role and provide age, immunity and stat effects. Imprints travel with Personality Gel.
- **More consistent body attributes:** PNA effects and body-development stages contribute to the **Orgasm Frequency Multiplier** alongside personal sensitivity. Completed **Hollow** bodies no longer have Joy, Comfort, Beauty, Outdoors, Indoors or RoomSize needs.

### Personal controls, body development and personality transfer

Set **activity permissions, romance behavior and partner categories per pawn**. Chain stages unlock more controls and one-time relationship orders. Separate reset buttons restore behavior or romance preferences while preserving relationships and memories; romance events have contextual mood and log feedback. Individual ability controls include milk production, fertility, damage sharing and optional **Bestow Bliss**.

Research and surgery open **uterine and stomach development**, with fertility control and feeding benefits. Other options include permanent lactation, limb modification, optional body sensitivity and full gelatinization with individual **color, opacity and rendering** settings. Personal sensitivity and fertility controls default to off.

**Personality Gel preserves personality-based growth and preferences across eligible bodies.** Uterine/stomach development, sensitivity, PNA production choices, gel appearance and preconception settings stay with the body. Current Masters cannot undergo personality extraction. Replicas and self-cloning remain deferred.

### PNA production, equipment and colony culture

Completed genital Training enables **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** production. Pause or resume it per pawn without losing batch progress, then research concentration and processing. PNA has drug effects on ordinary pawns and beneficial activation effects on eligible Chain-bound bodies; current Masters are immune to its drug effects.

**Nine PNA weapons** span medieval, industrial and spacer technology. **Master-slave Order** adds 28 precepts and cultural feedback with Ideology. **Cosmetic surgery and Reshaping Rituals** use PNA and medicine to improve vanilla Beauty, up to **+2**.

### Getting started

1. Load matching **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. **Ideology** is needed for Binding Rituals, culture and Reshaping Rituals; **Royalty** is optional.
2. Research **Training**, configure the trainer, and give the target SSC's **Sex Slave identity**. Vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.
3. Configure Training permissions and assignments, then progress toward binding and specialization.

When updating, back up saves, close the game and **replace the whole old mod folder**, retaining both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. Saves referencing old 1.7 launcher definitions removed in 2.4.3 have no migration path.

Supported UAP manual group requests are integrated. Pet Rabbit selection and legacy RimTalk integration remain unavailable. Traditional Chinese, English and Russian romance text is included; its in-game display still awaits separate confirmation.

## 中文

**培养角色、选择特化分工，让已经成长的人格在新的身体中延续。**

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是将长期角色成长、关系管理、身体改造与殖民地生活结合起来的 **RimJobWorld 成人向扩展**。建立主人与受训角色之间的绑定，选择各自的发展方向，再通过**人格凝胶**保存和延续已经培养的人格。

**当前开发版：2.4.9，未发布；公开下载：2.4.8。** 下述开发新增尚未进入公开安装包。安装包支持 **RimWorld 1.7 与 1.6**。下述功能以 **1.7** 为准，**1.6 保留 SSC 2.4.1 内容**。提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语文本。

### 绑定双方都能持续成长

指派调教员、下达手动命令，或允许符合条件的自我调教。调教积累恶堕与部位开发，**契约仪式**推进锁链阶段，逐步开放更多个人控制。**欲望之种**提供永久主人资格；主人亲自参与调教和仪式，还能推进**欲望共生**四阶段成长。

调教页与关系概览集中展示成长、绑定和调教员指派。2.4.8 支持**指定调教员时一并启用调教工作**，也可为当前指定者直接启用。

| 特化 | 主要用途 |
| --- | --- |
| **公交车** | 贸易与社交收益，以及额外的交易互动。 |
| **奶牛** | 强化产奶与储奶，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。 |
| **训导官** | 符合资格后，代主人培养其他角色。 |
| **宠物猫** | 强化移动与近战闪避；终极能力**贴心安抚**提供支援。 |
| **宠物狗** | 强化动物工作；终极能力**驯兽**帮助驯服或训练动物。 |
| **战斗员** | 战斗与生存增益，以及终极能力**战斗超频**。 |
| **孕母——2.4.8 新增** | 生育、孕期、育儿与魅力收益，后代预设及终极技能**受孕**。 |

各方向分别保留培养进度。**完成对应培养，再加工人格凝胶并植入，即可获得终极形态。**

- **个人PNA阶段维持：** 选择关闭或五档目标。合格角色在普通地图工作前补原液，不打断当前任务；旅队使用自有／共享含驮畜库存，夜休暂停，原版服药政策先行。
- **受孕专用图标：** 孕母技能改用花苞图案。设备／美术、地图与旅队维持及图标均已完成对应范围的实机验收，见[2.4.9开发说明](Releases/2.4.9/2.4.9发布说明.md#中文)。

### 2.4.8 已有内容

- **孕母培养：** 合格调教、教学、陪婴儿玩耍及活产均有相应培养途径。新选要求**锁链阶段 3 与完整子宫开发**。
- **孕前预设：** 随培养逐步开放后代性别、物种来源和初始印记设置，在真正生成后代时应用；孕期与临产禁止修改。原有基因遗传与胎数机制保留。
- **终极孕母的受孕技能：** 选择同图符合条件的非敌对成年类人对象，走近并正常完成同场 RJW 任务后，才建立**施法者自身的妊娠**。成功后起算 **7 天冷却**；中断不受孕、不扣成功冷却。
- **三态人格印记：** 初始态与两种分化态，沿种子和新绑定规则获得或分化。分化态锁定对应 SSC 身份，提供年龄、免疫及属性效果，并随人格凝胶流转。
- **身体属性补全：** PNA 药效、部位开发与个人敏感度共同影响**高潮频率系数**；**空壳完成态**禁用娱乐、舒适、美观、户外、室内、空间六项需求。

### 个人管理、身体开发与人格延续

为每名角色分别设置**行为许可、浪漫行为和对象类别**。锁链阶段逐步开放更多控制及一次性关系命令。行为和浪漫各有恢复默认按钮，重置偏好时保留关系与记忆；浪漫事件配有相应心情和日志反馈。个人能力控制包括泌乳、生育、伤害分担及可选的**欢愉恩赐**。

研究与手术开启**子宫开发和胃部开发**，带来生育控制与进食收益。还可选择永久泌乳、局部肢体改造、个人身体敏感度及全身凝胶化，并单独调整凝胶**颜色、不透明度和渲染**。个人敏感度和生育开关默认关闭。

**人格凝胶让属于人格的成长与偏好在合格身体间延续。** 子宫／胃部开发、敏感度、PNA 生产选择、凝胶外观和孕前预设留在身体上。当前主人不能进行人格排泄；复制体与自克隆仍暂缓开发。

### PNA 生产、装备与殖民地文化

生殖器调教完成后，可生产**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**。个人暂停／恢复生产会保留当前批次进度，后续研究可开放浓缩与加工。PNA 对普通角色产生药物效果，为合格锁链身体提供正面的微械活化；当前主人免疫其药效。

**九把 PNA 武器**覆盖中世纪、工业与太空科技。安装 Ideology 后，**主奴秩序**提供 28 条戒律及文化反馈；**医美手术与塑貌仪式**消耗 PNA 和药品，逐档改善原版美貌，最高 **+2**。

### 如何开始

1. 使用与游戏匹配的版本，按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 加载。契约仪式、文化和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）DLC**；**Royalty（皇权）可选**。
2. 研究**调教**、配置调教员，并为目标设置 SSC 的**性奴身份**；原版奴隶身份不能替代。
3. 设置调教许可与指派，开始培养，再逐步推进绑定与特化。

更新时备份存档、退出游戏，并**完整替换旧模组文件夹**，保留两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。引用 2.4.3 已从 1.7 移除的旧发射器定义的存档，不提供迁移支持。

已对接受支持的 UAP 手动多人入口。宠物兔选择入口与旧 RimTalk 对接仍不可用。繁中、英语和俄语浪漫文本已经提供，实机显示仍待独立确认。
