## English

**SexSlaveCraft (SSC)** is an adult RimJobWorld expansion for RimWorld. Assign trainers, develop a pawn’s specialization over time, and advance their Chain through Binding Rituals. Once training is complete, Personality Gel lets you transfer that pawn’s learned progress to an eligible body and unlock a final specialization.

**SSC 2.5.1** is for RimWorld 1.7. The same ZIP includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

### Main features

- **Manage training in one window.** Select a pawn and open the **Training** tab to see progress and cooldowns, manage the trainer roster, and change individual settings for behavior, romance, fertility, lactation, and gel-body appearance.
- **Choose from nine specializations.** Develop a Public Use, Cow, Training Officer, Combatant, or Broodmother specialization, or pick a Cat, Dog, Rabbit, or Fox path. Personality Gel carries progress and preferences tied to the personality. Physical development stays with the original body.
- **Produce and use PNA.** Use Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid for Chain activation, weapons, and cosmetic procedures. Eligible pawns can maintain their PNA stage automatically on maps and in caravans.

### What changed in 2.5.1

- **PNA mist equipment.** Mortar shells and IEDs raise eligible pawns’ PNA load within a five-tile radius. A single-tile emitter builds PNA load over time, with separate controls for radius and output mode. [How to use the mist equipment](../QUICK_START_EN.md#ssc-pna-mist)
- **Relationship commands.** With WOWGAG command list reuse enabled, the relationship card no longer flickers. The **Select All** and **Select Master** buttons use vanilla command behavior.
- **Compatibility and diagnostics.** SSC skips patches that fail to register and continues loading the others. If a required hook is still missing, SSC blocks the affected Daily Training jobs to prevent a start-and-retry loop. **Diagnostics and compatibility** in settings shows which patches were skipped or fell back and why. [2.5.1 release notes](Releases/2.5.1/2.5.1发布说明.md#english)

### Requirements and first steps

Load **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. **Ideology** is required for Binding Rituals, the Master-slave Order meme, and Reshaping Rituals. **Royalty** is optional and adds a techprint requirement for Training research. In-game text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Russian.

When upgrading, back up your saves and replace the whole mod folder. Keep both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. To start training, research **Training**, set the trainee’s SSC identity to **Sex Slave**, and assign an eligible trainer. ## 中文

**SexSlaveCraft（SSC）** 是 RimWorld 的成人向 RimJobWorld 扩展。你可以为角色安排调教员，逐步培养特化，并通过绑定仪式推进锁链阶段。培养完成后，可用人格凝胶将角色的培养成果转移到符合条件的身体，并解锁终极特化。

**当前版本**为面向 RimWorld 1.7 的 SSC 2.5.1。同一 ZIP 为 RimWorld 1.6 提供 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

### 主要玩法

- **在同一个窗口管理调教。** 单选角色后点击「调教」，查看进度与冷却、管理调教员名单，并按角色设置行为许可、浪漫偏好、生育、泌乳与凝胶外观。
- **选择九条特化路线。** 可培养公交车、奶牛、训导官、战斗员或孕母，也可选择猫、狗、兔、狐四种宠物特化。人格凝胶携带归属人格的进度和偏好，身体开发成果保留在原身体。
- **生产并使用 PNA。** 紫色纳米催情液可用于锁链活化、武器和医美。符合条件的角色可在地图和旅队中自动维持 PNA 阶段。

### 2.5.1 新内容

- **PNA 雾气装备。** 雾气迫击炮弹和 IED 提高半径 5 格内有效目标的 PNA 负荷。单格发烟器可持续累积负荷，半径与输出档位可分别调节。[雾气装备入门](../读我，玩法介绍.md#ssc-pna-mist)
- **主从关系命令栏。** 修复 WOWGAG 复用命令列表时的关系卡片闪烁。「选择所有性奴」和「选择主人」采用原版命令交互。
- **兼容与诊断。** SSC 会跳过注册失败的补丁并继续加载其余补丁。必要钩子仍缺失时，SSC 会停止派发受影响的日常调教任务，避免反复开始、退出和重派。可在设置的「诊断与兼容」查看补丁跳过或回退的原因。[2.5.1 版本说明](Releases/2.5.1/2.5.1发布说明.md#中文)

### 依赖与入门

按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 的顺序加载。绑定仪式、「主奴秩序」模因和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）**。**Royalty（皇权）**可选，安装后调教研究需要蓝图。游戏内文本提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语。

升级时先备份存档，再完整替换旧模组文件夹，保留两套版本目录和 `LoadFolders.xml`。初次游玩可先完成「调教」研究，将角色的 SSC 身份设为「性奴」，再指派符合条件的调教员。[快速入门](../读我，玩法介绍.md) · [完整机制](../机制详解.md)
