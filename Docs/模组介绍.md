## English

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult RimJobWorld expansion combining pawn development, master–slave bonds, colony specializations, personality transfer and body modification. Version **2.4.1** adds redesigned PNA effects and the optional **Bestow Bliss** Master ability.

**Training and bonds**

Arrange automatic Training, manual orders or eligible self-training. Build Corruption and six body-part tracks for work, movement, social and combat benefits; use Binding Rituals to advance Chain stages.

Bonds affect permissions, shared beds, mood and relationships. Masters gain the **Libidinal Resonance Field** and optional damage sharing with eligible bound pawns.

**Six specializations**

- **Public Use:** Trade and social abilities, with additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualify to train other pawns on the Master's behalf, with duty and trust feedback.
- **Pet Cat:** Movement and melee dodge; final **Soothing Resonance** calms an eligible pawn's current mental state or grants temporary mood and work bonuses.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Directed Taming** directly tames an eligible wild animal or completes one training project for an owned animal.
- **Combatant:** Melee and ranged combat bonuses and damage reduction; the final form unlocks **Combat Overdrive**.

Each direction retains its own ordinary training progress. Completing ordinary training does **not** automatically grant the final specialization: process the Personality Gel, then implant it to finalize.

**Master qualification and growth**

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification, obtained through an eligible initial quest or rare quest/ruin rewards. With Royalty, it also supplies missing Training techprints without completing research.

Direct Master selection defaults to **off** for new settings; saved choices remain. Enable it to bypass qualification. Seed use does not change identity—select Master afterward.

Personally completed Training and Rituals advance four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire**, improving physical capacities, Social Impact and Injury Healing. View growth and configure damage sharing in the Master panel.

**2.4.1: PNA and Bestow Bliss**

PNA has five-stage effects: ordinary exposure or positive **nanomachine activation** for eligible Chain-bound pawns. Current Masters are immune to its drug effects. Exposure and addiction remain with the body through personality transfer; new SSC clones do not inherit them.

Enable **Bestow Bliss** in the Master's ability panel; it defaults to off. Eligible ordinary RJW paired interactions or native Lovin can provide PNA, limited Corruption growth and mood/opinion feedback on completion. RJW interactions can also grant weak body-part development; native Lovin cannot. The ability creates no bond or Chain advancement.

**Personality transfer and body modification**

Use **Personality Gel** to transfer a personality into an eligible Hollow or finalize a specialization. Master qualification and growth travel with the personality; new clones inherit neither them nor Master identity.

Unlock permanent lactation and PNA production, modify limbs, or gelatinize an eligible Hollow for stronger capabilities, protection and regeneration. Failed transformation can cost a limb or body.

**Requirements and availability**

- **RimWorld 1.6**, with **Harmony and RimJobWorld** loaded before SSC.
- **Ideology** is needed for Binding Rituals and their stage-progression route.
- **Royalty is optional**; its Training techprint requirement is separate from personal Master qualification.
- Training targets require SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable. Legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. The mod contains adult themes of slavery, coercive relationships and body modification.

## 中文

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是 RimJobWorld 的成人向扩展模组，结合角色培养、主从绑定、殖民地分工、人格转移与身体改造。**2.4.1** 新增重置后的 PNA 机制与主人可选能力**欢愉恩赐**。

**培养与绑定**

安排自动调教、手动任务或符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与六项部位开发，获得工作、移动、社交和战斗方面的收益，再通过契约仪式推进锁链阶段。

绑定关系影响行为许可、同床、心情与社交。主人可获得**灵欲共振场**增益，并开启符合条件的绑定角色参与的伤害分担。

**六种特化方向**

- **公交车：** 偏向贸易与社交，并包含交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶能力，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 取得资格后代主人培养其他角色，并获得职责与信任反馈。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**安抚共鸣**可平复符合条件角色的当前精神状态，或提供短期心情与工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**定向驯导**可直接驯服符合条件的野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 获得近远程战斗加成与减伤；终极形态解锁**战斗超频**。

各方向分别保留普通培养进度。**培养完成不等于终极化**：需要进一步加工人格凝胶，再经植入获得终极特化。

**主人资格与成长**

**欲望之种**赋予永久主人资格，可通过符合条件的初次任务或稀有任务／遗迹奖励取得。有 Royalty 时，还会补齐调教研究缺少的蓝图，但不会直接完成研究。

新设置中，“允许直接选择主人”默认**关闭**，已有保存值保留；可开启此设置绕过资格门槛。种子不会自动切换身份，使用后仍需自行选择主人。

主人亲自完成日常调教和整场契约仪式，推进**欲望共生**的四个阶段，强化身体能力、社交影响与伤口愈合。在主人面板查看成长并设置伤害分担。

**2.4.1：PNA 与欢愉恩赐**

PNA 纳米催情液采用五阶段效果：普通角色承受催情侵蚀，符合条件的锁链对象获得正面的**微械活化**。当前主人免疫其药效。负荷与成瘾留在身体中，不随人格转移，新生 SSC 克隆也不继承来源状态。

在主人能力面板开启默认关闭的**欢愉恩赐**，可让符合条件的普通 RJW 双人互动或原版 Lovin 在完成后提供 PNA、有限恶堕成长及心情／好感反馈。RJW 互动还可提供部位弱开发，原版 Lovin 不提供。此能力不建立绑定，也不推进锁链。

**人格转移与身体改造**

通过**人格凝胶**将人格转移到合格空壳，或加工为终极特化。主人资格与成长随人格流转；新克隆不继承它们或主人身份。

解锁永久泌乳与 PNA 生产，改造局部肢体，或为空壳进行全身凝胶化，强化身体机能、防护与再生。改造失败可能损失肢体或整个身体。

**依赖与可用内容**

- **RimWorld 1.6**，**Harmony、RimJobWorld** 加载在 SSC 之前。
- **契约仪式及其阶段进阶路线需要 Ideology（文化）DLC。**
- **Royalty（皇权）为可选 DLC**；调教研究蓝图与个人主人资格分别管理。
- 受训目标必须设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**；原版奴隶身份不能替代。

宠物兔选择入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 兼容暂停。本模组包含奴役、强制关系与身体改造等成人题材。
