# RJW-SexSlaveCraft · TieJin Modify — 2.4.3

**RimWorld 1.7 / 1.6** | [English](#english) / [中文](#中文)

## English

**Train pawns, build bonds, develop colony roles and transfer personalities into new bodies.** SexSlaveCraft (SSC) expands RimJobWorld with long-term progression, six specializations and body modification.

**Version support:** the 2.4.3 package includes both runtimes. **RimWorld 1.7 receives the current features; 1.6 retains the frozen SSC 2.4.1 content.** The game selects the matching version automatically.

**2.4.3 highlights:** a visual bond overview, more accessible pre-industrial progression, nine PNA weapons across three eras, and Bestow Bliss doses that reflect the Master's body.

**Training, bonds and Master growth**

Use automatic Training, manual orders or eligible self-training to develop Corruption and six body-part tracks. Binding Rituals advance Chain stages, while bonds affect permissions, shared beds, mood and colony bonuses.

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification. Masters grow through four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire** by personally completing Training and Rituals. Their panel shows growth and controls damage sharing. The new bond overview adds portrait navigation, trainer assignment and group selection of available bound members on the current map.

**Six specializations**

- **Public Use:** Trade, social influence and additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualified pawns train others on their Master's behalf, with duty and trust feedback.
- **Pet Cat:** Movement and melee dodge; final **Soothing Resonance** calms eligible mental states or grants mood and work bonuses.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Directed Taming** tames an eligible wild animal or completes one training project for an owned animal.
- **Combatant:** Melee and ranged bonuses, damage reduction and final **Combat Overdrive**.

Each direction retains its ordinary training progress. **Finishing training does not automatically grant the final form:** process the Personality Gel, then implant it to finalize the specialization.

**PNA weapons from medieval to spacer technology — 1.7 only**

- **Medieval:** Short spear, blowpipe and light crossbow, crafted with normal PNA.
- **Industrial:** Short whip, repeating needle gun and precision needle rifle, crafted with concentrated PNA.
- **Spacer:** Silk whip, pulse gun and projector gun. Main attacks let valid Chain-bound bodies absorb PNA without wounds and repair an eligible injury.

The first two eras deliver PNA through fresh qualifying wounds; injuries remain and can be fatal. Spacer attacks still respect shields and cover. Dedicated artwork, potency displays, purple projectile trails and absorption hearts make the weapons easier to distinguish. Finished weapons require no separate ammunition or fluid refills.

Lower-tech research opens more SSC progression before industrial technology. After PNA Concentration research, campfires and compatible stoves can turn **10 normal PNA into 1 concentrated PNA**, alongside the more efficient drug-lab recipes.

**PNA and Bestow Bliss**

PNA has five stages of ordinary effects; valid Chain-bound pawns instead receive positive **nanomachine activation**. Current Masters are immune to its drug effects.

The optional **Bestow Bliss** ability provides PNA, limited Corruption growth and mood/opinion feedback after eligible completed RJW paired interactions or native Lovin. It defaults to off. In 2.4.3 on 1.7, the PNA dose scales with the Master's current fluid-producing organs. The ability creates no bond or Chain advancement.

**Personality transfer and body modification**

Use **Personality Gel** to move a personality into an eligible **Hollow**, or process it for a final specialization. Master qualification and growth follow the personality; PNA states and gel appearance belong to the body.

Unlock permanent lactation and PNA production, modify limbs, or fully gelatinize an eligible Hollow. On 1.7, fully gelatinized pawns can set their own color, opacity and rendering preference in **Gel Appearance** on the Training tab, preserving vanilla eyes and supported Facial Animation features. Failed transformation can cost a limb or body.

**Requirements and upgrading**

- **Harmony and RimJobWorld**, matching your game version and loaded before SSC.
- **Ideology** for Binding Rituals and their stage-progression route.
- **Royalty is optional.** Seeds can supply missing Training techprints; research still needs to be completed.
- Training targets need SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

Close the game and replace the old mod folder completely when updating. Keep both version folders and the included loading configuration intact.

**1.7 upgrade compatibility:** the old Basic PNA Launcher, projectile, recipe and dedicated research have been removed. Saves referencing those definitions have no migration path. Frozen 1.6 retains its original content.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable; legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. Adult themes include slavery, coercive relationships and body modification.

## 中文

**培养角色、建立绑定、发展殖民地分工，再让人格在新的身体中延续。** SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是 RimJobWorld 的成人向扩展，提供长期成长、六种特化与身体改造玩法。

**版本支持：** 2.4.3 安装包同时包含两套运行内容。**RimWorld 1.7 使用当前新增功能，1.6 保留 SSC 2.4.1 冻结内容。** 游戏会自动选择对应版本。

**2.4.3 亮点：** 可视化主从关系概览、更方便的低科技起步、贯穿三个科技阶段的九把 PNA 武器，以及随主人身体产液能力变化的欢愉恩赐剂量。

**培养、绑定与主人成长**

通过自动调教、手动命令或符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与六项部位开发。契约仪式推进锁链阶段，绑定关系则影响行为许可、同床、心情与殖民地增益。

**欲望之种**赋予永久主人资格。主人亲自完成日常调教与整场仪式，推进**欲望共生**四阶段；在主人面板查看成长、设置伤害分担。新增的关系概览支持头像定位、调教员指派，以及批选当前地图可用的绑定成员。

**六种特化方向**

- **公交车：** 贸易、社交影响与交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 取得资格后代主人培养其他角色，并获得职责与信任反馈。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**安抚共鸣**可平复合格目标的精神状态，或提供心情与工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**定向驯导**可驯服合格野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 获得近远程战斗加成与减伤，终极形态解锁**战斗超频**。

各方向分别保留普通培养进度。**培养完成不等于终极化**：需要加工人格凝胶，再经植入获得终极特化。

**从中世纪到太空的 PNA 武器——仅限 1.7**

- **中世纪：** 短矛、吹箭筒、轻弩，制造时使用普通 PNA 原液。
- **工业：** 短鞭、速射针枪、精确针枪，制造时使用浓缩 PNA。
- **太空：** 丝鞭、脉冲枪、投射枪；主攻击可让有效锁链身体无伤吸收 PNA，并修复一处合格伤势。

前两代武器必须造成合格的新伤口才能施药，真实伤势仍可能致死；太空攻击仍受护盾与掩体拦截。专用美术、效力栏、紫色投射尾迹与受药爱心让不同武器更易辨识。成品无需独立弹药或补液。

低科技研究让工业前殖民地更早接入部分 SSC 玩法。研究 PNA 浓缩后，可在篝火或兼容灶台用 **10 份普通原液制成 1 份浓缩液**；药物台仍保留更高效的配方。

**PNA 与欢愉恩赐**

PNA 纳米催情液对普通角色提供五阶段效果，有效锁链对象则获得正面的**微械活化**。当前主人免疫其药效。

可选能力**欢愉恩赐**在合格的普通 RJW 双人互动或原版 Lovin 完成后，提供 PNA、有限恶堕成长及心情／好感反馈，默认关闭。2.4.3 的 1.7 版本会按主人当前产液器官计算 PNA 剂量；该能力不建立绑定，也不推进锁链。

**人格转移与身体改造**

通过**人格凝胶**将人格转移到合格**空壳**，或加工为终极特化。主人资格与成长随人格延续，PNA 状态和凝胶外观则属于当前身体。

解锁永久泌乳与 PNA 生产、改造局部肢体，或为空壳进行全身凝胶化。在 1.7 中，完全凝胶化角色可从“调教”页的**凝胶外观**区独立设置颜色、不透明度和个人渲染，保留原版眼部及已支持的 Facial Animation 五官表现。改造失败可能损失肢体或整个身体。

**依赖与升级**

- **Harmony、RimJobWorld** 使用与游戏匹配的版本，加载在 SSC 之前。
- **契约仪式及其阶段进阶路线需要 Ideology（文化）DLC。**
- **Royalty（皇权）为可选 DLC。** 欲望之种可补齐调教研究缺少的蓝图，之后仍需完成研究。
- 受训目标必须设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**，原版奴隶身份不能替代。

更新时退出游戏，完整替换旧模组文件夹，并保留两套版本目录与加载配置。

**1.7 升级兼容：** 旧简易 PNA 发射器、弹丸、配方及专属研究已移除，不提供引用这些旧定义的存档迁移。1.6 冻结内容仍保留原有功能。

宠物兔选择入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 对接暂停。本模组包含奴役、强制关系与身体改造等成人题材。
