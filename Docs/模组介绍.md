# RJW-SexSlaveCraft · TieJin Modify — 2.4.2

**RimWorld 1.7 / 1.6** | [English](#english) / [中文](#中文)

## English

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult RimJobWorld expansion combining long-term pawn development, master–slave bonds, specialized colony roles, personality transfer and body modification.

**Version support:** the 2.4.2 download contains both game versions. **RimWorld 1.7 receives the new 2.4.2 features; RimWorld 1.6 retains the frozen SSC 2.4.1 content.** The game selects the appropriate runtime automatically.

**New in 2.4.2: individual gel appearance — 1.7 only**

Give fully gelatinized pawns their own color, opacity and rendering preference from **Gel Appearance** on the Training tab. Defaults are dark purple at 85% opacity. Turning rendering off preserves the saved appearance and gameplay bonuses.

Body and head rendering are coordinated, preserving vanilla eyes and supported Facial Animation features. These appearance controls are available after full gelatinization.

**Training, bonds and Master growth**

Arrange automatic Training, manual orders or eligible self-training. Build Corruption and six body-part tracks for work, movement, social and combat benefits; use Binding Rituals to advance Chain stages.

The **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification. Direct Master selection defaults to off for new settings, with an optional bypass in settings. Personally completed Training and Rituals advance four stages of **Symbiosis of Desire**. The Master panel displays growth and controls damage sharing; bonds also affect permissions, shared beds, mood and the **Libidinal Resonance Field**.

**Six specializations**

- **Public Use:** Trade and social abilities, with additional trader interactions.
- **Cow:** Milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs.
- **Training Officer:** Qualify to train other pawns on the Master's behalf, with duty and trust feedback.
- **Pet Cat:** Movement and melee dodge; final **Soothing Resonance** calms an eligible pawn's mental state or grants mood and work bonuses.
- **Pet Dog:** Animal handling; final **Directed Taming** tames an eligible wild animal or completes one training project for an owned animal.
- **Combatant:** Melee and ranged combat bonuses and damage reduction; the final form unlocks **Combat Overdrive**.

Each direction retains its ordinary training progress. **Completion does not automatically grant the final specialization:** process the Personality Gel, then implant it to finalize.

**PNA and Bestow Bliss**

PNA has five-stage ordinary effects or positive **nanomachine activation** for eligible Chain-bound pawns. Current Masters are immune to its drug effects.

The optional **Bestow Bliss** ability lets eligible completed ordinary RJW paired interactions or native Lovin provide PNA, limited Corruption growth and mood/opinion feedback. Ordinary RJW interactions can also grant weak body-part development; native Lovin cannot. The ability defaults to off and creates no bond or Chain advancement.

**Personality transfer and body modification**

Use **Personality Gel** to transfer a personality into an eligible Hollow or finalize a specialization. Master qualification and growth travel with the personality; PNA exposure, addiction and the new gel appearance settings belong to the body. New SSC clones do not inherit the source's Master records or PNA states, and their gel appearance initializes independently.

Unlock permanent lactation and PNA production, modify limbs or gelatinize an eligible Hollow for stronger capabilities, protection and regeneration. Failed transformation can cost a limb or body.

**Current 1.7 branch: medieval PNA weapons**

The short spear, blowpipe and light crossbow deliver ordinary PNA through fresh wounds, with distinct melee, rapid close-range and slower long-range roles. They have dedicated art, a baseline potency stat and purple projectile trails. Inverted hearts mark accepted drug input; the spearhead glows on contact. Wounds remain and can still be fatal; the existing launcher keeps its separate behavior.

These branch additions, along with low-tech adaptation and the bond overview, have not been repackaged into the existing 2.4.2 download. See the [current completion audit](Development/Completed/SSC武器拓展首批完成核对.md) and [branch weapon rules](../PLAYER_GUIDE_EN.md#116-medieval-pna-weapons-and-visual-feedback).

**Requirements and installation**

- **Harmony and RimJobWorld**, matching your game version and loaded before SSC.
- **Ideology** for Binding Rituals and their stage-progression route.
- **Royalty is optional**; a Seed of Desire can supply missing Training techprints without completing research.
- Training targets require SSC's **Sex Slave identity**; vanilla slave status alone is insufficient.

When updating, close the game and replace the old mod folder completely. Keep both version folders and the included loading configuration together.

Pet Rabbit selection remains unavailable. Legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. The mod contains adult themes of slavery, coercive relationships and body modification.

## 中文

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是 RimJobWorld 的成人向扩展模组，结合长期角色培养、主从绑定、殖民地分工、人格转移与身体改造。

**版本支持：** 2.4.2 安装包同时包含两套游戏版本内容。**RimWorld 1.7 使用 2.4.2 新功能，RimWorld 1.6 保留 SSC 2.4.1 冻结内容。** 游戏会自动选择对应版本加载。

**2.4.2 新增：个体凝胶外观——仅限 1.7**

完全凝胶化的角色可在“调教”页的**凝胶外观**区，独立设置颜色、不透明度和个人渲染开关。默认深紫色、不透明度 85%；关闭渲染仍保留配色与玩法属性。

身体与头部渲染统一适配，保留原版眼部及已支持的 Facial Animation 五官表现。完成全身凝胶化后即可使用这些外观设置。

**培养、绑定与主人成长**

安排自动调教、手动任务或符合条件的自我调教，积累恶堕与六项部位开发，获得工作、移动、社交和战斗方面的收益，再通过契约仪式推进锁链阶段。

**欲望之种**赋予永久主人资格。新设置中直接选择主人默认关闭，也可在设置中开启以绕过资格门槛。主人亲自完成日常调教和整场契约仪式，推进**欲望共生**四阶段；在主人面板查看成长并设置伤害分担。绑定关系还影响行为许可、同床、心情及**灵欲共振场**增益。

**六种特化方向**

- **公交车：** 偏向贸易与社交，并包含交易后的额外互动。
- **奶牛：** 强化产奶与储奶能力，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。
- **训导官：** 取得资格后代主人培养其他角色，并获得职责与信任反馈。
- **宠物猫：** 强化移动与近战闪避；终极**安抚共鸣**可平复合格角色的精神状态，或提供心情与工作增益。
- **宠物狗：** 强化动物工作；终极**定向驯导**可驯服合格野生动物，或完成己方动物的一项训练。
- **战斗员：** 获得近远程战斗加成与减伤；终极形态解锁**战斗超频**。

各方向分别保留普通培养进度。**培养完成不等于终极化**：需要加工人格凝胶，再经植入获得终极特化。

**PNA 与欢愉恩赐**

PNA 纳米催情液对普通角色提供五阶段效果，符合条件的锁链对象则获得正面的**微械活化**。当前主人免疫其药效。

可选能力**欢愉恩赐**让符合条件的普通 RJW 双人互动或原版 Lovin 在完成后提供 PNA、有限恶堕成长及心情／好感反馈。普通 RJW 互动还可提供部位弱开发，原版 Lovin 不提供。该能力默认关闭，不建立绑定，也不推进锁链。

**人格转移与身体改造**

通过**人格凝胶**将人格转移到合格空壳，或加工为终极特化。主人资格与成长随人格流转；PNA 负荷、成瘾和新增的凝胶外观设置属于身体。新生 SSC 克隆不继承来源的主人记录与 PNA 状态，凝胶外观也独立初始化。

解锁永久泌乳与 PNA 生产，改造局部肢体，或为空壳进行全身凝胶化，强化身体机能、防护与再生。改造失败可能损失肢体或整个身体。

**当前 1.7 分支：原始 PNA 武器**

短矛、吹箭筒和轻弩通过新伤口送入普通原液，分别承担近战、中近距离速射与较远距离准确投送；配有专用图、基线效力属性及紫色尾迹。倒置紫心表示成功受药，短矛泛光表示命中接触；真实伤口仍保留，仍可能致死，旧发射器维持自身物理分支。

这些分支增量及低科技适配、主从关系概览尚未重新打包进既有 2.4.2 下载。当前范围见[完成核对](Development/Completed/SSC武器拓展首批完成核对.md)，数值与获取见[武器规则](../机制详解.md#116-原始纳米催情液武器与视觉反馈)。

**依赖与安装**

- **Harmony、RimJobWorld** 使用与游戏版本匹配的版本，加载在 SSC 之前。
- **契约仪式及其阶段进阶路线需要 Ideology（文化）DLC。**
- **Royalty（皇权）为可选 DLC**；欲望之种可补齐调教研究缺少的蓝图，但不会直接完成研究。
- 受训目标必须设置为 SSC 的**性奴身份**；原版奴隶身份不能替代。

更新时退出游戏，完整替换旧模组文件夹；保留安装包内两套版本目录及加载配置。

宠物兔选择入口尚未开放，旧 RimTalk 兼容暂停。本模组包含奴役、强制关系与身体改造等成人题材。
