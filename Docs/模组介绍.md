## English

**SexSlaveCraft (SSC)** is an adult RimJobWorld expansion for long-term pawn development. Build a colony around Masters, trainers, and specialized slaves, develop pawns through Training and rituals, and use Personality Gel to continue a trained personality’s progress in another eligible body.

**Current release is SSC 2.5.3 for RimWorld 1.7.** The same package includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.3](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.3)

### Training and everyday management

The **Training** tab opens a movable, six-page management window. View identity, stage progress, and cooldowns, assign multiple trainers to a trainee, and manage schedules, poses, behavior permissions, romance preferences, and personal abilities. Trainers can take work automatically or receive manual orders. Each trainee has one active daily Training job at a time.

A **Seed of Desire** grants eligible pawns permanent Master qualification, after which they can select the Master role. Masters gain experience through daily Training and Binding Rituals. Qualified Training Officers can help train other pawns, and eligible slaves can also use self-training.

### Corruption, stages, and Submission Breakdown

Daily Training raises Corruption, develops body parts, and contributes to eligible specialization progress. Corruption can reach 100% at any slave stage. Bound and unbound slaves share four progression stages, which provide minimum Corruption levels and affect the pawn’s attributes, mood, and applicable abilities.

At very high Corruption, eligible pawns can enter **Submission Breakdown**. A request letter lets the player approve a single Training job and its available outcome. Depending on the pawn’s state and the trainer’s qualification, successful Training can establish an initial bound or unbound stage, create a bond, or advance existing progression. Breakdown is controlled in mod settings and defaults to enabled for new settings.

### Binding and release

**Unbound slaves** retain their stage and progress while having their own stage effects, automatic behavior rules, and sensitivity. An eligible Master can bind an unbound pawn through approved breakdown Training or a **Binding Ritual**, preserving the pawn’s exact progress. A qualified Training Officer can develop an unbound pawn through the applicable Training and ritual outcomes.

Bound pawns gain access to features that use their actual Master relationship, including shared beds, directed commands, and damage sharing. Manual release or confirmed Master death preserves slave progression in an unbound state. A two-day release effect lowers work speed and mood and delays rebinding.

### Nine specialization paths

Specializations give pawns different colony roles. Each path has its own research and eligibility requirements, and completed training can lead to a final form through Personality Gel.

| Path | Main focus |
| --- | --- |
| Public Use | Social and trading benefits |
| Cow | Lactation and PNA production |
| Training Officer | Training other eligible pawns |
| Combatant | Combat bonuses and Combat Overdrive |
| Broodmother | Fertility, pregnancy, and offspring settings |
| Pet Cat | Movement and hunting stealth |
| Pet Dog | Animal handling and plant work |
| Pet Rabbit | Cleaning, cooking, and Careful Cooking |
| Pet Fox | Research, tending, and Emergency Bandaging |

### Personality Gel and body development

**Personality Gel** preserves personality-linked development, including specialization progress, Master qualification, and slave progression. Extract an eligible personality, process the gel at a sculpting table to finalize completed specialization training, then implant it into an eligible **Hollow**. Remaining release-effect time pauses during gel storage and processing and resumes after implantation.

The **personality imprint** system has an initial form and two finalized forms: **Lord of Lust** and **Servant of Lust**. Seeds and qualifying development determine how an imprint forms. Finalized imprints lock the corresponding SSC identity, provide their own effects, and travel with the personality through gel transfer.

Physical development stays with the body. SSC provides body-part training, stomach and uterine development, fertility and lactation controls, and sensitivity settings for eligible pawns. Semi- and Full Gelatinization offer further surgical development. Fully gelatinized pawns can have their gel color and transparency adjusted.

### PNA, equipment, and culture

Develop lactation to produce **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** and refine it into concentrated PNA. Use it for Chain activation, weapons, and cosmetic reshaping. Eligible bound pawns can automatically maintain a chosen PNA stage on maps and in caravans.

Equipment includes melee and ranged PNA weapons, mist mortar shells and IEDs, and area emitters. **Depravity Gel** is another resource that eligible bodies can produce. Consumption gives temporary mood benefits and additional Corruption gains from qualifying events, with a work-speed cost.

With **Ideology**, **Master-slave Order** provides precepts and feedback for SSC identities, relationships, and colony roles. **Preaching Corruption** lets eligible slaves influence other participants in the same scene. Binding Rituals develop or establish bonds, while the **Reshaping Ritual** can improve Beauty. [2.5.3 release notes](Releases/2.5.3/2.5.3发布说明.md#english)

### Requirements and getting started

Load **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. Binding Rituals, SSC ideology, and Reshaping Rituals require **Ideology**. **Royalty** is optional and adds a techprint requirement for Training research. In-game text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Russian.

Research **Training**, select the intended pawn’s SSC identity in the management window, and assign an eligible trainer with Training work enabled. When upgrading, back up saves, replace the whole old mod folder, and keep both version folders and **LoadFolders.xml**. ## 中文

**SexSlaveCraft（SSC）** 是以长期角色培养为核心的 RimJobWorld 成人向扩展。你可以围绕主人、调教员和不同特化的性奴组织殖民地，通过调教与仪式培养角色，再用人格凝胶将已培养人格的成果延续到另一具符合条件的身体。

**当前正式版为面向 RimWorld 1.7 的 SSC 2.5.3。** 同一安装包为 RimWorld 1.6 提供 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.3](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.3)

### 日常调教与角色管理

点击「**调教**」打开可拖动的六页管理窗口，查看身份、阶段进度和冷却，为同一受训者指派多名调教员，并管理排程、姿势、行为许可、浪漫偏好和个人能力。调教员可以自动接单，也可接受手动命令，每名受训者同一时间只进行一项日常调教。

「**欲望之种**」可为符合条件的角色授予永久主人资格，取得资格后可选择主人身份。主人通过日常调教与绑定仪式积累自己的成长经验，取得资格的训导官也能协助培养其他角色。符合条件的性奴还可进行自我调教。

### 恶堕、阶段与奴性爆发

日常调教提高恶堕率、培养身体部位，并推进符合条件的特化。任何阶段的恶堕率均可达到 100%。有主与无主性奴共用四阶性奴化进度，阶段提供恶堕下限，并影响角色属性、心情及相应能力。

高恶堕角色可能进入「**奴性爆发**」。玩家可通过请求信批准本场调教及可用的结算分支。根据角色原状态和调教员资格，成功调教可用于首次形成有主或无主阶段、建立绑定，或推进已有进度。该功能可在模组设置中控制，新建设置默认开启。

### 主从绑定与解绑

**无主性奴**保留自己的阶段与进度，具有对应的阶段属性、自动行为规则和敏感度。符合资格的主人可通过获批的爆发调教或**绑定仪式**，按角色原始进度建立绑定。取得资格的训导官可通过相应调教与仪式分支继续培养无主角色。

建立绑定后，角色可使用依赖实际主从关系的同床、点名命令和伤害分担等功能。主动解绑或确认主人死亡后，角色保留进度转为无主。两天的解绑后效会降低工作速度和心情，并暂时限制再次绑定。

### 九种特化路线

特化为角色提供不同的殖民地用途。各路线有自己的研究和资格要求，培养完成后可通过人格凝胶加工获得终极形态。

| 路线 | 主要方向 |
| --- | --- |
| 公交车 | 社交与贸易收益 |
| 奶牛 | 泌乳和 PNA 生产 |
| 训导官 | 调教其他符合条件的角色 |
| 战斗员 | 战斗加成与战斗超频 |
| 孕母 | 生育、妊娠与后代预设 |
| 宠物猫 | 移动与狩猎隐蔽 |
| 宠物狗 | 动物工作与种植 |
| 宠物兔 | 清洁、烹饪及精心料理 |
| 宠物狐 | 科研、护理及应急包扎 |

### 人格凝胶与身体开发

**人格凝胶**保存归属人格的培养成果，包括特化进度、主人资格和性奴化进度。可将符合条件的人格提取成凝胶，在雕刻台加工已完成培养的特化，再植入符合条件的**空壳**。解绑后效的剩余时间在凝胶存放和加工期间暂停，植入后继续计时。

**人格印记**包含初始态，以及「**淫欲之主**」「**淫欲之奴**」两种定型形态。欲望之种与符合条件的成长过程决定印记的形成和分化。定型印记会锁定对应的 SSC 身份，提供相应效果，并随人格通过凝胶迁移。

身体开发保留在原身体。SSC 提供部位培养、胃部和子宫开发，以及符合条件角色的生育、泌乳和敏感度设置。手术还提供半凝胶化与全身凝胶化路线，完成全身凝胶化后可调整颜色和透明度。

### PNA、装备与文化

通过泌乳培养生产**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**，并加工为浓缩液，用于锁链活化、武器和医美。符合条件的绑定角色可在地图或旅队中自动维持指定的 PNA 阶段。

装备包括 PNA 近战和远程武器、雾气迫击炮弹、IED 及范围发烟设备。符合条件的身体还可生产**堕落凝胶**，摄取后暂时提高心情，并在合格事件中额外增加恶堕，代价是工作速度下降。

安装 **Ideology（文化）**后，「**主奴秩序**」提供围绕 SSC 身份、关系和殖民地分工的戒律与反馈。「**淫堕传道**」可使符合条件的性奴影响同场参与者。绑定仪式用于建立主从关系或推进相应成长，**塑貌仪式**则可提升美貌。[2.5.3 版本说明](Releases/2.5.3/2.5.3发布说明.md#中文)

### 依赖与入门

按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 的顺序加载。绑定仪式、SSC 文化和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）**。**Royalty（皇权）**可选，安装后调教研究需要蓝图。游戏内文本提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语。

完成「**调教**」研究，在管理窗口设置目标角色的 SSC 身份，再指派符合条件且已启用调教工作的调教员。升级时先备份存档，再完整替换旧模组文件夹，保留两套版本目录和 **LoadFolders.xml**。[快速入门](../读我，玩法介绍.md) · [完整机制](../机制详解.md)
