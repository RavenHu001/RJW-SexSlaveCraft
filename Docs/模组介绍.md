## English

**Train your pawns. Give them a role. Preserve the personality you have developed.**

SexSlaveCraft (SSC) is an adult **RimJobWorld expansion** built around long-term pawn development, Master–slave bonds and personality transfer. Turn trained pawns into specialists, customize their bodies and personal rules, and connect their growth to your colony's production, combat and culture.

**Version 2.4.10.** The package supports **RimWorld 1.7 and 1.6**, with SSC 2.4.1 content on 1.6. The features below describe 1.7. Text is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Russian.

**New in 2.4.10** are Pet Rabbit housekeeping with **Careful Cooking**, Pet Fox research and medicine with **Emergency Bandaging**, and Cat/Dog balance changes. The four pet paths share the finalization limit. [2.4.10 release notes](Releases/2.4.10/2.4.10发布说明.md#english)

### What's new in 2.4.9

- **Depravity Gel:** eligible SSC slaves can enable production after completing the required body development; production defaults to Off. Production costs work efficiency and speeds up natural Corruption decay. The gel grants a one-day mood boost and extra Corruption from the recipient's own orgasms, within stage and scene limits. Masters are immune. This consumable is separate from **Personality Gel**, which carries personalities.
- **Three tiers of room emitters:** choose a fuel-burning medieval censer, an industrial electric atomizer or a spacer emitter. Each applies RJW's humpshroom effect within the same room and radius. The censer needs no power; the industrial model uses power and humpshrooms; the spacer model uses power without running fuel. Electric models offer a switch and adjustable radius. All three have dedicated artwork.
- **Personal PNA maintenance:** choose Off or one of five activation targets. Eligible bound pawns replenish ordinary PNA before their next ordinary map job without interrupting current tasks. Caravans can use personal or shared supplies, including pack animals; vanilla drug policies run first, and maintenance pauses during night rest. The feature defaults to Off.

The Broodmother's **Conception** ability also gains its own bud icon. See the [2.4.9 release notes](Releases/2.4.9/2.4.9发布说明.md#english) for full details.

### Build a bond and develop nine specializations

Assign trainers, issue manual Training orders or allow eligible self-training. Develop Corruption and body parts, then advance **Chain stages through Binding Rituals** to unlock more options. Masters grow too: the **Seed of Desire** grants permanent Master qualification, and personal participation in Training and Rituals develops **Symbiosis of Desire**.

| Specialization | What it offers |
| --- | --- |
| **Public Use** | Trade and social benefits, plus additional trader interactions. |
| **Cow** | Increased milk production and storage, with physical tradeoffs. |
| **Training Officer** | Qualified pawns train others on their Master's behalf. |
| **Pet Cat** | Movement speed, hunting stealth and the final **Gentle Comfort** support ability. |
| **Pet Dog** | Animal handling, plant work speed and the final **Animal Training** ability. |
| **Pet Rabbit** | Cleaning, cooking and the final **Careful Cooking** ability. |
| **Pet Fox** | Research, tending and the final **Emergency Bandaging** ability. |
| **Combatant** | Combat and survival bonuses, with final **Combat Overdrive**. |
| **Broodmother** | Fertility, pregnancy, childcare and Beauty benefits, offspring presets and final **Conception**. |

Each direction keeps its own progress. **Complete the relevant development, process its Personality Gel and implant it to obtain the final form.** Training and bond panels bring progress, relationships and assignments together; trainer assignment can also enable Training work in one action.

### Customize the pawn and preserve the personality

Set **activity permissions, romance behavior and partner categories per pawn**. Chain stages unlock more controls and one-time relationship orders. Contextual moods and logs reflect relationship events; separate reset buttons restore behavior or romance preferences while preserving relationships and memories.

Research and surgery unlock **uterine and stomach development**, fertility control, permanent lactation and other body modifications. Choose personal sensitivity settings, manage individual abilities, or customize full gelatinization with **color, opacity and rendering controls**. Broodmother progression unlocks preconception choices for offspring sex, species source and initial imprint; final Conception establishes pregnancy in the caster after a completed scene with an eligible adult partner.

**Personality Gel lets personality-based growth, imprints and preferences continue in another eligible body.** Uterine/stomach development, sensitivity and other body-specific settings stay with the body. Current Masters cannot undergo personality extraction.

### Bring progression into colony life

Completed genital Training enables **Purple Aphrodisiac Nanofluid (PNA)** production. Pause or resume production per pawn without losing batch progress, then research concentration and processing. PNA provides activation benefits to eligible Chain-bound bodies and supplies **nine weapons across medieval, industrial and spacer technology**.

With Ideology, **Master-slave Order** adds 28 precepts and cultural feedback. **Cosmetic surgery and Reshaping Rituals** use PNA and medicine to improve vanilla Beauty, up to **+2**.

### Getting started

1. Load matching **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC**. Binding Rituals, culture and Reshaping Rituals require **Ideology**; **Royalty is optional**.
2. Research **Training**, configure a trainer and give the target SSC's **Sex Slave identity**. Vanilla slave status alone does not qualify.
3. Set permissions and assignments, begin Training, then work toward binding and specialization.

When updating, back up saves, close the game and **replace the whole old mod folder**, retaining both version folders and `LoadFolders.xml`. Saves referencing the old 1.7 launcher definitions removed in 2.4.3 have no migration path.

Supported UAP manual group requests are integrated. Legacy RimTalk integration is suspended. Replicas and self-cloning remain deferred. Traditional Chinese, English and Russian romance text is included, with in-game display awaiting separate confirmation.

**培养角色、赋予分工，让已经成长的人格延续下去。**

SexSlaveCraft（SSC）是一款围绕长期角色培养、主从绑定与人格转移展开的 **RimJobWorld 成人向扩展**。将受训角色培养成不同领域的专长角色，调整身体与个人规则，让成长成果参与殖民地的生产、战斗和文化生活。

**版本 2.4.10。** 安装包支持 **RimWorld 1.7 与 1.6**，其中 1.6 使用 SSC 2.4.1 内容。下文介绍 1.7 玩法，提供英语、简中、繁中和俄语文本。

**2.4.10 新增**宠物兔家政与「精心料理」、宠物狐科研医疗与「应急包扎」，并调整猫狗常驻效果。猫、狗、兔、狐的终极成果互斥。[2.4.10 版本说明](Releases/2.4.10/2.4.10发布说明.md#中文)

### 2.4.9 有哪些新内容

- **堕落凝胶：** 符合资格的 SSC 性奴完成对应身体开发后，可主动开启默认关闭的生产功能，以工作效率和更快的恶堕自然衰减为代价获得新消耗品。服用后提供持续一天的心情增益，并在受药者本人高潮时增加额外恶堕，受阶段与场次上限限制；主人免疫。它与承载人格的**人格凝胶**是两种物品。
- **三档房间发烟设备：** 中世纪香薰炉、工业电热雾化器和太空发烟器，在同房间且半径内累积 RJW 驼峰菇药效。香薰炉不耗电，工业型号耗电、耗菇，太空型号只耗运行电力；两款电力设备可直接开关、调整半径。三档均配有专用贴图。
- **个人 PNA 阶段维持：** 选择关闭或五档活化目标，让合格的绑定角色在下一份普通工作前补充原液，不打断当前任务。旅队可使用自有或共享库存，包含驮畜携带的原液；原版服药政策先行，夜休暂停。本功能默认关闭。

孕母的**受孕**技能也获得了专用花苞图标。详细变化见 [2.4.9 发布说明](Releases/2.4.9/2.4.9发布说明.md#中文)。

指派调教员、下达手动调教命令，或允许符合条件的自我调教。积累恶堕、完成部位开发，再通过**契约仪式推进锁链阶段**，逐步开放更多选项。主人同样可以成长：**欲望之种**提供永久主人资格，亲自参与调教和仪式则能推进**欲望共生**。

| 特化 | 主要用途 |
| --- | --- |
| **公交车** | 贸易、社交收益与额外交易互动。 |
| **奶牛** | 提高产奶和储奶能力，同时承担身体机能方面的代价。 |
| **训导官** | 符合资格后，代主人培养其他角色。 |
| **宠物猫** | 移速、狩猎隐蔽与终极能力**贴心安抚**。 |
| **宠物狗** | 动物工作、种植速度与终极能力**驯兽**。 |
| **宠物兔** | 清洁、烹饪与终极能力**精心料理**。 |
| **宠物狐** | 科研、包扎与终极能力**应急包扎**。 |
| **战斗员** | 战斗、生存增益，以及终极能力**战斗超频**。 |
| **孕母** | 生育、孕期、育儿与魅力收益，后代预设及终极技能**受孕**。 |

各方向分别保留培养进度。**完成对应培养，再加工人格凝胶并植入，即可获得终极形态。** 调教页与绑定概览集中展示进度、关系和指派；指定调教员时，还可一并启用调教工作。

### 个人管理、身体改造与人格延续

为每名角色分别设置**行为许可、浪漫行为与对象类别**。锁链阶段逐步开放更多控制和一次性关系命令；关系事件配有相应心情与日志。行为、浪漫各有恢复默认按钮，重置偏好时保留关系和记忆。

研究与手术开启**子宫开发、胃部开发**、生育控制、永久泌乳及其他身体改造。可设置个人敏感度、管理独立能力，也可调整全身凝胶化的**颜色、不透明度和渲染**。孕母培养逐步开放后代性别、物种来源与初始印记的孕前预设；终极技能受孕在与合格成年对象完成同场任务后，建立施法者自身的妊娠。

**人格凝胶让属于人格的成长、印记与偏好，在另一具合格身体中延续。** 子宫／胃部开发、敏感度等身体专属项目留在原身体上，当前主人不能进行人格排泄。

### 让培养成果参与殖民地生活

生殖器调教完成后，可以生产**紫色纳米催情液（PNA）**。每名角色都能独立暂停、恢复生产并保留批次进度，后续研究开放浓缩与加工。PNA 为合格锁链身体提供活化收益，也用于覆盖**中世纪、工业与太空科技的九把武器**。

安装 Ideology 后，**主奴秩序**提供 28 条戒律与文化反馈。**医美手术和塑貌仪式**消耗 PNA 与药品，逐档改善原版美貌，最高 **+2**。

### 如何开始

1. 使用与游戏匹配的版本，按 **Harmony → RimJobWorld → SSC** 加载。契约仪式、文化和塑貌仪式需要 **Ideology（文化）DLC**；**Royalty（皇权）可选**。
2. 研究**调教**、配置调教员，并为目标设置 SSC 的**性奴身份**；原版奴隶身份不能替代。
3. 设置许可与指派，开始培养，再逐步推进绑定和特化。

更新时备份存档、退出游戏，并**完整替换旧模组文件夹**，保留两套版本目录及 `LoadFolders.xml`。引用 2.4.3 已从 1.7 移除的旧发射器定义的存档，不提供迁移支持。

已对接受支持的 UAP 手动多人入口。旧 RimTalk 对接暂停，复制体与自克隆暂缓开发。繁中、英语和俄语浪漫文本已提供，实机显示仍待独立确认。
