## 中文

### 同床许可与睡后心情

- 将 SSC 的额外许可限定在床位相关检查，不再影响全局恋爱判断。SSC 性奴可同时与绑定主人及有效指定调教员同床；普通原版奴隶不会仅凭奴隶身份获得这项许可，原版恋人/配偶同床照常。
- 恶堕必须严格高于 0.1%。额外许可限普通多人床；医疗和死眠优先，普通休息依次尝试可用的已分配床、主人床、调教员床，环境、容量、可达与预留继续使用原版检查。
- 原版奴隶身份的性奴不能直接分配空殖民者床，应先分配主人或有效指定调教员，再分配达标性奴。殖民者身份按原版分配规则处理，不强制清除已有床位。
- 同床心情改为双方实际同时睡着后，在结束躺卧时结算的一天记忆，不叠加；仅有床位分配或清醒躺卧不触发。保留六档数值 `-6、-3、+2、+5、+9、+13`。
- 主人及调教员使用不同记忆文案；两者都实际同睡时优先主人记忆，先起床不会丢失记录。修复起床结算红字及 DLL/心情定义不匹配时的空引用问题。

### 床位标签与 Dubs Mint Menus

- 原版与 Mint 共用主人（金色）、性奴（紫粉色）、调教员（青蓝色）三个独立标签，兼任身份分别显示。标签位于姓名右侧，不改变姓名起点，也避开分配按钮。
- 空床显示全部实际身份；分配人员后仅标记已分配者及其直接 SSC 床伴，多人床取直接关系并集，不递归扩散。无标签不隐藏角色行或额外拒绝原版合法分配。
- 独立状态标记区分有效许可、空床等待、已有床位保留与当前无额外许可。悬停按字段列出主人、指定调教员、相关性奴的当前恶堕及达标状态；长姓名与原版拒绝原因仍可完整查看。

### 调教员身份与绑定保护

- 总栏统一显示“是调教员”：主人固定开启、未选择固定关闭、性奴可选且默认关闭。指定菜单、直接指派、自动/强制普通调教和启动前检查统一使用此身份；接受调教开关与工作优先级仍独立。
- 停用调教员后保留原指派并明确标注，不误当成未指定而开放他人接替。旧档仅为原本已被指定的 SSC 性奴一次性保留资格；未选择身份不自动升级为主人。更新后请检查原有未选择调教员，按意愿设为主人或开启性奴调教员身份。
- 已有锁链的角色，以及缰绳仍有有效绑定对象的主人，禁止切换 SSC 身份，避免隐式解绑、丢失锁链和成长进度；明确解绑后按剩余关系重新判断。性奴调教员个人开关仍可操作。本修复不自动恢复过去已丢失的锁链。
- 修复点击指定调教员菜单时的集合修改异常及后续滚动视图鼠标栈红字。
- 仪式拒绝提示明确说明“指定调教员与所选主人不匹配”，避免把指定关系误称为主人绑定。

## English

### Shared-bed permission and sleep memories

- Restricts SSC’s extra permission to bed checks without altering global romance checks. SSC Sex Slaves may share with both their bonded Master and active Assigned Trainer. Ordinary vanilla slaves receive no exception solely from slave status; native lover/spouse sharing remains intact.
- Requires current Corruption strictly above 0.1% and an ordinary multi-person bed. Medical rest and deathrest take priority. Ordinary rest tries the usable assigned bed, then the Master’s bed, then the trainer’s bed, preserving native environment, capacity, reachability and reservation checks.
- An SSC Sex Slave with vanilla slave status cannot be assigned directly to an empty colonist bed: assign the Master or active trainer first. Colonist Sex Slaves follow native assignment rules; existing bed ownership is not forcibly removed.
- Mood now comes from a one-day, non-stacking memory after actual simultaneous sleep, settled when laying down ends. Assignment alone or lying awake is insufficient. The six values remain `-6, -3, +2, +5, +9, +13`.
- Master and trainer memories use separate wording. If both actually shared the sleep, the Master memory takes priority, even when the Master wakes first. Fixes wake-up settlement errors and null-reference failures caused by missing or mismatched thought definitions.

### Bed badges and Dubs Mint Menus

- Vanilla and Mint show separate Master (gold), Sex Slave (pink-purple) and Trainer (cyan-blue) badges to the right of the name, preserving its starting position and button space.
- Empty beds show all actual roles. Occupied beds show assigned pawns and their direct SSC partners, taking the union for multiple owners without recursive expansion. Missing badges do not hide rows or reject otherwise legal native assignments.
- A separate marker distinguishes qualifying partners, waiting on an empty bed, retained assignments and no extra permission. Hover text lists the Master, Assigned Trainer and relevant slaves’ Corruption and threshold results. Full names and native rejection reasons remain available.

### Trainer roles and bond protection

- The trainer toggle is always on for Masters, always off for Unset pawns, and optional for Sex Slaves, defaulting to off. Trainer selection, direct assignment, automatic/forced ordinary Training and pre-start checks use the same identity. Receiving Training and work priorities remain separate.
- Disabling a trainer preserves the assignment and marks it inactive. Existing saves retain trainer eligibility once for SSC Sex Slaves already assigned as trainers; Unset pawns are not automatically promoted. Review old Unset trainers after updating and choose their identity explicitly.
- Pawns with a Chain, and Masters whose Bridle still has valid bound targets, cannot switch SSC identity. This prevents implicit unbinding and loss of Chain progress. Explicit unbinding restores switching when no blocking relationship remains; the Sex Slave trainer toggle stays available. Previously lost Chains are not reconstructed.
- Fixes trainer-menu collection-modification errors and the resulting scroll-view mouse-stack error.
- Ritual rejection text identifies a mismatch between the Assigned Trainer and selected Master instead of incorrectly claiming an existing bond.

### Translation contributions from Baphomet

- Incorporates three language files containing 38 translations from **developer Baphomet's maintenance version based on 2.2.15** into the current 2.3.0 version. Thanks to Baphomet for these localization contributions.
- Adds 8 Traditional Chinese entries covering the labels and descriptions of four dynamic training mood stages, plus 15 Traditional Chinese and 15 English entries covering the labels, descriptions and work messages of five final personality-shaping recipes. These recipes now have English translations instead of falling back to Simplified Chinese.
- This import changes language text only. The 2.3.0 gameplay logic, DLL and version number remain unchanged; unfinished Pet Cat / Pet Rabbit choices remain disabled.

### Compatibility and scope

- New UI text is synchronized in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian. Mint remains optional.
- Supports RimWorld 1.6. DLL file version: 2.3.0.0; product version: 2.3.0; assembly reference identity remains 1.0.0.0.
- Fully restart the game after updating the DLL and Defs. Legacy RimTalk remains suspended; unfinished Pet Cat / Pet Rabbit choices remain disabled. The new individual restriction system, hard bed-sharing restrictions, rotations and companion lists are not included.
