日期 / Date：2026-09-15。

## 中文

本版基于上游 2.2.8 接续维护，适用于 RimWorld 1.6，包含 2.2.13 及更早版本的修复。

### 永久泌乳计时修复

- 修复按批次结算时漏算此前经过的时间，导致基础产奶明显偏慢的问题。现在累计实际 tick 后统一计算奶量与营养消耗，产奶速度不再随组件调用间隔变化。
- 默认配置下，食物充足、从空开始且无额外产量或挤奶时，约 6 个游戏小时充满，额外消耗约 0.075 营养。
- 接近满容量时只按实际存入量扣营养；满容量或关闭泌乳时不积攒可供后续补发的生产时间。
- 保存未结算的短时进度。旧存档保留已有奶量，不追补过去漏算的产量；HumanCattle 接管的生产流程不受本修复影响。

### 兼容与后续开发

旧 RimTalk 兼容已暂停并归档，调教排班和旧设置数据保留；本版继续保持这一状态。

以下三个方向写入 [未来内容开发规划](未来内容开发规划.md)，尚未实现：

1. 重置整个 RimTalk 兼容模块。
2. 拆分针对性奴主动自慰与主动和他人性爱的保护。
3. 主奴同床优化调整和可视化。

现有自慰拦截及同床兼容暂不视为 bug。已回滚的同床调整不计入本版修复。

## English

This continuation builds on upstream 2.2.8 for RimWorld 1.6 and includes fixes from 2.2.13 and earlier versions.

### Permanent lactation timing fix

- Fixes batch processing that discarded elapsed time and made base milk production much slower than configured. Milk production and nutrition costs now use accumulated ticks, independent of component update intervals.
- With sufficient nutrition and no extra production or milking, an empty reservoir fills in about six in-game hours under default settings, consuming about 0.075 additional nutrition.
- Near capacity, nutrition costs cover only the milk actually stored. Time does not accumulate while full or disabled for later production.
- Progress below one batch is saved. Existing saves retain their milk without backfilling past losses; HumanCattle-controlled production is unaffected.

### Compatibility and future development

Legacy RimTalk integration remains suspended and archived. Training schedules and legacy preferences are retained.

Three directions remain [future plans (Chinese)](未来内容开发规划.md): a complete RimTalk integration reset; separate protections for slave-initiated masturbation and sex with others; and improvements and visualization for shared beds between Masters and slaves.

Current masturbation blocking and shared-bed compatibility are not classified as bugs for now. The reverted shared-bed change is not included as a fix.
