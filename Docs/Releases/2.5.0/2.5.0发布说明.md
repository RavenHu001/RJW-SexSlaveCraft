# SSC 2.5.0 · Training Management / 调教管理

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文) · [开发归并](2.5.0开发归并核对.md) · [验证记录](2.5.0版本验证.md)

## English

**SSC 2.5.0 for RimWorld 1.7.** The package includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.0](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.0)

- **Training Management window.** Open the original Training tab with one pawn selected. The movable window follows single selection on the map or colonist bar. Its six pages cover identity and progress, relationships, daily Training, specializations, body and appearance, and behavior rules. The overview shows stage milestones and the Training cooldown. Final specializations appear as cards with tooltips.
- **Multiple trainers per trainee.** In Relationships, drag the handle next to a candidate portrait to add them. Drag a roster member back to remove that assignment. Locate individual trainers or enable their Training work from the same page. The bond overview provides access to the roster. Existing single-trainer assignments migrate on load.
- **Training and related activities.** Trainers take work according to their own work priorities. Each trainee has one active daily Training slot, shared by automatic jobs and manual orders. In-progress assignments recover on load. Binding Rituals, shared beds, self-training and Public Use specialization read the roster according to their existing rules.
- **Interface and maintenance.** Updated the interface text in four languages, retired the old 1.7 Training tab layout, and streamlined affected regression tests.

Both feature areas received scoped in-game acceptance on RimWorld 1.7.5005 rev1082. The version verification record documents the formal build and release checks. [Validation record](2.5.0版本验证.md)

## 中文

**SSC 2.5.0 面向 RimWorld 1.7。** 同一安装包为 RimWorld 1.6 提供 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.0](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.0)

- **调教管理窗口。** 单选角色后点击原「调教」标签，打开可拖动的独立窗口，随地图或殖民者栏的单选切换。六页分别管理概况、关系、日常调教、特化、身体与外观、行为规则。概况显示成长阶段刻度和调教冷却，终极成果以带悬浮说明的卡片展示。
- **多调教员指派。** 在「关系」页拖动候选头像旁的把手即可追加，反向拖动可撤销该人。可逐人启用调教工作和定位，命令栏关系概览提供名单入口。旧档单人指派会在读档时迁移。
- **接单与联动。** 调教员按各自的工作优先级接单。同一受训者的自动任务与点名命令共用一个日常调教占用，读档可恢复在途任务。绑定仪式、同床、自我调教和公交车特化按各自规则查询名单。
- **界面与维护。** 更新四语界面文本，清理 1.7 旧调教页布局，精简相关回归测试。

两项功能均在 RimWorld 1.7.5005 rev1082 完成各自范围的实机验收。正式构建和完整门禁见版本验证。[验证记录](2.5.0版本验证.md)
