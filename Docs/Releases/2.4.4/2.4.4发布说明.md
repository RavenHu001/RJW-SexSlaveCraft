# SSC 2.4.4 — 文化拓展、仪式演化点与医美应用

## English

**2.4.4 development scope, registered 2026-10-23; not released.** This version groups development after `v2.4.3` through `f3e47c9`. The public download remains [v2.4.3](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.3), which does not contain these additions. All new features target **RimWorld 1.7**; **1.6 and Common remain frozen at SSC 2.4.1**.

### Ideology and ritual progression

- Adds the **Master-slave Order** meme and six precept groups: attitudes toward sex slaves, Training Officers and Masters; Master and bound-pawn counts; and six gender rules. There are **28 precepts**, with mood, opinion, environment, leadership and count-based feedback. Cultural bond checks use actual Chains rather than the Training tab's identity selection; precepts do not grant qualification or create bonds.
- Adds seven dedicated cultural icons, plus Binding and Reshaping Ritual icons. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian text includes ideology names, leader and moral-guide titles, ritual name presets and generated backgrounds with dynamic deity/founder names. Existing saved names are retained. A title mentioning a chief slave does not add a separate specialist role.
- Adds a global **ritual development points** setting, **off by default**, including old configurations. When enabled, SSC Binding Rituals follow the vanilla development conditions, reform cap and outcome curve: the four quality tiers give **0/0/1/2 points** before the cap. Disabling the setting keeps existing points and other ritual rewards; no historical rewards are backfilled.

### Cosmetic reshaping

- Successful surgery or a Reshaping Ritual improves vanilla **Beauty by one step**, from -2 → -1 → no Beauty trait → +1 → +2. The cap is fixed at +2. Existing higher modded levels remain untouched; conflicting traits, gene-sourced/suppressed or ambiguous Beauty states and ongoing recovery block treatment.
- **Surgery:** research PNA Cosmetic Application (1,500 points; Basic PNA Application + Sterile Materials), Medical 8, **10 normal PNA + 3 industrial/glitterworld medicine**, work 4,000. Eligible adult player-managed humanoids can include prisoners. Vanilla anesthesia and surgery quality apply.
- **Ritual:** requires Master-slave Order and a distinct practitioner and recipient of that ideology; practitioner Medical 6, **20 normal PNA + 2 herbal medicine**, no research prerequisite. Eligible recipients are adult colonists or colony slaves; prisoners use surgery. Spectators are optional and may follow other ideologies. Preparation brings materials before the 5,000-tick main stage. At Medical 6, baseline success is approximately 61.5%, modified by vanilla ritual quality.
- Both routes cause a shared recovery reaction, stronger after failure; health details show the remaining time. Treatment is blocked until recovery. The ritual includes paired facing, purple mist and four new name presets for newly created instances.

### Fixes and documentation

Fixes Personality Excretion aborting when the fixed interaction has no RJW anatomy match. It reuses the existing empty-contact path while retaining participant checks, work permissions and extraction settlement. The maintainer confirmed that the originally reported female pair can complete the work.

Completed culture/cosmetic plans and implementation records are separated from future work. Animal modifications, SSC scenarios/factions, composite social labels, chief roles and broad refactoring/performance plans are **not implemented by this version**. Existing 2.4.3 weapons, low-tech progression and dose changes retain their original introduction version.

### Upgrade and verification status

Replace the old mod folder completely when updating; retain the version folders and loading configuration. The 2.4.3 removal of the old 1.7 Basic PNA Launcher and related definitions still applies, with **no save migration for references to them**. Frozen 1.6 retains its original content.

This is a documentation/version registration, not a new build or release. The current 1.7 DLL, BUILD-INFO, About and source product attributes still say **2.4.3**. Batch tests and maintainer feedback are preserved at their actual dates; they do not constitute a fresh 2.4.4 release gate. See [verification](2.4.4版本验证.md) before packaging.

## 中文

**2.4.4 开发范围，登记日期：2026-10-23；尚未发布。** `v2.4.3` 之后截至 `f3e47c9` 的开发统一归入本版。公开下载仍为 [v2.4.3](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.3)，不包含以下新增内容。新功能仅进入 **RimWorld 1.7**；**1.6／Common 继续冻结于 SSC 2.4.1**。

### 文化与仪式发展

- 新增**主奴秩序**模因和六组、共 **28 条戒律**：性奴态度、训导官、主人、主人数量、性奴数量及六档性别规范。提供本人心情、社交、环境、领袖与数量累进反馈。文化按实际锁链判断绑定，不以调教页身份预选替代；戒律本身不授予资格或建立绑定。
- 七张文化专属图标，以及契约／塑貌仪式专属图标；简中、繁中、英语、俄语正式文案、文化名称、领袖／道德导师称谓、仪式名称预设及动态神名／创始者姓名背景。已有存档名称保留；候选称谓包含“首席”不表示已实装独立首席职位。
- 新增全局**仪式演化点**开关，**默认关闭**，旧配置缺键同样关闭。开启后 SSC 契约仪式按原版发展条件、改革上限和随时举办曲线结算：糟糕／平庸／成功／完美为 **0／0／1／2 点**，实际增量受上限约束。关闭保留已有点数及其他仪式收益，不追补历史奖励。

### 纳米催情液医美应用

- 手术或塑貌仪式成功后，原版 **Beauty 每次提升一档**：-2 → -1 → 无美貌特质 → +1 → +2，固定封顶 +2。已有模组更高档保留；冲突特质、基因来源／抑制、重复或未知档，以及恢复期均拒绝施作。
- **工业手术：** 研究“纳米催情液医美应用”1500 点，前置 PNA 简易应用和无菌材料；医术 8，**普通原液 10＋工业／闪耀药 3**，工作量 4000。面向合格成年玩家管理人形肉身，包含囚犯；沿用原版麻醉与医疗质量。
- **塑貌仪式：** 限定主奴秩序，主持者与受塑者须为同一文化的不同角色；主持医术 6，**普通原液 20＋草药 2**，无研究前置。成年殖民者／殖民地奴隶可受塑，囚犯仅走手术；观众可为零、可跨文化。准备阶段先取料，正式阶段 5000 tick；医术 6 时基础成功率约 61.5%，仍受原版仪式质量修正。
- 两条途径共用恢复反应，失败更重，健康条目显示剩余时间，恢复前不能重复施作。仪式采用相邻固定朝向、紫色迷雾，并为新建实例提供四个名称预设。

修复人格排泄在固定动作无法解析 RJW 部位时到位即中断：复用既有空接触流程，保留双方校验、工作准入和提取结算。维护者已确认原报告的女性组合可以正常完成。

文化／医美的已完成设计、实施记录与未来需求已分开归档。动物改造、SSC 特殊开局与势力、复合社交称谓、首席职位及整体重构／性能规划**不作为本版已实现内容**。2.4.3 武器、低科技及恩赐剂量保留原引入版本。
