# SSC 2.4.10 · Pet Rabbit and Pet Fox / 宠物兔与宠物狐

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文) · [开发归并核对](2.4.10开发归并核对.md) · [版本验证](2.4.10版本验证.md)

## English

**Development milestone, registered 2026-11-09.** The 2.4.10 scope covers changes after `v2.4.9` through `5640afba`. New gameplay targets **RimWorld 1.7**. The public package is [v2.4.9](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.9), with SSC 2.4.1 on RimWorld 1.6.

- **Pet Rabbit housekeeping.** Eligible SSC pawns can train through cleaning, cooking and shared Training. Cleaning and cooking speed improve at 20%, 50% and after Personality Gel finalization. The final ability, **Careful Cooking**, gives four game hours of faster cooking and protects eligible meals finished during the effect from preparation-related food poisoning. It can be used during the current cooking job and has a one-day cooldown. [Acceptance and icon](../../Development/Completed/兔特化家政第二批实机验收.md)
- **Pet Fox research and medicine.** A medieval research project unlocks the path. Research at a bench and tending another eligible patient build progress. The passive improves research speed, tending speed and tending quality. Final Fox gains **Emergency Bandaging**, which approaches an allied humanlike patient on the same map and tends all currently eligible wounds in one cast without medicine. Downed patients are eligible. A successful cast starts a one-day cooldown. [Acceptance and icon](../../Development/Completed/狐狸特化第二批实机验收.md)
- **Four pet paths share finalization rules.** Ordinary directions retain their own progress. The Cat, Dog, Rabbit and Fox final forms are mutually exclusive and can remain active when the pawn pursues a direction outside the pet group. Personality Gel carries the training and finalization result.
- **Cat and Dog balance.** Cat gains movement speed and hunting stealth. Dog retains taming and training bonuses and gains plant work speed. Research, hediffs, abilities, finalization recipes and menus received simpler, consistent animal specialization wording across four languages.
- **Maintenance.** Filled missing English work-tab and Binding Ritual text, revised player feedback and Master/slave terminology, declared that SSC loads before `abscon.privacy.please` and `eseeker.fo`, and updated the three emitter textures. Documentation includes the rewritten 2.4.9 overview, archive and roadmap work.

The Rabbit and Fox batches received scoped in-game acceptance. The 2.4.10 release gate and package are pending. [Validation record](2.4.10版本验证.md)

## 中文

**2.4.10 开发里程碑，登记于 2026-11-09。** 纳入 `v2.4.9` 之后至 `5640afba` 的开发。新增玩法面向 **RimWorld 1.7**，公开安装包为 [v2.4.9](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.9)，其中 1.6 使用 SSC 2.4.1。

- **宠物兔家政重做。** 通过清洁、烹饪及公共受训培养，20%、50% 和凝胶终极化后提高相应速度。终极技能 **精心料理** 可在烹饪途中自施，持续 4 游戏小时，进一步提高烹饪速度并使期间出炉的合格餐食免于制作染毒，冷却 1 游戏日。[验收与专用图标](../../Development/Completed/兔特化家政第二批实机验收.md)
- **宠物狐科研医疗。** 新增中世纪研究。研究台科研及合格的对他人护理提供进度，分阶段提高科研速度、包扎速度与品质。终极技能 **应急包扎** 走近同图己方人形伤员后，无需药品即可一次处理全部当前可包扎伤口，支持倒地伤员，成功施放后冷却 1 游戏日。[验收与专用图标](../../Development/Completed/狐狸特化第二批实机验收.md)
- **四种宠物共享终极化规则。** 猫、狗、兔、狐的普通培养各自保留进度。终极成果组内互斥，切到组外方向仍可保有终极被动与技能。培养进度与终极成果可通过人格凝胶流转。
- **猫狗平衡与四语文案。** 猫的常驻效果调整为移速和狩猎隐蔽，狗保留动物驯服及训练加成并新增种植速度。统一猫、狗、兔、狐的研究、健康状态、技能、终极化配方及菜单用词。
- **维护。** 补齐工作栏和绑定仪式英文，修订玩家反馈及主从称谓，声明 SSC 先于隐私和家庭模组加载，更新三档发烟器贴图。文档包括 2.4.9 简介改写、发布归档、索引整理和规划核对。

兔与狐各批次均已获得对应的实机验收。2.4.10 完整发布门禁和正式安装包待处理。[版本验证](2.4.10版本验证.md)
