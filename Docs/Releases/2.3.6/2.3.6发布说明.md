## 中文

### 特化成长

- SSC 性奴正常完成日常调教，或整场绑定仪式成功结束后，当前正在培养的特化获得一次基础经验。中断、重复结算、普通培养完成及已有本方向终极成果时不发放；其他历史方向不会同时增长。
- 所有当前方向沿用战斗员动态公式：`min(评分 / 2000, 0.04)`。实际绑定主人施教时乘以 1.5，单次最多 6 个百分点；其他合格施教者单次最多 4 个百分点。整场仪式使用 `仪式品质 × 75` 作为特化评分；部位经验仍使用自身的独立公式。
- 训导官原固定受训奖励、公交车旧日常恶堕增量奖励和战斗员原日常专用入口由公共基础经验替换。训导官施教、战斗员直接击杀、狗的动物工作、公交车交易等特色来源保留，并按原有条件分别结算。
- 猫、兔的培养选择入口仍禁用；已有旧档当前培养记录按现有资格接收基础经验。自我调教不发放基础特化经验。本版未加入猫狗终极技能、宠物互斥或亲昵扩展。

### 安装与验证

- 支持 RimWorld **1.6**，继续要求 Harmony、RJW 及既有加载顺序。程序集引用身份保持 `1.0.0.0`，文件／产品版本为 `2.3.6.0`／`2.3.6`。
- 完整自动回归为 **20 套、968/968 项通过**；维护者已确认统一经验在游戏内有效。构建、包校验和验证边界见[版本验证](2.3.6版本验证.md)。
- 退出游戏，将旧模组文件夹移出 `RimWorld/Mods/`，再将安装包内的新文件夹放入，避免遗留旧文件。无需重开存档；特化进度仍按已有角色记录保存。

## English

### Specialization progress

- An SSC Sex Slave's current specialization gains one base progress reward after completed daily Training or a successful full Binding Ritual. Interrupted or duplicate outcomes, ordinary completion and prior finalization grant no reward; historical specializations do not progress at the same time.
- All current paths use the Combatant formula, `min(score / 2000, 0.04)`. Training by the pawn's actual bound master multiplies this by 1.5, for a maximum of 6 percentage points; other qualified trainers have a 4-point maximum. Full Rituals use `ritual quality × 75` as the specialization score. Body-part experience retains its separate formula.
- The former fixed Training Officer receiver reward, Public Use daily Corruption-based increment and Combatant-only daily entry are replaced by the shared base reward. Training Officer teaching, Combatant direct kills, Dog animal work, Public Use trade and other distinct sources remain subject to their existing conditions.
- Pet Cat and Pet Rabbit selection remain disabled. Existing saves with a current training record can receive base progress under the existing eligibility checks. Self-training grants no base specialization progress. This release does not add Cat/Dog final abilities, pet exclusivity or affection changes.

### Installation and validation

- Supports RimWorld **1.6** with the existing Harmony, RJW and load-order requirements. Assembly identity stays `1.0.0.0`; file/product versions are `2.3.6.0` / `2.3.6`.
- All **20 automated suites passed, 968/968 checks**, and the maintainer confirmed the shared progress works in game. See the [validation record](2.3.6版本验证.md) for build, package and test scope.
- Close the game, move the old mod folder out of `RimWorld/Mods/`, and install the replacement folder from the ZIP to avoid leftover files. Existing saves retain their specialization progress.
