版本归并日期：**2026-10-10**（维护者当地时间）。本版**尚未打包、尚未打标签、尚未发布 GitHub Release**；安装 ZIP 生成后在此补充入口。构建、自动回归与验收边界见[2.3.9 版本验证](2.3.9版本验证.md)。

本版收录已发布 `v2.3.8`（`fd60efa`）之后至 `7564173` 的全部 **12 项开发提交**：统一受训身份门槛与旧档恢复、训导官职责心情与主人评价、训导官独立弱化记忆套、弱值取整口径修正、训导官文案基调重写与去性别化，以及训导官对实际主人的反向信任。2.3.8 发布结果归档（`cd97b5c` 的前半部分）与两次 PR 合并不重复计为开发增量。历史 2.3.8 安装包不包含本版任何改动。

## 中文

### 统一受训身份门槛

- 日常自动调教、手动强制调教、绑定仪式，以及新增的调教员指派，**统一要求目标在本模组中的身份为 SSC 性奴**。原版奴隶身份、关闭行为限制开关和强制命令都不能替代或豁免这一条。
- 门槛作用在四个环节：候选筛选、实际选入（提交）、任务执行和结算。任一环节查出身份不符都会拒绝或中止，同时保留各入口原有的身体、执行者、许可、研究、冷却、排班和通行条件。
- **旧档不被改写。** 读档不会自动给人加上或剥掉性奴身份，也不会删除既有成长；只是不合资格的旧任务会停止。如果一个小人没有设身份、但保留着有效的历史锁链，可以在面板上明确确认“恢复性奴身份”，保留原主人、配置与成长。迟到返回的外部回调既不能发放收益，也不能清理新任务已经占用的准备状态。

### 训导官反馈总览

- **职责心情**：有效任职的训导官正常完成一次日常调教或一整场绑定仪式后，获得“履行训导职责”**+3 心情**，持续一天。不同受训对象共用一份记忆，重复完成只刷新时长，不叠加。普通培养已到 100% 还没终极化、以及有效终极训导官跨方向调教，都不因为经验增量为零而漏发。取消、失败、仪式六个阶段单独结束、自我调教，以及自己作为受训者时，都不发放。
- **主人评价**：实际主人对自己**绑定且有效任职**的训导官有 **+5“受委任的训导官”** 评价。关闭任职、解绑、退阶或终极效果被禁用后消失；临时倒地、停工或旅行不取消。
- **反向信任**：同一任职条件还让训导官对**这位实际主人**获得 **+5 好感**与 **+3 常驻心情**（“受到主人信任”）。方向与主人评价相反，两者共同构成双向任职反馈。
- 三条都是**条件性想法，不是记忆**：没有时长和容量、不写进记忆列表、不进人格凝胶、不产生可累积的实例。关闭任职、切向、退阶、解绑或终极禁用后立即消失，条件恢复后重新生效，反复开关不会累积。社交显示沿用原版约 100 tick 的查询缓存，所以界面上的变化可能有极短延迟。

### 训导官使用独立的弱化记忆套

- 训导官作为执行者时，发给受训者的记忆改用**独立弱化套**，系数 **K=0.5**：心情档 **-5／+1／+4／+8**，社交档 **-10／+3／+8**，时长沿用原套。档位仍由**未弱化**的评分或等级决定，弱化只改强度。
- **主人的原套完全没有改动**：Def、文案、数值、实例类与启动修正都保持原样。两套家族独立分组、独立计时、互不覆盖；**单次结算只发一套**，不会同时拿到强弱两组。
- 执行者是主人或其他原本合法的执行者时，仍发原套。结算时已经失去训导官资格的执行者，本次不发受训者记忆，也不会退回领取强套。
- **绑定仪式沿用原套，且不发社交记忆。** 首版的 +3 职责心情与 +5 主人评价不乘 K。

### 弱值取整口径修正

- 弱值按**四舍五入到整数**计算，基准非零时保底绝对值 1。实现使用显式的**半值远离零**取整，因此基准 +5 得 **+3**、+9 得 **+5**、-5 得 **-3**。
- 修正前曾使用运行时默认的“取偶”舍入，会出现 +5 得 +2、+9 得 +4、-5 得 -2 的偏差。现在结果不再依赖宿主运行时的舍入实现，回退（好感）与舍入使用同一口径，全部半值边界已被回归逐项钉住。

### 训导官文案重写与去性别化

- 训导官相关的心情、评价、弱化记忆与同床文案**整体重写**，与主人原套同一基调：露骨但不描写具体动作，主线是“**受训者是主人的所有物，训导官只是主人授权的支配工具**”；动作词统一为“调教”，“训导官”作为角色名保留。
- **全文去除性别指向。** 中文不再使用“他／她”，英文不再使用整词 he／him／his／she／her，俄文改用现在时、无人称句和关系从句。本模组含性别转换机制，受训者、训导官与主人的性别都不固定，因此不再假定任何一方。
- 顺带修正三处既有问题：简体中文原先**缺少** `Thought_SharedBedWithTrainer.xml`（游戏内会回退到 Def 源文并用旧称），已补齐；繁体中文与俄文的角色名与模组其他文件不一致，已统一。
- **如果你从 2.3.8 升级**：`SSC_TrainerOfficer_DutyFulfilled`、`SSC_TrainerOfficer_Appraisal` 与 `SSC_SharedBedWithTrainer` 这三条文案在 2.3.8 里已经出现过，本版把它们重写了，方向跨度较大。旧档里的记忆条目文字看上去不同，属于本版刻意修改，不是翻译错误。

### 准入边界与继承内容

- 身份检查统一在**本模组**的身份之上，不改变 RJW 自身的性行为资格判定；两者都需要通过。
- 2.3.8 的绑定仪式观众助兴修复与选人提示（金鞭子主持候选、紫项圈性奴候选、悬停与拒绝原因），以及 2.3.7 猫狗特化与宠物互动、2.3.6 统一特化基础经验、2.3.5 自我调教和更早内容，全部继续包含在本版。
- 此前仍待验收或规划的项目不会因为本版发布转为已完成：成年猫狗 20% 亲昵自愿后续仍待实机验收；兔选择入口仍禁用；旧 RimTalk 兼容继续暂停。

## English

### Unified receiver identity

- Automatic Training, forced manual Training, Binding Rituals and the new trainer assignment all **require the receiver to hold Sex Slave identity in this mod**. Vanilla slave status, the disabled-restrictions switch and forced commands neither substitute for nor waive it.
- The check applies at four points: candidacy, actual selection (submission), execution and outcome. A mismatch refuses or stops the attempt at any of them, while each entry point keeps its existing body, executor, permission, research, cooldown, schedule and reachability conditions.
- **Existing saves are not rewritten.** Loading never adds or removes Sex Slave identity and never deletes progress; only ineligible old tasks stop. A pawn with no identity but a valid historical Chain can explicitly confirm restoring the role from the panel, keeping the original Master, settings and growth. Late external callbacks can neither grant rewards nor clear preparation already claimed by a newer task.

### Trainer Officer feedback at a glance

- **Duty mood:** an actively appointed Officer gains a **+3 mood** duty thought for one day after completing daily Training or a whole Binding Ritual. Different receivers share one memory; further completions refresh its duration instead of stacking. Ordinary completion at 100% before finalization, and an active final Officer training another direction, still qualify even when the experience gain is zero. Cancellation, failure, an individual ritual stage, self-training, and being the receiver grant nothing.
- **Owner appraisal:** the actual Master holds a **+5** conditional appraisal of an Officer who is both bound and actively appointed. Disabling appointment, unbinding, regressing a stage or suspending the final effect removes it; temporary downing, work suspension or travel does not.
- **Reverse trust:** the same conditions also give the Officer **+5 opinion** and a **+3 steady mood** toward that actual Master. The two directions together form a two-way appointment feedback.
- All three are **conditional thoughts, not memories**: no duration, no capacity, nothing written into memory lists, no personality-gel capture, no accumulating instances. They disappear immediately when conditions fail and return when conditions do, without accumulating across repeated toggling. Social display follows vanilla's roughly 100-tick query cache, so the interface may lag briefly.

### The Officer's separate weakened memory set

- When an Officer is the executor, receiver memories use a **separate weakened set** with **K=0.5**: mood stages **-5/+1/+4/+8**, social stages **-10/+3/+8**, keeping the original durations. Stage selection still uses the **unweakened** score or level; only strength is reduced.
- **The owner's set is completely untouched**: defs, text, values, instance class and startup adjustment all keep their existing form. The two families group and time independently and never overwrite each other, and **one settlement grants exactly one set**.
- The actual Master and other originally eligible executors still grant the original set. An executor who has lost Officer eligibility by settlement grants no receiver memory that time, and does not fall back to the stronger set.
- **Binding Rituals keep the original set and grant no social memory.** The first-version +3 duty mood and +5 owner appraisal are not scaled by K.

### Weak-value rounding

- Weak values round to whole numbers, with a minimum non-zero magnitude of 1. The implementation rounds **half away from zero**, so base +5 becomes **+3**, +9 becomes **+5** and -5 becomes **-3**.
- An earlier build used the host runtime's default banker's rounding, producing +2 for +5, +4 for +9 and -2 for -5. Results no longer depend on the host runtime's rounding implementation; the opinion fallback uses the same rule, and every half-boundary case is pinned by regression.

### Rewritten, gender-neutral Officer text

- Officer mood, appraisal, weakened memories and shared-bed text was **rewritten as a whole** to match the owner's register: explicit without describing specific acts, framed as the **receiver being the Master's property and the Officer merely the instrument the Master authorizes**. The action verb is consistently “train”, and “trainer” remains the role name.
- **All gendered wording was removed.** Chinese no longer uses 他/她, English no longer uses the whole words he/him/his/she/her, and Russian uses present, impersonal or relative-clause forms. Because this mod includes gender-change mechanics, no fixed gender is assumed for the receiver, the Officer or the Master.
- Three pre-existing issues were fixed along the way: Simplified Chinese was **missing** `Thought_SharedBedWithTrainer.xml` (the game fell back to the Def source text with an older role name) and now has it; Traditional Chinese and Russian role names were made consistent with the rest of the mod.
- **Upgrading from 2.3.8:** `SSC_TrainerOfficer_DutyFulfilled`, `SSC_TrainerOfficer_Appraisal` and `SSC_SharedBedWithTrainer` already shipped in 2.3.8, and this version rewrites them substantially. Different wording on a memory entry from an older save is intentional rather than a translation error.

### Admission boundaries and retained features

- Identity checking sits on top of **this mod's** identity and does not change RJW's own sex-eligibility rules; both must pass.
- The 2.3.8 Binding Ritual spectator fixes and selection hints (gold whip host candidates, purple collar Sex Slave candidates, hover and refusal reasons), plus 2.3.7 Cat/Dog specializations and pet interactions, 2.3.6 shared specialization progress, 2.3.5 self-training and earlier content, all remain included.
- Pending acceptance and planned items do not become complete through this consolidation: the adult Cat/Dog 20% consensual affection follow-up still awaits in-game acceptance, Rabbit selection remains disabled, and legacy RimTalk remains suspended.

## 安装 / Installation

- 支持 RimWorld 1.6，按既有顺序加载 Harmony、RJW 和本模组。退出游戏后，将 ZIP 内唯一的模组文件夹解压到 `RimWorld/Mods/`；更新时先移出旧模组文件夹，避免残留文件。
- Use RimWorld 1.6 with Harmony and RJW loaded before this mod. Close the game, move the previous mod folder out of `RimWorld/Mods/`, then extract the enclosed mod folder there.
- 校验附件为相邻 `.zip.sha256`；发布包内 `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` 记录源码提交、DLL 重编译状态、当次完整回归结果和有效载荷哈希。The adjacent `.zip.sha256` verifies the ZIP; its manifest records source commit, rebuilt-DLL status, current regression results and payload hashes.
