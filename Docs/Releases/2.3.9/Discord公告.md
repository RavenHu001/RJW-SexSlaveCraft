**SexSlaveCraft 2.3.9 已发布｜统一受训身份与训导官反馈**

- **统一受训身份**：日常自动／手动调教、绑定仪式和调教员指派，统一要求目标属于 SSC“性奴”；原版奴隶身份、关闭行为限制和强制命令都不能替代。读档不改写已有身份与成长，保留有效历史锁链的角色可在面板确认恢复。
- **训导官职责反馈**：有效任职的训导官完成一次日常调教或整场仪式，获得 +3 心情、持续一天的“履行训导职责”；实际主人对这名训导官有 +5“受委任的训导官”评价。
- **反向信任**：同一任职条件让训导官对实际主人获得 +5 好感与 +3 常驻心情（“受到主人信任”）。这几项都是条件性想法而不是记忆：关任职、解绑、退阶或终极禁用后立即消失，恢复后重新生效，反复开关不累积。
- **训导官弱化记忆套**：训导官亲手调教时，受训者拿到的是独立弱化套（K=0.5，心情 −5／+1／+4／+8、社交 −10／+3／+8）；主人原套完全没动，两套各自计时、互不覆盖，单次结算只发一套。
- **弱值取整修正**：弱值四舍五入到整数、非零保底 1，半值远离零，因此 +5 得 +3、+9 得 +5、−5 得 −3。
- **文案重写**：训导官相关的心情、评价、弱化记忆与同床文案整体重写，与主人原套同一基调，并全文去除性别指向；顺带补齐简体中文原先缺失的同床条目。

提醒：`SSC_TrainerOfficer_DutyFulfilled`、`SSC_TrainerOfficer_Appraisal` 与 `SSC_SharedBedWithTrainer` 这三条文案在 2.3.8 已经出现过，本版把它们重写了；旧档里文字看起来不一样属于本版刻意修改，不是翻译错误。

本版保留 2.3.8 的仪式观众助兴修复与选人图标、2.3.7 猫狗特化、2.3.6 统一特化经验、2.3.5 自我调教。兔入口仍禁用，旧 RimTalk 兼容继续暂停。

**SexSlaveCraft 2.3.9 is out — Unified receiver identity and Trainer Officer feedback**

- **Unified receiver identity:** automatic and forced Training, Binding Rituals and trainer assignments now all require SSC Sex Slave identity. Vanilla slave status, disabled restrictions and forced commands cannot replace it. Loads never rewrite saved identity, and a pawn with a valid historical Chain can confirm the role back on the panel.
- **Trainer Officer duty feedback:** an actively appointed Trainer Officer gains a one-day +3 duty mood for completing daily Training or a whole Ritual, and their actual Master holds a +5 appraisal of that Officer.
- **Reverse trust:** the same condition gives the Trainer Officer +5 opinion and a +3 steady mood toward that actual Master. These are conditional thoughts, not memories: they vanish as soon as the appointment, bond or qualification fails.
- **Trainer weakened memory set:** when a Trainer Officer does the Training, the receiver gets a separate weakened set (K=0.5, mood −5/+1/+4/+8, social −10/+3/+8). The owner's set is untouched, the two sets time independently, and one settlement grants only one.
- **Weak-value rounding fix:** weak values round to whole numbers with a minimum non-zero magnitude of 1, half away from zero — so +5 becomes +3, +9 becomes +5, −5 becomes −3.
- **Rewritten Trainer text:** Trainer mood, appraisal, weakened-memory and shared-bed text was rewritten to match the owner's register and made gender-neutral, and Simplified Chinese gained its missing shared-bed entry.

Note: three strings already shipped in 2.3.8 (`SSC_TrainerOfficer_DutyFulfilled`, `SSC_TrainerOfficer_Appraisal`, `SSC_SharedBedWithTrainer`) are rewritten here — different wording from an older install is intentional.

Retains 2.3.8 fixes and icons, 2.3.7 Cat/Dog and earlier features. Rabbit stays disabled. Back up your save, close the game, then replace the old folder.
