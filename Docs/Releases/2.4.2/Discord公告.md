**SexSlaveCraft 2.4.2｜RimWorld 1.7 与凝胶外观**

- **双版本支持**：自动选择1.6／1.7目录；1.6保留2.4.1冻结内容，新功能在1.7生效。
- **凝胶渲染升级**：柔化黑边，统一身体、头部与SizedApparel的光照表现，保留原版眼部及FA五官、眨眼与遮挡，并优化材质缓存。
- **个体外观**：完全凝胶化后，从“调教”ITab开启个人渲染、调整颜色与不透明度；默认深紫／85%，支持恢复默认。旧全局设置自动迁入个人开关，关闭后保留配色。
- **界面与兼容**：重排调色弹窗，修复身份／任职控件禁用显示，移除命令栏重复调色按钮；同时修复1.7凝胶过程结算与线程问题，安装包精简开发资料。

**SexSlaveCraft 2.4.2 — RimWorld 1.7 and gel appearance**

- **Versioned layout:** automatically selects 1.6 or 1.7. The 1.6 runtime stays frozen at 2.4.1; new features target 1.7.
- **Improved gel rendering:** softer outlines, consistent body/head/SizedApparel lighting and cached materials, preserving vanilla eyes and FA facial animation, blinking and occlusion.
- **Per-pawn appearance:** fully gelatinized pawns use the Training tab for rendering, color and opacity. Defaults remain dark purple / 85%, with reset support. Legacy global preferences migrate to individual switches; disabling rendering keeps saved colors.
- **UI and compatibility:** clearer color controls, proper disabled identity controls, no duplicate color gizmo, 1.7 process/thread fixes and a leaner package.
