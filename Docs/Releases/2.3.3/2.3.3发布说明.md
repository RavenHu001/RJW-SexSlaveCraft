## 中文

### RJW 泄欲对象指派

- SSC 性奴的锁链达到第 2 阶段后，可在 RJW 面板中按 RJW 原有的设置、权限和身体资格被指派为泄欲对象。见习性奴不会仅凭 SSC 身份获得该资格。
- 保留 RJW 原有的受虐狂、囚犯和奴隶资格路径；本次未将 SSC 个体限制接入泄欲对象指派判定。
- 维护者已在本机游戏中确认该补丁有效；其他模组组合尚未实测。

### 特化科技说明

- 奶牛、公交车、训导官及宠物狗科技介绍现在写明实际功能、培养条件和经验增长来源。公交车的性行为、调教与交易成长，奶牛的产奶成长，训导官的受训、施教与主动双人行为，以及宠物狗的动物处理与实际互动均可直接在研究界面查看。
- 猫、兔和战斗员说明明确区分预设内容与当前可用功能：猫、兔选择入口尚未完成，战斗员尚未实装；三者目前没有正常可用的特化经验来源。宠物亲昵主人不会提供特化经验。
- 人格编辑科技名称移除过时的“目前只有公交车可用”；简中、繁中、英文和俄文说明同步更新。本项只调整界面文字，不改变特化玩法数值。

### 范围与安装

`v2.3.2` 之后还有 `2ffdeb0`，仅修改未来开发规划，不作为本版已实现功能。2.3.2 的训导官升级提示、旧档开关迁移及其他内容继续见[上一版说明](../2.3.2/2.3.2发布说明.md)。RimWorld 支持版本仍为 1.6；Harmony 和 RimJobWorld（RJW）仍为必需依赖。

退出游戏后，将旧模组文件夹移出 `RimWorld/Mods/`，再把新 ZIP 中的 `RJW-SexSlaveCraft-TieJin-Modify` 文件夹解压进去，避免残留已删除的文件。

## English

### RJW Comfort designation

- An SSC sex slave at Chain stage 2 or higher can be designated as a Comfort pawn in the RJW panel, subject to RJW's existing settings, permissions, and physical checks. SSC trainees gain no eligibility from identity alone.
- RJW's existing Masochist, prisoner, and slave eligibility paths remain. SSC individual restrictions are not connected to Comfort designation by this change.
- The maintainer confirmed the patch in game on their machine. Other mod combinations have not been tested.

### Specialization research descriptions

- Cow, Public Use, Training Officer, and Pet Dog research now describes implemented effects, requirements, and experience sources directly in the research interface.
- Cat and Rabbit selection remains unfinished; Combatant is not implemented. Their descriptions identify the unavailable experience paths. Showing affection to a master does not grant pet specialization experience.
- The obsolete “Public Use only” label is removed from Personality Editing. Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Russian descriptions are updated. These text changes do not alter specialization gameplay values.

### Scope and installation

The intervening `2ffdeb0` commit only updates a future development plan; it is not counted as an implemented feature. See the [2.3.2 notes](../2.3.2/2.3.2发布说明.md) for Training Officer upgrade and save migration details. RimWorld 1.6 remains supported; Harmony and RimJobWorld remain required.

Close the game, remove the old mod folder from `RimWorld/Mods/`, then extract the `RJW-SexSlaveCraft-TieJin-Modify` folder from the new ZIP to avoid stale files.
