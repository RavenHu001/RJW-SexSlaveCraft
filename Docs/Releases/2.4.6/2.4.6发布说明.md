## English

2.4.6 groups all work after the **2.4.5 registration commit `7225f729`** through **`385cdd8e`**: 52 non-merge commits and 363 changed paths. There is no v2.4.5 release tag; 2.4.5 remains an earlier unreleased milestone. Its uterine/fertility and stomach development, movement fix and Master/pet maintenance are retained, without being counted again as new features.

New runtime work targets **RimWorld 1.7**. **1.6/Common stay frozen at SSC 2.4.1.** The latest public package remains **v2.4.4** and does not include the 2.4.5 or 2.4.6 additions.

### New and improved

- **Personal ability controls:** Bestow Bliss, damage sharing, milk production, fertility and personal gel rendering share registration, queries and UI submission checks. Existing save owners, fields, defaults and gameplay rules are preserved. The framework's three implementation batches have maintainer acceptance; it does not provide general dependency, migration or bulk-template features.
- **Romance controls:** Separate behavior and partner preferences for courtship, proposals and relationship endings; partner categories are the actual Master, SSC sex slaves and other pawns. Chain stage 2 unlocks initiative controls, stage 3 response controls, and stage 4 one-time named commands. Three response guarantees default off. Ordinary necessary interaction conditions still apply; interrupting a command ends that request.
- **Mood and relationship feedback:** Fourteen continuous branches and 108 event branches, with 26 long-duration variants reusing text. Event memories use the saved partner plus five groups: received courtship, commanded courtship, received proposal, commanded proposal and relationship ending. A new real event updates its group; valid zero outcomes settle it without creating a zero-value memory. Relationship changes clear relevant history, with precise handling of matching vanilla mood effects. This adds the controlled pawn's mood, not a new multi-perspective opinion system.
- **Logs and task results:** Three log categories with thirteen event entries, participant-perspective history, vanilla letters, behavior-specific task reports and completion results share confirmed interaction facts. Clicking a setting alone does not award an event memory.
- **Personal reset buttons and clearer UI:** Separate reset actions for behavior restrictions and romance preferences, target multi-selection, narrow-panel layout and actual localized Chain-stage names. Personality transfer retains romance preferences/history; a new clone receives default control preferences. Resetting preferences does not erase relationships or memories.
- **Four-language copy:** Each language now has 270 romance texts: thought names/descriptions, thirteen log entries, six task reports and five results. Simplified Chinese v1 has in-game acceptance; Traditional Chinese, English and Russian have review approval and targeted text checks, with deployment/in-game display still pending. Technical carrier fallback text is separate.
- **PNA production guidance:** Two item and two research descriptions explain existing production, nutrition, progress/output location and concentration recipes. This adds guidance, not a production buff, new facility or rabbit-clone route. Final wording has been deployed; in-game display confirmation is pending.
- **Fixes and presentation:** More specific first-binding refusal reasons, refreshed Combat Overdrive art, lifecycle and command safeguards, and isolation of text-only test mode from the full regression/release gate.

### Validation and upgrade boundary

Mechanism batches and Simplified Chinese copy have recorded local acceptance. Three-language display, PNA guidance display, balance and unreported mod combinations retain their own open scope. Earlier suite counts are dated evidence, not a newly executed 2.4.6 release gate.

This registration updates **VERSION and documentation only**. About, source product attributes, the checked-in DLL and BUILD-INFO remain at 2.4.4; the DLL is an older development artifact, not the latest source build. A matching rebuild, full release gate and package audit remain necessary before release. No game synchronization or publication is performed here.

Back up saves, close the game and replace the whole mod folder when installing a prepared build. Keep both version folders and LoadFolders.xml. The old 1.7 Basic PNA Launcher definitions removed in 2.4.3 still have **no save migration**. Pet Rabbit and legacy RimTalk remain unavailable/suspended. Seed acquisition/use extensions, PNA facilities/specializations, new social opinions/events and other proposals remain plans.

### 新增与改进

- **个人能力开关：** 欢愉恩赐、伤害分担、泌乳、生育及凝胶个人渲染复用注册、查询和界面提交校验；保留原字段、保存载体、默认值与业务规则。三批约定功能已获维护者实机认可；依赖／互斥、迁移及批量模板不属于已交付范围。
- **浪漫行为控制：** 独立设置求爱、求婚、结束关系的主动许可及求爱／求婚回应；对象区分实际主人、SSC 性奴和其他人。锁链二阶开放主动控制、三阶开放被动回应、四阶开放一次性点名命令；三项强制接受默认关闭。保留互动必要条件，一次命令被打断即结束。
- **综合心情：** 14 个持续分支、108 个事件分支，26 个长期变体复用文案。事件按保存对象和被动求爱／受命求爱／被动求婚／受命求婚／关系结束五组维护；同组新真实事件更新旧记录，合法零值结清，关系变化定向清理，精确处理相应原版心情。当前是受控本人视角，不新增多视角社交评价系统。
- **日志与任务反馈：** 三类十三入口日志、双方本人历史、原版信件、分行为任务和完成结果共用已确认互动事实；仅点击开关不触发事件记忆。
- **个人恢复默认与界面：** 性行为和浪漫分别提供个人恢复默认，补齐目标多选、窄栏布局和锁链具体阶段名。浪漫配置及历史沿人格迁移，新克隆控制偏好回默认；恢复偏好不清空关系或历史。
- **四语文案：** 每语落实 270 项心情名称／描述、十三日志、六任务及五结果。简中 v1 已获本机实机认可；繁中、英语、俄语已审阅认可且通过文案专项，尚未同步和实机确认。底层载体技术回退另保留。
- **PNA 产出引导：** 两物品、两研究描述说明现有分泌、营养、进度／去向与浓缩工艺；没有新增增产效果、设施或兔分身路线。最终术语已同步，待实机显示确认。
- **修复与表现：** 细分首次绑定拒绝原因，重绘战斗超频图标，完善生命周期与命令边界；总回归／发布门禁隔离仅文案测试模式，拒绝将文案通过摘要当成完整机制结果。

### 验证与升级边界

机制分批、简中文案已有相应本机认可；三语显示、PNA 引导显示、数值平衡及未报告模组组合分别保留状态。历史用例计数不相加或冒充本次 2.4.6 正式门禁。

本次只更新 **VERSION 与文档**。About、源码产品属性、仓库 DLL／BUILD-INFO 仍为 2.4.4；仓库 DLL 是较早开发产物，不代表当前源码已构建。正式发布前须对齐元数据、重编、执行统一门禁和安装包审计；本次不进行游戏同步或发布。
