## English

New runtime work targets **RimWorld 1.7**. **1.6/Common remain frozen at SSC 2.4.1.** The2.4.7 download is available from [v2.4.7](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.7); version-aligned build/gate evidence and upload receipts are recorded separately.

### Body sensitivity and clearer controls

- Adds optional per-body sensitivity levels, off by default. Chain stages 1–4 unlock levels with ×2/×4/×8/×16 frequency factors and +0.30/+0.75/+1.50/+3.00 vulnerability offsets. Ongoing mood depends on the effective level and Chain stage.
- Saved selection and effective level are separate: lowering a Chain stage limits the current effect while preserving the selection. Body settings stay with the body during personality transfer.
- Adds the general **Orgasm Frequency Multiplier** stat. Its default is ×1; other valid stat sources can affect RJW pawns without SSC identity. SSC sensitivity contributes one factor. Current RJW, animation and UAP timing paths share actual-progress acceleration and validated fractional carry restoration; unsupported third-party timers need explicit integration. No automatic warm-up, fatigue slowdown or standalone framework is included.
- Moves behavior restrictions directly below identity controls. Sensitivity selection and its label share a row; an indented line shows the effective level, with locked-option reasons in tooltips.

### Personal PNA production

- Adds a personal production switch to the body panel when the existing ordinary PNA producer is present. New and missing-field saves default to enabled. Pausing preserves the current batch progress and stops natural production, the relevant Cow maturation contribution, extra production nutrition cost and full-batch settlement; resuming continues without catch-up.
- Shows current batch accumulation beneath the switch, with distinct disabled and insufficient-nutrition states. The health page uses the same nutrition check; a full batch is not incorrectly labeled as nutrition-starved. Production speed, yields, drug effects and recipes stay unchanged.

### Bond behavior, UAP and fixes

- For eligible adult bound pairs, the slave's voluntary RJW attraction toward their actual Master has a base floor of 1 before native final modifiers. Local willingness checks follow the same direction; the Master's willingness, body conditions, permissions, cooldowns and other prerequisites remain in force. The native attraction explanation is retained with an SSC result appended. This does not guarantee an interaction or force partner selection.
- Fixes manual UAP group startup with SSC restrictions enabled, including plan/result flow, pooled-job ownership and RAF initialization ordering. A separate **manual UAP permission override** defaults to off; when enabled it only applies to that explicit manual request. UAP matching and body requirements remain. Loading a save cancels unfinished group sessions; session continuation is not implemented.
- Keeps an already-started SSC receiver's Training and Binding Ritual role when the receiver becomes downed. Trainer downing, death, cancellation and invalid pairs retain their original rules; already-interrupted old rituals are not reconstructed.
- Combines repeated Master resonance statistics within each query, preserving values and refresh rules. No measured overall TPS/FPS improvement is claimed.

### Documentation and validation

Sensitivity and the new controls/fixes have scoped maintainer in-game acceptance. The earlier PNA production guidance was confirmed effective on 2026-10-27. Traditional Chinese, English and Russian romance display and other independent open checks retain their original scope.

Testing now has Core/Special execution profiles, reduced repeated combinations and a lightweight localization key/parameter check. **50 suites / 6333 cases are registered** (Core 5400, Special 933); the last pre-release maintenance run passed 3241 related cases plus 138 independent romance-text cases. These are separate historical runs, **not a new complete 2.4.7 release gate**. See the validation record for per-batch evidence and remaining release steps.

## 中文

新增运行内容仅面向 **RimWorld 1.7**；**1.6／Common 继续冻结于 SSC 2.4.1**。本版下载入口为[v2.4.7](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.7)；版本对齐、正式门禁和上传回执分别记录。

### 身体敏感度与界面

- 新增默认关闭的个人身体敏感度。锁链一至四阶段依次开放四档，频率系数为 ×2／×4／×8／×16，脆弱度偏移为 +0.30／+0.75／+1.50／+3.00；持续心情由实际档位与锁链阶段共同决定。
- 保存选择与实际档位分开：降阶只限制当前效果，不覆盖原选择；人格流转不携带身体档位。
- 新增通用「高潮频率系数」属性，默认 ×1；非 SSC 身份也可受合法外部属性来源影响，SSC 档位只贡献一次倍率。当前 RJW、动画及 UAP 计时入口统一按实际推进补足倍率，保留小数余数与合法存读档恢复；未接入的第三方计时器不自动支持。自动升温、疲劳减速与独立框架尚未实现。
- 行为限制移至身份区下方；敏感度名称与选择同行，缩进行显示实际档位，锁定原因置于菜单悬浮提示。

### 个人 PNA 生产

- 身体栏为已有普通 PNA 生产载体提供个人启停，旧档缺字段及新载体默认开启。暂停保留当前批次进度，停止自然生产、相关奶牛催熟贡献、额外生产营养扣除和满批结算；恢复后继续，不追补暂停期间的生产。
- 开关下显示当前批次积累，区分关闭与营养不足；健康页共用同一营养判断，满批不再误标缺营养。原产速、产量、药效和配方不变。

### 绑定行为、UAP 与修复

- 对符合成年条件的有效绑定双方，性奴对真实主人的自愿 RJW 基础吸引力在原生最终修正前保底为 1，局部意愿按同一方向接入。主人侧意愿、身体条件、许可、冷却等仍须满足；原生吸引力解释与分项保留，末尾补充 SSC 实际结果。不保证必定执行，也不强制选人。
- 修复 SSC 限制开启时 UAP 手动多人启动，包括计划／结果流转、池化任务归属及 RAF 初始化顺序。另有默认关闭的手动许可豁免，仅作用于本次明确的手动请求，仍保留 UAP 匹配与身体条件。读档取消未完成会话，不提供续播。
- SSC 已开始的受方倒地后保留接收任务及绑定仪式角色，继续原流程。施教者倒地、死亡、取消与失效配对仍按原规则；已中断旧仪式不重建。
- 共振查询合并重复统计，保留数值和刷新规则；不声明整体 TPS／FPS 的量化提升。
