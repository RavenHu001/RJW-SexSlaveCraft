## 中文

### 观众助兴恢复正常结算

- 主持者和仪式性奴不再重复算入观众。准备窗口按分配的观众预估加成，结束时按实际合格出席人数结算。
- 修复观众基本全程观看，却因阶段进度始终为零而无法提供加成的问题。现在以**本场实际经过时间**计算门槛，实际观看至少半场才计入；空场时间计入全场时长，观看过半后离场仍保留贡献。
- 加成沿用线性曲线：**0 人 +0%、1 人 +5%、3 人 +10%、4 人 +12.5%、5 人 +15%、10 人及以上 +20%**。观众须属于本场并在观看区域或分配的观看站位；死亡、倒地、未到场和非人类观众不累计观看时间。
- 结果信件始终显示观众助兴分项，包括零观众，便于核对。沿用原版出席记录与仪式计时的存档格式，已经结束的旧仪式不追补奖励。维护者已复测确认修复有效。

### 鞭子与项圈角色图标

- 在仪式准备窗口的头像左上添加常驻小图标：**金色鞭子为主持候选，紫色项圈为 SSC 性奴候选**。双资格人物横向显示两枚图标，避开姓名及原版文化图标；已选角色保留勾选，当前不可选角色变暗并带斜线。
- 鞭子表示主持及调教职能，不等于 SSC 主人身份或仪式性奴的所有权。符合条件的性奴调教员可以同时拥有两枚图标。
- 图标表示基础候选，仍需检查当前配对、行为许可、身体和可达性条件。悬停头像或拖拽到角色槽位时显示允许／拒绝边框及实际原因，配对提示遵守既有指定调教员、原版角色锁定和容量限制。
- 保留原版点击、右键、拖拽、换人和角色交换流程；落到满槽空白处与替换已有头像分别判断。不能担任执行角色的小人仍可按原有规则观看仪式。提示只作用于 SSC 绑定仪式。
- 四语说明和状态更简明，图例说明鞭子、项圈、斜线与勾选的含义；选人提示中的“目标”统一称为“性奴”。维护者已确认候选身份收紧和图标布局有效；最后一轮文案通过资源校验，未单独进行实机复测。

### 准入边界与继承内容

- 紫色候选图标只显示给 **SSC 身份为性奴**且满足其他基础条件的小人，普通未设置 SSC 身份的小人不再获得该图标。这是视觉候选条件调整，**实际选角、提交和启动没有新增性奴身份硬性门槛**；后台仍沿用既有规则。
- 首次绑定时，主持者仍需 SSC 主人身份及有效指定关系；已有绑定时，实际主人和第三方调教员沿用原有主持与许可规则。图标和显示缓存不替代提交或启动时的最新验证。
- 将受调教资格与相关入口统一到共同门槛已列入未来开发计划，尚未实现，不计入本版功能。
- 2.3.7 的猫狗培养、终极技能及宠物互动，以及更早版本的自我调教与统一特化经验继续包含在本版。此前仍待验收或规划的项目不会因本次仪式发布转为已完成。
- 支持 RimWorld **1.6**，必需依赖仍为 Harmony 与 RJW；绑定仪式使用 Ideology 仪式系统。旧 RimTalk 暂停与兔选择入口禁用状态保留。

## English

### Correct spectator bonus settlement

- The host and ritual Sex Slave are no longer counted again as spectators. The setup window estimates the bonus from assigned spectators; the outcome uses spectators with sufficient actual attendance.
- Fixes spectators watching almost the entire ceremony but receiving no bonus because stage-progress time stayed at zero. The threshold now uses **actual elapsed ritual time**. At least half of the ceremony must be attended; empty time counts toward its duration, while spectators leaving after the threshold retain their contribution.
- The linear curve gives **+0% for 0 spectators, +5% for 1, +10% for 3, +12.5% for 4, +15% for 5, and +20% for 10 or more**. Attendance accrues only for spectators belonging to the ceremony and in its gathering area or assigned viewing position; dead, downed, absent and non-human spectators accrue no viewing time.
- Result letters always include the spectator factor, even at zero. Native attendance records and ritual timers retain their save format. Already completed rituals are not recalculated. The maintainer confirmed the fix works in game.

### Whip and collar role icons

- Persistent portrait icons identify **host candidates with a gold whip and SSC Sex Slave candidates with a purple collar**. Dual icons share one row at the top left, preserving names and native ideology icons. Assigned roles retain a checkmark; unavailable roles are dimmed and crossed by a slash.
- The whip represents hosting/trainer eligibility, not SSC Master identity or ownership of the ritual Sex Slave. Eligible Sex Slave trainers can display both icons.
- Icons indicate basic candidacy. The current pair, permissions, bodies and reachability still require validation. Hover and drag feedback displays eligibility borders and actual refusal reasons, respecting existing trainer assignments, native role locks and capacity limits.
- Native click, right-click, drag, replacement and role-swap flows remain. Dropping on empty space in a full slot is distinguished from replacing its portrait. Pawns unable to perform a ritual role can still attend as spectators under existing rules. These hints apply only to SSC Binding Rituals.
- Four-language instructions, legends and states are shorter, explaining the whip, collar, slash and checkmark. Selection hints call the target a “sex slave.” The maintainer confirmed candidate-identity filtering and the icon layout; the final text revision passed resource checks without a separate in-game text retest.

### Admission boundaries and retained features

- The collar candidate icon is shown only for pawns with **SSC Sex Slave identity** and the other basic conditions. Ordinary pawns without that identity no longer receive it. This changes visual candidacy; **actual role selection, submission and startup do not gain a new hard Sex Slave identity requirement** and retain existing backend rules.
- First-time binding still requires an SSC Master host with the valid assignment. Existing bonds retain current owner/third-party hosting and permission rules. Icons and display caches do not replace fresh submission or startup validation.
- Unifying Training eligibility across related entry points is recorded as future work and is not implemented in this release.
- Cat/Dog training, final abilities and pet interactions from 2.3.7, plus earlier self-training and shared specialization progress, remain included. Their existing pending acceptance and planned items remain pending.
- RimWorld **1.6**, Harmony and RJW requirements remain. Binding Rituals use Ideology's ritual system. Legacy RimTalk remains suspended and Rabbit selection remains disabled.

## 安装 / Installation

- 支持 RimWorld 1.6，按既有顺序加载 Harmony、RJW 和本模组。退出游戏后，将 ZIP 内唯一的模组文件夹解压到 `RimWorld/Mods/`；更新时先移出旧模组文件夹，避免残留文件。
- Use RimWorld 1.6 with Harmony and RJW loaded before this mod. Close the game, move the previous mod folder out of `RimWorld/Mods/`, then extract the enclosed mod folder there.
- 校验附件为相邻 `.zip.sha256`；发布包内 `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` 记录源码提交、DLL 重编译状态、当次完整回归结果和有效载荷哈希。The adjacent `.zip.sha256` verifies the ZIP; its manifest records source commit, rebuilt-DLL status, current regression results and payload hashes.
