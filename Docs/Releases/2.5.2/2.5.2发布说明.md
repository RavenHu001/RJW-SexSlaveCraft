## English

**2.5.2 development milestone for RimWorld 1.7**, registered on 2026-11-19. The public package is SSC 2.5.1, which includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **Corruption and Chain progression.** Corruption can reach 100% at any Chain stage. Chain stages retain their minimum Corruption levels, and a completed Binding Ritual can advance the Chain by at most one stage. Natural decay uses a neutral Chain multiplier at every stage. Corruption display and ongoing mood use the same whole-percent thresholds. Bound slaves receive additional mood penalties according to how far their Corruption exceeds their Chain stage.
- **Identity, imprints, and notifications.** Slave traits require formal SSC slave identity and use historical peak Corruption to determine their tier. Eligible pawns with an initial imprint can become Servants of Lust at full Corruption. Master qualification and finalized imprint acquisitions have notifications. The update also fixes SSC identity restoration for new vanilla duplicates and adds bounded retries for failed full-Corruption differentiation.
- **Unbound slaves and binding.** Unbound slaves share the four progression stages with bound slaves. Their behavior and sensitivity follow their stage automatically. Binding through a ritual or breakdown preserves existing unbound progress. Releasing a bond or losing a Master to death preserves progress and starts a two-day rebinding cooldown, which pauses in personality gel.
- **Submission breakdown.** Enabled by default. Eligible pawns at full Corruption enter it when the shared cooldown and other requirements allow. The state has a risk alert, a six-hour health-tab countdown, training requests and a one-day cooldown. Approved training can establish slave status, bind an unbound pawn or advance existing progress. The solo opening is followed by wandering and invitations, with immediate dispatch upon acceptance and a one-hour per-partner cooldown.
- **Preaching Corruption and Depravity Gel.** The new Ideology issue offers Off, Men, Women, and Everyone settings. Eligible bound and unbound participants can influence other participants in the same non-SSC scene. Preaching Corruption and Depravity Gel share confirmed-event bookkeeping while retaining separate cooldowns and per-scene limits. Their gains are configurable through XML.
- **Text, visuals, and maintenance.** Adds four-language letters, ongoing moods, breakdown text, a culture issue icon, and breakdown visual effects. Regression cleanup removes repeated combinations and expands lightweight localization checks.

The implemented work has scoped automated and in-game results recorded by development batch. Four-language in-game display checks and a deferred save edge remain documented in the validation record. A proposed 5% daily decay default, corruption crystals, and further UI/anatomy work remain in the roadmap. [Validation record](2.5.2版本验证.md) · [Roadmap](../../Design/README.md)

## 中文

**2.5.2 开发版，2026-11-19 登记，面向 RimWorld 1.7。** 当前公开安装包为 SSC 2.5.1，包内 RimWorld 1.6 内容为 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **恶堕率与锁链阶段解耦。** 各阶段的恶堕率均可积累到 100%，锁链继续提供阶段硬下限，完成一场绑定仪式最多升一阶。各阶段自然衰减的锁链倍率统一为 1.0。界面与持续心情共用整数百分比档位，有链性奴按恶堕超出锁链的档数承担额外心情扣分。
- **身份、印记与通知。** 性奴特性按正式 SSC 性奴身份及历史恶堕峰值重算。符合条件且持有初始印记的角色满恶堕后可定型为「淫欲之奴」。主人资格与定型印记取得新增通知信，修正新生成原版复制体的 SSC 身份恢复，并为满恶堕分化失败增加有限重试。
- **无主性奴与绑定。** 有主和无主共用四阶性奴化进度，无主行为和敏感度随阶段自动调整。爆发和仪式可以按原进度绑定无主角色。主动解绑或主人死亡会保留进度并开始两天的再绑定冷却，冷却在凝胶内暂停。
- **奴性爆发。** 默认开启。角色持续满恶堕时，通过共用准入及冷却检查后触发。提供风险警报、健康页六小时倒计时、特批调教请求和一天冷却。获批调教可用于首次成形、无主转有主或继续升阶。单人开场后继续游荡并向合格对象发起求用邀约，接受后立即派发任务，同一对象冷却一小时。
- **淫堕传道与堕落凝胶结算。** 新增文化议题及「不传／传男子／传女子／传众生」四档。符合条件的有主和无主性奴可在非 SSC 场景中影响同场对象。传道与堕落凝胶共用真实事件结算入口，各自独立保存冷却与单场额度，数值由 XML 配置。
- **文案、美术与维护。** 落实通知信、通用恶堕持续心情和奴性爆发的四语文案，新增文化图标及发作特效；精简重复回归组合，扩展轻量本地化校验。
