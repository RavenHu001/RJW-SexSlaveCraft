# SSC 2.5.2 · Corruption and Submission Breakdown / 恶堕成长与奴性爆发

## English

**2.5.2 development milestone for RimWorld 1.7**, registered on 2026-11-19. The public package is SSC 2.5.1, which includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **Corruption and Chain progression.** Corruption can reach 100% at any Chain stage. Chain stages retain their minimum Corruption levels, and a completed Binding Ritual can advance the Chain by at most one stage. Natural decay uses a neutral Chain multiplier at every stage. Corruption display and ongoing mood use the same whole-percent thresholds. Bound slaves receive additional mood penalties according to how far their Corruption exceeds their Chain stage.
- **Identity, imprints, and notifications.** Slave traits require formal SSC slave identity and use historical peak Corruption to determine their tier. Eligible pawns with an initial imprint can become Servants of Lust at full Corruption. Master qualification and finalized imprint acquisitions have notifications. The update also fixes SSC identity restoration for new vanilla duplicates and adds bounded retries for failed full-Corruption differentiation.
- **Submission breakdown.** This optional mental state is disabled by default. Eligible colonists and colony slaves can enter it at very high Corruption. It has a risk alert, a health-tab countdown, requests for player-approved Training, and a one-day cooldown. Bound pawns seek their actual Master, and successful approved Training can advance the existing Chain by at most one stage. The final version uses a single solo opening followed by wandering and invitations to eligible partners, with immediate job dispatch and a one-hour per-partner cooldown.
- **Preaching Corruption and Depravity Gel.** The new Ideology issue offers Off, Men, Women, and Everyone settings. Eligible bound participants can influence other participants in the same non-SSC scene. Preaching Corruption and Depravity Gel share confirmed-event bookkeeping while retaining separate cooldowns and per-scene limits. Their gains are configurable through XML.
- **Text, visuals, and maintenance.** Adds four-language letters, ongoing moods, breakdown text, a culture issue icon, and breakdown visual effects. Regression cleanup removes repeated combinations and expands lightweight localization checks.

The implemented work has scoped automated and in-game results recorded by development batch. Four-language in-game display checks and a deferred save edge remain documented in the validation record. The unbound-slave system, a proposed 5% daily decay default, corruption crystals, and further UI/anatomy work remain in the roadmap. ## 中文

**2.5.2 开发版，2026-11-19 登记，面向 RimWorld 1.7。** 当前公开安装包为 SSC 2.5.1，包内 RimWorld 1.6 内容为 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **恶堕率与锁链阶段解耦。** 各阶段的恶堕率均可积累到 100%，锁链继续提供阶段硬下限，完成一场绑定仪式最多升一阶。各阶段自然衰减的锁链倍率统一为 1.0。界面与持续心情共用整数百分比档位，有链性奴按恶堕超出锁链的档数承担额外心情扣分。
- **身份、印记与通知。** 性奴特性按正式 SSC 性奴身份及历史恶堕峰值重算。符合条件且持有初始印记的角色满恶堕后可定型为「淫欲之奴」。主人资格与定型印记取得新增通知信，修正新生成原版复制体的 SSC 身份恢复，并为满恶堕分化失败增加有限重试。
- **奴性爆发。** 新增默认关闭的独立精神状态，符合条件的高恶堕殖民者和原版殖民地奴隶可进入发作。提供风险警报、健康页倒计时、玩家批准的单场调教请求和一天冷却。有链角色向实际主人求助，特批调教成功后按原锁链进度最多升一阶。最终流程为一次单人开场，随后游荡并向合格对象发起求用邀约，接受后立即派发任务，同一对象冷却一小时。
- **淫堕传道与堕落凝胶结算。** 新增文化议题及「不传／传男子／传女子／传众生」四档。符合条件的持链角色可在非 SSC 场景中影响同场对象。传道与堕落凝胶共用真实事件结算入口，各自独立保存冷却与单场额度，数值由 XML 配置。
- **文案、美术与维护。** 落实通知信、通用恶堕持续心情和奴性爆发的四语文案，新增文化图标及发作特效；精简重复回归组合，扩展轻量本地化校验。

实现按各批候选保留自动验证和实机结果。四语逐语实机显示及暂缓的读档边界见版本验证。第 6 项无主性奴、默认日衰减改为 5%、堕落结晶、后续界面和部位适配继续按规划跟踪。[验证记录](2.5.2版本验证.md) · [当前规划](../../Design/README.md)
