## 中文

### 欲望之种：主人资格的新入口

- 新增具有独立贴图的**欲望之种**。符合使用条件的角色使用后永久取得主人资格，但不会自动成为主人；仍需在 SSC 身份面板中选择。
- **默认仍允许直接手动选择主人。** 关闭模组设置中的手动赋予后，未取得资格的角色不能新选择主人，已有主人身份不受影响。手动选择只改身份，不写入永久资格；已有的绑定身份锁继续生效。
- 资格是个人的人格记录，研究是存档的全局记录，两者分开显示和保存。角色身份栏与人格凝胶信息页会显示各自的“主人资格：已取得／未取得”。
- 有 **Royalty** 时，种子同时补齐基础“调教”研究缺少的原版蓝图；不增加研究进度，也不直接完成研究。原版独立调教蓝图保留为另一研究来源，不赋予个人资格。无 Royalty 时不新增蓝图门槛，种子仍能授予资格。

### 初次免费任务与后续来源

- 读档时检查：基础调教研究未完成、没有存活且非空壳的玩家 SSC 主人，并且本存档尚未发放初次任务时，提供一次免费领取任务。主人检查涵盖地图及世界中的玩家角色，不只看当前地图。
- 任务无费用和战斗要求，接受后以运输舱投放一枚种子。存档记录避免重复发放和重复领取；投放时有可定位的**“答复”**通知，任务正文及物品说明已同步四语叙事。
- 后续种子以神器为参照进入任务和古代遗迹奖励池；相应 Ideology 宝物池也可提供。商队、城镇和商船暂不刷新购买库存，已有种子仍可出售，没有制作配方。奖励池权重不是每次探索的固定掉落概率。
- 模组设置新增生成种子和手动触发领取任务按钮。手动任务可绕过研究、已有主人和历史发放条件，但不能叠出另一份未完成的领取任务；按钮本身不会直接给角色资格。

### 欲望共生：四阶段主人成长

首次成功成为 SSC 主人时建立基础成长。主人亲自完整完成一次日常调教获得 **1 经验**，亲自完成整场绑定仪式获得 **3 经验**；对象已满进度仍可提供实践经验。训导官代劳不把经验记到主人身上，中断、取消和重复结算不发经验。开发模式与 SSC 调试按钮同时开启时，可使用 **主人经验 +10** 按钮。

| 阶段 / Stage | 累计经验 / Total XP | 意识 / Consciousness | 视觉 / Sight | 听觉 / Hearing | 社交影响 / Social Impact | 伤口愈合 / Injury Healing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 萌芽 / Budding | 0 | +10 个百分点 / pp | +5 pp | +5 pp | ×1.05 | ×1.10 |
| 觉醒 / Awakening | 12 | +20 pp | +10 pp | +10 pp | ×1.10 | ×1.20 |
| 掌控 / Command | 40 | +30 pp | +20 pp | +20 pp | ×1.15 | ×1.35 |
| 升华 / Ascendance | 100 | +40 pp | +30 pp | +30 pp | ×1.20 | ×1.50 |

各行是该阶段的总加成，不叠加前面阶段；伤口愈合因子同时作用于自然及治疗后的伤口恢复。成长不新增主奴绑定关系，也不改变原有锁链与共振职责。

- 主人调教页显示**主人成长（随人格保留）**、阶段、累计经验、下一阶段进度和加成摘要；详细数值可悬停查看。
- 原**伤害分担**开关迁入主人面板，原 Gizmo 移除；保存仍使用缰绳原字段，默认值、存档和已有可用条件保持，未新增成长门槛。
- 日常实践或调试升阶会显示可定位提示；读档、人格恢复及重新进入主人身份不会误报升级。
- 健康页只展示成长名称、阶段及加成，经验放在主人面板。退出主人身份后暂停所有阶段加成，并从普通健康页隐藏条目；重新成为主人时恢复原阶段和经验。

### 人格迁移与旧档

- 永久资格和累计成长随人格凝胶保存、复制、加工及植入；成功抽取后原身体不再保留它们。植入按来源人格**精确替换**宿主旧记录，来源没有记录时也会清除宿主残留，不取双方较高值。
- 新克隆不继承来源的主人资格、主人身份或成长。人格凝胶复制仍保留记录，和克隆是不同流程。
- 非空壳、非克隆的旧主人，以及保存主人身份的旧人格凝胶，分别进行一次资格和基础成长迁移；成长从零经验开始，不按历史绑定或调教次数补经验。正常读档不反复推断记录，已经开始或完成的基础研究遵循原版蓝图兼容规则。
- 2.3.9 及更早已交付内容继续保留。欲望侵染、PNA 重置、新增主人主动技能与多调教员指派重构未在本版实现。

## English

### Seed of Desire: a new route to Master qualification

- Adds the **Seed of Desire** with dedicated artwork. An eligible pawn can use it to gain permanent personal Master qualification; use does not change the pawn's identity. Choose Master from the SSC identity panel afterward.
- **Direct manual Master selection remains enabled by default.** Disabling it requires qualification for new Master selections without removing existing Master identities. Manual selection grants no permanent qualification, and existing bond-related identity locks still apply.
- Qualification belongs to the personality, while research belongs to the save. The pawn identity section and personality-gel card show their own qualification records separately.
- With **Royalty**, a seed also supplies any missing vanilla techprints for basic Training research. It grants neither research progress nor instant completion. The separate vanilla Training techprint remains another research source and grants no personal qualification. Without Royalty, there is no added techprint gate, and seeds still grant qualification.

### Initial free quest and later sources

- Loading can offer a one-time free claim quest when Training is unfinished, no living non-Hollow player SSC Master exists and the save has not already received the initial quest. The Master check includes player pawns on maps and in the world.
- The quest requires no payment or combat. Accepting it delivers one seed by drop pod, with saved deduplication and a targeted **“Reply”** delivery letter. The quest and item use localized narrative text.
- Later seeds use artifact-referenced quest and ancient ruin reward pools, including applicable Ideology loot pools. Caravans, settlements and orbital traders do not stock them for purchase, but players can sell existing seeds. There is no crafting recipe; pool weights do not represent a fixed per-exploration drop chance.
- Settings can spawn a seed or manually trigger the claim quest. Manual triggering bypasses research, existing-Master and past-grant checks but cannot duplicate another unfinished claim quest. These buttons do not directly qualify a pawn.

### Symbiosis of Desire: four-stage Master progression

The first successful Master selection creates the progression record. Personally completing daily Training grants **1 XP**; a whole Binding Ritual grants **3 XP**. Fully trained targets still provide practice. Trainer Officer work grants no proxy credit to the Master, and interrupted, cancelled or repeated outcomes grant none. Developer mode plus SSC debug buttons enables a **Master experience +10** command.

The bilingual table above lists all four stages at **0 / 12 / 40 / 100 total XP**. Each stage replaces the previous stage's values rather than stacking with it. Bonuses affect Consciousness, Sight, Hearing, Social Impact and Injury Healing; the final stage grants **+40 / +30 / +30 percentage points, ×1.20 and ×1.50** respectively. Injury Healing affects natural and tended wounds. This progression does not create bonds or replace the existing Chains and resonance system.

- The Master tab shows **Master growth (retained with personality)**, stage, total XP, progress toward the next stage and a bonus summary with detailed hover information.
- **Damage sharing** moves from its Gizmo into this panel. It retains the bridle's existing field, default and availability rules, without a new growth requirement.
- Practice/debug level-ups produce a targeted message. Loading, personality restoration and returning to Master identity do not produce false level-ups.
- Health tooltips show lore, stage and bonuses; XP is displayed in the Master panel. Leaving Master identity suspends all stage bonuses and hides the entry from the normal health tab. Returning restores the saved stage and experience.

### Personality transfer and existing saves

- Qualification and growth survive gel saving, copying, processing and implantation, and successful extraction clears the original body. Implantation **replaces** the host records with the source personality's exact values, including removing the host record when the source has none.
- New clones inherit neither source Master qualification, Master identity nor growth. Personality-gel copying remains a separate process that preserves these records.
- Eligible non-Hollow, non-clone legacy Masters and legacy gels holding Master identity receive one-time qualification and zero-XP baseline migrations. Historical training or bond counts do not grant retroactive XP. Repeated loads do not continually infer records, and already-started/completed research follows vanilla techprint compatibility.
- Previously released 2.3.9 and earlier features remain included. Desire Infestation, the PNA redesign, new Master active abilities and the multi-trainer assignment overhaul are not implemented in this release.

Natural reward frequency and overall balance remain subject to further observation. See the [validation record](2.4.0版本验证.md) for the current automated checks and package details.
