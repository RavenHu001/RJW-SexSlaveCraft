**SexSlaveCraft 2.4.0｜欲望之种与主人成长**

- **欲望之种**：永久取得个人主人资格，不自动改变身份；默认仍允许手动选择主人，可在设置中关闭。
- **获取与研究**：初次免费任务按存档去重，后续来自任务／遗迹，暂不进入贸易库存。有 Royalty 时种子补齐调教研究蓝图，原版独立蓝图保留。
- **欲望共生**：萌芽 → 觉醒 → 掌控 → 升华。亲自完成日常调教 +1 经验，整场仪式 +3，逐步强化意识、视觉、听觉、社交影响和伤口愈合。
- **主人面板**：显示阶段、经验及进度，伤害分担开关迁入面板。资格与成长随人格凝胶保留；退出主人身份隐藏成长并暂停加成，返回恢复原阶段和经验。新克隆不继承资格、主人身份或成长。

**SexSlaveCraft 2.4.0 — Seed of Desire and Master progression**

- **Seed of Desire:** permanently grants personal Master qualification without changing identity. Direct manual Master selection remains enabled by default and can be disabled in settings.
- **Acquisition and research:** a save-deduplicated free quest provides initial access; later seeds come from quest/ruin rewards, with trader stock disabled. With Royalty, seeds supply missing Training techprints; the separate vanilla techprint remains available.
- **Symbiosis of Desire:** Budding → Awakening → Command → Ascendance. Personally completed daily Training grants +1 XP; a whole Ritual grants +3. Stages boost Consciousness, Sight, Hearing, Social Impact and Injury Healing.
- **Master panel:** shows stage, XP and progress, and hosts damage sharing. Qualification and growth transfer with personality gel. Leaving Master identity hides growth and pauses bonuses; returning restores the saved stage and XP. New clones inherit neither qualification, Master identity nor growth.
