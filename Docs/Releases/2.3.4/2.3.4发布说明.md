## 中文

### 完整战斗员特化

- 完成战斗员研究后，SSC 身份为性奴的角色可在特化面板选择培养；无需绑定主人或达到特定锁链阶段。原版奴隶也须设置为 SSC 性奴，主人和未设定身份的角色不显示特化培养区。
- 普通进度达到 20%／50% 时解锁基础／熟练收益，提高射击和近战命中评分，缩短瞄准及近战冷却，降低精神崩溃临界值。熟练阶段承伤 ×0.90，终极阶段承伤 ×0.80；同一方向的阶段效果不叠加，切换方向保留各自进度。
- 正常完成日常调教按评分增长，单次最多 4 个百分点；实际绑定主人施教为基础经验的 1.5 倍，最多 6 个百分点。直接击杀按目标实际体型增长，单次最多 3 个百分点。中断调教、绑定仪式、屠宰及间接死亡不提供该经验，并防止重复结算。
- 普通培养完成后，抽取人格凝胶，在雕刻台终极化后重新植入。兼容八种凝胶基底，消耗材料前复查资格；普通完成不会自动终极化。
- 终极成果随人格保存，切换培养方向、解绑或锁链退阶不清除成果。抽取或替换人格会清理旧身体对应的战斗员状态、技能和临时强化；重新植入按人格恢复流程授予能力。

### 战斗超频、图标与测试按钮

- 终极战斗员在征召时可对自身发动战斗超频，持续 1 游戏小时，冷却 4 游戏小时。强化为近战伤害 ×1.30、近战冷却 ×0.75、远程冷却 ×0.80、承伤 ×0.75、移动速度 ×1.25、疼痛 ×0.50；解除征召不提前结束已发动的效果。
- 正常存读档保留技能冷却和强化剩余时间。人格迁移按共用流程重新授予技能，不携带原身体的旧冷却或临时强化。
- 修复能力效果组件初始化红字，以及技能发动后未添加对应健康状态的问题。
- 新增正面女性剪影、皮革圆环项圈和紫色神经纹路构成的平面化专属图标。
- 同时开启游戏开发模式和模组调试按钮后，可直接拉满当前特化的普通培养进度，便于测试；该按钮不会自动完成终极化。

### 文案与特化显示

- 简中、繁中、英文和俄文统一采用纳米机械液进一步改造身体与神经系统、主动活化产生超频的设定。技能按钮保留功能概述，健康状态描述不重复游戏自动列出的属性效果；补充普通培养完成后的终极化操作提示。
- 战斗员健康状态采用与训导官一致的性奴特化命名格式；删除终极公交车和奶牛重复显示的阶段名称及对应翻译。
- 训导官使用青绿色，禁用终极状态为灰绿色；战斗员使用淡紫色，终极状态颜色更明亮。

### 人格排泄任务修复

- 接收者不会在正常人格排泄期间被搬运、进食、睡眠或娱乐等自动工作抢走。手动和自动发起采用相同保护，保留玩家取消与任务失效时的退出。
- 修复两名人格排泄执行者争抢同一目标的问题。先开始者保持执行，后来者被拒绝，原任务与进度不因争抢重启；取消后允许其他执行者重新建立配对并接手。
- 任务结束、取消或配对失效后释放相应保护，不依赖新增的持久化锁。两轮修复已同步本机并获维护者实机有效确认，提交为 `928b5d2`。

## English

### Complete Combatant specialization

- After Combatant research, pawns with the SSC Sex Slave identity can select the specialization. No master bond or Chain-stage threshold is required. Vanilla slaves also need the SSC identity; Masters and unset identities do not show the specialization section.
- Basic and proficient bonuses unlock at 20% and 50%, improving shooting/melee accuracy ratings, aim time, melee recovery and mental stability. Incoming damage is ×0.90 at proficiency and ×0.80 after finalization. Stages do not stack; switching paths retains their separate progress.
- Completed daily Training grants score-based experience capped at 4 percentage points. Training by the actual bound master grants 1.5 times the base reward, capped at 6 points. Direct kills grant experience based on actual victim body size, capped at 3 points. Interrupted Training, Binding Rituals, slaughter, indirect deaths and duplicate payouts are excluded.
- Full ordinary progress requires personality extraction, gel finalization at a sculpting table and reinsertion. All eight gel bases are supported, with eligibility checked before consuming materials. Full progress does not automatically finalize the specialization.
- Final achievements follow the personality and survive path changes, unbinding and Chain regression. Extraction or replacement clears the old body's Combatant state, ability and temporary buff; reinsertion grants abilities through the personality restoration flow.

### Combat Overdrive, icon and testing command

- Final combatants can activate Combat Overdrive on themselves while drafted. It lasts 1 in-game hour with a 4-hour cooldown: ×1.30 melee damage, ×0.75 melee cooldown, ×0.80 ranged cooldown, ×0.75 incoming damage, ×1.25 movement speed and ×0.50 pain. Undrafting does not end an active buff.
- Normal saves retain cooldown and remaining buff duration. Personality transfer grants the ability through the shared flow without carrying over the old body's cooldown or temporary buff.
- Fixes ability effect-component initialization errors and missing health effects after activation.
- Adds a dedicated flat icon featuring a frontal female silhouette, a ringed leather collar and violet neural accents.
- With developer mode and mod debug gizmos enabled, a command completes the currently selected ordinary specialization for testing. It does not finalize it.

### Text and specialization display

- Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian consistently describe nanofluid modification of the body and nervous system, with active stimulation producing Overdrive. Ability text stays concise; health descriptions avoid repeating automatically listed attributes and explain the next finalization step.
- Combatant health effects follow the Training Officer's sex-slave-specialization naming format. Duplicate final Public Use and Cow stage names and their translations are removed.
- Training Officer labels use teal, with muted gray-green for disabled final states. Combatant labels use lavender, with a brighter final state.

### Personality-excretion fixes

- Automatic hauling, eating, sleeping and recreation jobs no longer replace the recipient's job during normal personality excretion. Manually and automatically initiated jobs receive the same protection; explicit cancellation and invalid-job cleanup remain available.
- Prevents two initiators from competing for one target. The first continues and later contenders are rejected without restarting the scene or progress. Cancellation allows another initiator to establish a new pair.
- Protection ends with completion, cancellation or an invalid pair, without a new persistent lock. Both fixes were installed locally and confirmed effective in game by the maintainer (commit `928b5d2`).

### Documentation, validation and upgrading

- Adds a bilingual mod overview, Combatant implementation/completion records and updated player guides, development logs and plans. Unimplemented plans are not counted as completed features.
- Expands automated regression from 18 to 19 suites. The excretion fixes add 32 cases; the latest feature baseline passed 886/886 cases. A Combatant audit checks XML, localization, icon references, local game components and duration conversion. See the [validation record](2.3.4版本验证.md) for dated evidence and in-game scope.
- RimWorld 1.6, Harmony and RJW remain the requirements. Legacy RimTalk integration remains suspended; unfinished Cat/Rabbit choices remain disabled. See [2.3.2](../2.3.2/2.3.2发布说明.md) for the earlier Training Officer upgrade guidance.
- Select a package matching the required version; historical 2.3.3 packages do not contain these additions. Once the 2.3.4 package is available, close the game, move the old folder out of `RimWorld/Mods/` and install the replacement `RJW-SexSlaveCraft-TieJin-Modify` folder to avoid stale resources.
