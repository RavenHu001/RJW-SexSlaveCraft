## 中文

### 1. 新增“训导官”特化

训导官是代主人调教其他性奴的职能特化。角色仍使用 SSC 性奴身份，调教工作复用现有任务和身体开发机制，绑定归属保持不变。

- 新增“训导官特化”研究，前置为“性奴特化”，研究成本 1200；解锁培养方向与终极人格塑形配方。
- 开始及继续培养须为 **SSC 性奴身份的自由殖民者**，已绑定实际主人，且当前锁链达到第 3 阶段“屈服性奴”或更高。原版奴隶、囚犯不满足这一执行者条件；指定调教员不能代替实际主人。
- 普通培养达到 **20%** 后取得基础调教员资格，达到 **50%** 进入熟练属性阶段。任职仍须手动开启个人调教员开关。
- 主人继续固定具备调教员身份；SSC 身份为“未选择”的角色不能任职。普通性奴不能再仅凭手动开关直接成为调教员。
- 指派菜单、自动工作、手动任务、绑定仪式以及既有调教员同床许可和标签共同读取当前有效资格。工作安排、身体、目标许可、冷却和可达性等条件仍各自生效。

### 2. 培养进度与终极化

| 已成功完成的行为 | 当前训导官培养进度增量 |
| --- | --- |
| 接受日常调教或完成整场绑定仪式的受训者 | +5 个百分点 |
| 以有效调教员身份完成日常调教或整场绑定仪式 | +2.5 个百分点 |
| 本人主动发起并正常完成的 RJW 双人行为 | +1 个百分点 |

- 主动双人行为包含现有规则允许的强制行为。普通被动参与和单人行为不取得这项主动经验；专用调教与人格任务不会重复计入普通双人奖励。
- 经验只增加到当前正在培养、且持续条件有效的训导官方向。准备失败、取消或未完成的任务不会冒充成功结算；同一次完成事件不重复领取。
- 普通培养达到 100% 后仍须通过人格凝胶终极化。新增雕刻台配方，消耗一份符合条件的人格凝胶，工作量为 3500；普通与已编辑等既有八种凝胶基底均可按资格筛选。
- 终极化会替换同系普通状态；已持有有效或禁用终极记录的人格不能重复加工。配方开始消耗之前再次检查来源，失效、缺失、重复及异常堆叠原料会保留材料与账单次数。
- 界面完成状态和配方共用既有 `0.999` 浮点容差，避免实际累计略低于 1 时出现“显示 100% 却未完成”的不一致。

### 3. 分阶段属性加成

以下为当前 XML 实际数值；每个阶段提供该行的完整加成，阶段之间不累加。

| 当前有效状态 | 崩溃临界值 | 压制能力¹ | 教化能力¹ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 普通培养低于 20% | 无 | 无 | 无 |
| 普通培养 20% 至不足 50% | −3 个百分点 | +10 个百分点 | ×1.05 |
| 普通培养达到 50%、尚未终极化 | −6 个百分点 | +20 个百分点 | ×1.10 |
| 有效终极训导官 | −10 个百分点 | +30 个百分点 | ×1.15 |
| 终极禁用，或退出普通培养且没有有效终极状态 | 无 | 无 | 无 |

¹ 压制能力和教化能力要求启用 Ideology；未启用时保留崩溃临界值加成。压制能力作用于原版监管者压制原版奴隶，教化能力增强角色将他人转变为**自身文化**的能力。这些属性不会让 SSC 调教自动执行压制或文化转换。

个人调教员开关控制是否任职；关闭开关不取消仍然有效的特化属性。

### 4. 持续条件、限制规则与人格传承

- 普通训导官失去绑定、所需锁链阶段或自由殖民者资格时，退出当前培养并保存历史进度；重新满足条件后可手动选择恢复培养。
- 终极化记录可以在培养其他方向或选择“未选择”后保留。持续条件失效时转为禁用，暂停资格、属性和特化强制效果；恢复条件后重新生效。
- 个人任职选择和已有指派在失格时保留为停用状态。恢复资格后按保存的开关决定任职，玩家也可在失格时主动关闭开关。
- 当前选中训导官方向且持续条件有效时，**从零进度起**提供“允许主动发起自愿性行为”和“允许主动发起强制行为”两项特化强制值，不要求达到 20% 或开启调教员开关；有效终极状态可跨方向继续提供。
- 上述两项沿用 2.3.1 的限制总开关、特化强制开关及“装备强制 > 特化强制 > 个人保存值”优先级。接收方的规则继续独立检查，覆盖结果不写回个人选择。
- 人格凝胶保存当前培养方向、各方向进度及终极化事实。植入时以源人格整体替换宿主相关历史，清除宿主残留，再按接收身体最终条件决定普通、终极或禁用状态，避免恢复中暂时无绑定造成进度丢失。

### 5. 从 2.3.1 升级时的变化

旧存档中的 **SSC 性奴个人调教员开关会一次性关闭**，不会仅凭旧身份或原指派自动取得训导官资格。已有主人、绑定、指派及特化进度保留，停用指派不会自动改派给其他人；主人固定资格保持原规则。

升级后应检查原有性奴调教员：完成研究、满足培养条件并达到 20% 后，手动重新开启任职；也可通过有效终极状态取得资格。迁移处理包含地图外及间接被指派对象，重复读档不再次覆盖迁移后的新选择。

### 6. 仪式选人优化与修复

- SSC 绑定仪式的两个执行槽默认留空，由玩家手动选择；观众继续使用原版分配。
- 打开窗口、浏览候选和高亮角色时使用轻量检查；实际选入、替换、交换及点击开始时验证当前人员和组合，减少反复完整查询。
- 普通身体资格检查最多调用一次 RJW，不再在候选扫描中生成完整诊断报告或大量拒绝警告；显式诊断入口保留。
- 选择被拒绝时恢复原角色、观众分配及品质预览；覆盖延迟点击、右键和拖拽，保留合法的满槽替换。
- 修复空角色进入候选及观众流程造成的空引用和后续头像绘制红字。补丁限定 SSC 仪式准备窗口，其他仪式保持原流程。

### 7. 连续调教与 Education 兼容

- 修复连续处理多个待调教目标时，调教员停在前一位置、工作栏显示下一目标但接收任务尚未启动的问题。接近方式统一为 `Touch`，走位前清理调教员遗留的 UAP 位置锁。
- 存档中已停止寻路的在途任务可限频重试；持续无法启动时有限退出并使用既有失败冷却，避免无限显示正在调教。
- 接收任务只释放当前发起 Job 仍实际持有的预约，修复“目标未被预约却尝试释放”的红字，并保留其他 Job 的预约。
- 可选兼容 **Progression: Education**：自动调教避让已加入活动课程的听课者和授课者；目标在途中或执行中加入课程时退出，下课离开活动后恢复候选资格。
- Education 不成为必需依赖。手动强制命令保持原行为，教育模组仍可能将其拉回课堂；本版不修改教育侧的学生资格或课程调度。

### 8. 界面、本地化与维护

- ITab 提供训导官选择、培养完成、有效终极和禁用状态，以及研究、身份、绑定和锁链不足的原因；开关显示保存选择与当前任职状态。
- 修复终极化后无法保持或无法看出“未选择”的两条路径：持久保存明确留空意图，按钮与进度只显示当前方向。清空后显示“未选择”和 0%，保留已取得的终极效果及历史进度。
- 身份区按实际文字高度布局，保持单列并支持长译文；同次资格查询复用锁链，界面复用本次查询结果。
- 同步简中、繁中、英文、俄文的研究、配方、状态、阶段和提示，补齐熟练阶段译文，简化任职与禁用说明。
- 统一回归入口从 2.3.1 的 16 套扩展至 18 套，新增仪式选人及训导官人格恢复集成测试，并补充凝胶消耗顺序、连续目标、预约交接、浮点进度和真实 ITab 显示路径的回归。
- 状态维护复用现有低频更新和相关状态事件，稳定状态避免重复写入。没有新增逐 Tick 全图维护；实际耗时与长期数值平衡仍未量化。

### 验证状态

维护者已分批确认仪式选人优化及点击修复、Education 自动避让、训导官主体及属性加成、连续调教、预约释放和“未选择”显示修复有效。历史完整回归曾为 **18 套 780/780**，后续显示修复专项为 **24/24 和 87/87**；这些结果对应各自开发批次。

本次整理时，最新代码的身份专项检查为 **85/87**，两项失败是新增阶段与文案调整后未同步的旧测试断言，详见[整理与验证记录](2.3.2版本验证.md)。本版尚未执行正式发布构建及验收，不将历史通过结果当作当前发布预检结论。

训导官专属心情和社交反馈、定向身体开发、自我调教及其他未来规划未包含在本版已实现内容中。

## English

### Training Officer specialization

Training Officers perform existing Training duties on their owner's behalf while retaining their SSC Sex Slave identity and owner bond.

- Adds a 1,200-point Training Officer research project after Slave Specialization, unlocking the direction and final personality-shaping recipe.
- Requires an SSC Sex Slave who is a free colonist, has an actual owner bond, and has reached Chain stage 3 or higher. Vanilla slaves and prisoners do not meet the trainer condition.
- Ordinary progress unlocks trainer eligibility at **20%** and stronger attribute bonuses at **50%**. The player must still enable the personal trainer toggle. Masters retain their fixed trainer identity; ordinary Sex Slaves can no longer opt in without specialization eligibility.
- Assignment, automatic work, manual Training, ritual roles, and existing trainer bed permissions/badges read the same effective eligibility. Normal job requirements and target permissions remain in force.

### Progression, finalization and bonuses

- Successful Training received grants **5 percentage points**, Training performed as an eligible trainer grants **2.5**, and completed ordinary RJW pair activity initiated by the pawn grants **1**. This includes forced initiation permitted by the existing rules. Passive participation, solo activity, cancelled jobs and duplicate completion callbacks do not grant the initiation reward; dedicated Training/personality jobs do not receive it again.
- Growth requires the current Training Officer direction and valid ongoing conditions. A complete binding ritual settles once for the whole ritual.
- Reaching full ordinary progress does not finalize automatically. The sculpting-table recipe uses one eligible personality gel and 3,500 work, supporting the existing eight gel bases. Active and disabled final records prevent repeat finalization.
- Revalidates ingredients before consumption and bill completion, preserving materials and repeat counts for invalid or malformed inputs. The UI and recipe share the existing `0.999` completion tolerance.

| Effective stage | Mental break threshold | Suppression power¹ | Conversion power¹ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Below 20% ordinary progress | None | None | None |
| 20% to below 50% | −3 percentage points | +10 percentage points | ×1.05 |
| 50% or higher, not finalized | −6 percentage points | +20 percentage points | ×1.10 |
| Active final state | −10 percentage points | +30 percentage points | ×1.15 |
| Disabled final state, or no active ordinary/final state | None | None | None |

Each row replaces the preceding stage's bonuses. ¹ Suppression and conversion bonuses require Ideology; without it, the mental break threshold bonus remains. They affect native suppression of vanilla slaves and conversion toward the pawn's own ideoligion. SSC Training does not automatically perform either action. Turning off trainer duty does not remove otherwise valid specialization attributes.

### State, permissions and upgrading

- Losing the owner bond, required Chain stage or free-colonist status ends ordinary cultivation while saving its progress. It can be selected again when eligible. Final records persist across direction changes and Unset; invalid conditions disable their benefits until conditions recover.
- Saved trainer toggles and assignments remain during temporary ineligibility. Two enabled specialization overrides allow consensual and forced initiation from **zero progress**, independent of the trainer toggle; active finalization retains them across directions. Global switches, equipment priority and the recipient's own permissions still apply, without overwriting saved choices.
- Personality gels transfer source progress/history and final records, remove conflicting host remnants, and defer eligibility maintenance until the recipient's identity and bond have been restored.
- **Upgrading from 2.3.1 resets old Sex Slave trainer toggles once.** Bonds, assignments and progress are retained; inactive assignments do not silently move to another trainer. Develop an eligible Training Officer and manually enable duty again. Masters retain their fixed eligibility, and subsequent loads preserve new choices.

### Ritual selection, job reliability and compatibility

- The two SSC ritual performer slots start empty for manual selection; spectators retain native assignment. Candidate browsing uses lightweight checks, with full validation on selection, replacement, swapping and start. Normal eligibility checks call RJW at most once and avoid full diagnostic reports during scanning.
- Invalid selections restore roles, spectators and quality previews. Fixes delayed clicks, context menus, drag-and-drop, valid full-slot replacements, null participants and resulting portrait errors within the SSC ritual window.
- Fixes consecutive Training targets getting stuck at the previous location. Uses Touch approach, clears the trainer's stale UAP position lock, and retries a stopped saved path at limited intervals before a bounded failure exit.
- Releases reservations only when the initiating job still owns them, avoiding repeated-release errors and preserving unrelated reservations.
- Optional Progression: Education compatibility makes automatic Training yield to active students and teachers, including classes joined during travel or execution. Eligibility resumes after leaving the class. Manual forced orders retain existing behavior and can still be interrupted by Education; the education mod's own rules are unchanged.

### Interface, localization and validation

- Adds specialization selection and status/reason feedback, separates saved trainer choice from effective duty, and fits long identity text in one column.
- Preserves explicit Unset choices after finalization and displays Unset/0% instead of substituting a historical finalized direction. Acquired final benefits and training history remain.
- Updates Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian research, recipe, health-stage and eligibility text, including the experienced stage.
- Expands the existing unified runner from 16 to 18 suites with ritual-selection and personality-restoration integration coverage, plus ingredient-consumption, receiver-handoff, reservation, progress and actual UI-text regressions. Reuses existing infrequent updates and state events; no measured performance percentage is claimed.
- The maintainer confirmed the main feature, attribute bonuses and reported fixes in separate in-game rounds. Historical results include **780/780 across 18 suites** and later **24/24 plus 87/87** targeted checks. The current identity check is **85/87**, with two outdated assertions after stage/text changes; see the [validation status](2.3.2版本验证.md). Release build and validation are still pending.
- Dedicated Training Officer mood/social feedback, targeted body development, self-training and other future plans are outside the implemented scope of 2.3.2.
