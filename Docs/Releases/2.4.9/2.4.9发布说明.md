### Three tiers of aphrodisiac emitters

Adds three 1×1 devices using the same final loaded RJW **HumpShroomEffect**. A target must be within the radius and in the same room. There is no additional identity, faction, age or species filter. Multiple devices add to the same effect; stopping exposure lets the existing effect decay normally. This is humpshroom exposure, not PNA-load production or a new gas simulation.

| Device | Radius | Running cost | Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medieval censer | Fixed 5 cells | No electricity; 1 humpshroom/day, capacity 5 | Runs while fueled; no on/off switch |
| Industrial electric atomizer | 5–10 cells, default 5 | 50＋2r² W; 0.02r² humpshrooms/day, capacity 10 | Direct player switch and integer radius slider |
| Spacer emitter | 5–20 cells, default 10 | 100＋4r² W; no running refills | Direct player switch and integer radius slider |

The two lower tiers are built empty and use vanilla refueling work. PNA is a construction ingredient, not an ongoing refill. Research progresses through 600-point incense application, 1200-point electric mist and the 3000-point spacer emitter; the latter also retains fabrication/PNA-concentration requirements. Existing completed research is not reset.

All three have dedicated art and four-language descriptions. Source smoke, room coverage, staggered lifetimes and fade-out have been refined. Electricity/fuel loss or switching off stops new exposure and smoke. Effect stages and caps follow the actual loaded Def, including external patches; no culture-mod dependency or fixed four-stage promise is added. Effect-scan optimization remains deferred, with no measured performance claim.

### Personal PNA stage maintenance, on maps and in caravans

The Training tab adds **Off plus five target stages**, defaulting to Off. The choice belongs to the current **Chain**, not the personality or body load: save/load preserves it, removing/replacing the Chain discards it, and a new Chain starts Off. It is an enum setting, separate from the PNA production toggle and general ability booleans.

- **Maps:** eligible bound SSC slaves try one ordinary PNA before their next ordinary job. Current work, eating, sleeping, Training, forced orders and queued jobs are not interrupted. Own inventory is preferred, then reachable, permitted and reservable map supplies. Missing supplies fall back to normal work.
- **Caravans:** reuses the vanilla 120-tick scheduled-drug check. Ordinary policy runs first; if still below target, SSC adds at most one ordinary PNA per pawn per check. Own inventory is preferred, then shared caravan inventory including pack animals. Travel and daytime stops are supported; night rest pauses maintenance. The Master need not travel with the caravan.
- **Limits:** ordinary PNA only; no automatic concentrate substitution. Stage 1 means replenishment after activation disappears. One dose can overshoot a target stage. The target uses current activation-Def thresholds; SSC neither creates a new need nor replaces native drug policies.

Pending map jobs and consumption recheck their original Chain, owner, settings and personality context. Caravan membership and item ownership are checked again at consumption. Changing settings or losing eligibility cannot authorize an old request against a new carrier.

### Depravity gel: production, costs and consumption

Adds an ingestible **Depravity Gel**, separate from Personality Gel used for personality transfer. All four steps, dedicated 256×256 art and concise four-language text have scoped in-game acceptance.

- **Production:** eligible SSC slaves with completed anal development and the required present organ can enable the eighth body ability, default Off. The body Comp alone saves the choice and batch. Base output is one unit per two days, with Chain-stage factors 1/1.25/1.5/2 (stage 0 uses 1). Map and caravan delivery are supported; failed delivery keeps a full batch. Off preserves progress, without offline catch-up.
- **Costs and acceleration:** while actually accumulating, work speed×0.90 and natural Corruption decay×2 apply. Off, lost eligibility/location or a full batch stops these costs; existing global decay settings and protections remain. The pawn's first eligible real orgasm per scene adds 0.10 of a batch. A full batch still consumes that opportunity, without banking it for later.
- **Drug effect:** one unit, one layer lasting one game day; mood+20 and work speed×0.95. Another dose refreshes duration rather than stacking. Ordinary humanlikes can receive it; SSC Masters are immune and becoming a Master clears the effect. Duration/effect belong to the body, without personality-transfer or clone copying.
- **Consumption reward:** each real orgasm by the affected pawn grants two extra percentage points of Corruption, up to ten extra points from gel per current RJW job, settled immediately. Modern scoring retains the 12% unbound cap and current Chain-stage caps; old scoring uses its existing MaxLevel. No automatic binding/progression or missing-need creation is added; other rewards remain independent.
- **Scene persistence:** production claims and consumed reward budgets are saved separately. Redosing, cycle resets, changing the driver for the same Job and loading do not refresh the budget. Only connected real RJW event paths apply; vanilla Lovin, future idle stimulation and independent external timers are not inferred as events.

Values are accepted development settings, open to later balance. Trader supply/recipes, source-fragment data, tolerance/addiction/overdose and derivative uses are outside this delivery. See the [completion record](../../Development/Completed/堕落凝胶/堕落凝胶四步开发完成核对.md).

Release-gate integration also fixes idle gel-observer allocations before RJW's actual event threshold, while preserving event rewards and saved budgets. The original zero-allocation assertion and native save checks remain in place.

### 三档催情发烟器

新增三种1×1设备，统一累积实际环境最终加载的RJW **HumpShroomEffect**。目标须同时在半径内与同一房间，不额外按身份、阵营、年龄或种族筛选。多台设备累积同一药效，停止暴露后原药效自然衰减；不增加PNA负荷，不模拟真实气体扩散。

| 设备 | 半径 | 运行消耗 | 控制 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 中世纪催情香薰炉 | 固定5格 | 无电；驼峰菇1份／天，容量5份 | 有料运行，无开关 |
| 工业电热催情雾化器 | 5～10格，默认5 | 50＋2r² W；驼峰菇0.02r²份／天，容量10份 | 玩家直接开关、整数半径滑条 |
| 太空催情发烟器 | 5～20格，默认10 | 100＋4r² W；无需运行补料 | 玩家直接开关、整数半径滑条 |

### 个人PNA阶段维持：地图与旅队

调教页新增**关闭＋五阶段**目标，默认关闭。设置保存在当前**锁链**，不属于人格或身体负荷；存读档保留，删链／换链丢失，新链关闭。此项是枚举设置，与PNA生产启停及通用布尔能力分别处理。

- **地图：** 合格绑定性奴在下一份普通工作前尝试补一份普通PNA，不抢断正在工作、吃睡、调教、强制命令或排队任务。优先自有背包，其次本图可达、未禁止且可预留药物；缺药继续原工作。
- **旅队：** 沿原版120tick服药检查，先执行原政策，仍低于目标才每人每轮至多追加一份。优先自有背包，其次旅队共享库存，包含驮畜；行进及白天停留可补给，夜休暂停，主人无需同行。
- **边界：** 仅用普通原液，不自动替换浓缩液。第一档表示活化消失后再补，单份可能超过所选阶段。阈值读取当前活化Def，不新增Need，也不替代原版服药政策。

地图任务和消费复查原链、主人、设置世代及人格上下文；旅队还在消费前复查实际成员与物品持有者。设置改变或失格后，旧请求不能借新载体重新取得权限。

### 堕落凝胶：生产、代价与服用收益

新增可服用物品**堕落凝胶**，与用于人格转移的“人格凝胶”分开处理。四步功能、专用256×256贴图与精简四语说明均已获对应实机认可。

- **生产：** 肛门开发完成且当前身体仍有合格器官的SSC性奴，可在身体管理中启用第八项通用能力，默认关闭。选择及批次库存由身体Comp唯一保存；基础2天1份，锁链四阶段产率倍率1／1.25／1.5／2，零阶1倍。地图与旅队均有交付路径，交付失败保留满批次；关闭保留进度，不离线补算。
- **积累代价与催熟：** 实际可积累时工作速度×0.90、恶堕自然衰减×2；关闭、失格、无交付位置或满批次时停代价，原全局衰减开关与维持规则保留。本人每场首次合格真实高潮增加0.10批次；满批次仍消耗机会，不预存到下批。
- **服用药效：** 一次一份，单层持续1游戏日，心情+20、工作速度×0.95；续药刷新期限，不叠层。普通类人角色可受药，SSC主人免疫，转主人清除此药效。期限与药效属于当前身体，不随人格转移或克隆复制。
- **消费收益：** 受药者本人的真实高潮每次额外增加2个百分点恶堕，凝胶每个当前RJW任务最多额外10个百分点，实时结算。新版评分无链受原12%上限限制，有链按当前阶段；旧评分沿现有MaxLevel。不自动绑定或进阶，不为缺需求者补建需求，其他来源收益独立计算。
- **场次保存：** 生产催熟认领和消费已用额度独立保存；续药、周期重设、同Job换驱动及读档不刷新额度。支持已接入的真实RJW事件入口，不把原版Lovin、未来日常刺激或外部独立计时器折算成事件。

当前数值为已验收的开发配置，长期平衡仍可调整。商人供货／制作配方、来源碎片数据化、耐受／成瘾／过量与衍生用途不在本次交付范围。完成依据见[四步核对](../../Development/Completed/堕落凝胶/堕落凝胶四步开发完成核对.md)。

发布集成另修复未达RJW实际事件条件时凝胶观察器的空调用分配；事件收益与保存额度不变，原零分配及原生存档断言保留。
