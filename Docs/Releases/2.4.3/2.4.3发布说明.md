## English

**2.4.3 — released 2026-10-20.** All development after `v2.4.2` through `dea5c78` is included, followed by documentation reconciliation and release metadata preparation. [Download v2.4.3](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.3).

New features target **RimWorld 1.7**. The **1.6 runtime and Common baseline remain frozen at SSC 2.4.1**. Gel appearance and rendering introduced in 2.4.2 remain available on 1.7.

### Bond overview and low-tech progression

- Master/slave cards show validated relationships, portrait navigation and trainer assignment. Masters can select available bound pawns on the current map as a group. Ordinary queries use direct relationship reads and local refresh; global scans are limited to explicit developer diagnostics.
- Existing research without an industrial requirement is available at lower technology levels, while material, skill, techprint and body requirements remain separate.
- **Rough concentration** at campfires and compatible stoves converts **10 normal PNA into 1 PNA Plus**, work 600, using Cooking. PNA Concentration research is required. Drug-lab recipes retain 5→1 and 20→4 efficiency. Modded tables supporting vanilla simple-meal recipes receive the rough recipe after loading.

### Nine PNA weapons across three eras

| Era | Weapons | Base PNA load per effective hit | Research and crafting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medieval | Short spear / blowpipe / light crossbow | 15 / 6 / 10 percentage points | Primitive PNA Weapons, 800 points; normal PNA at crafting; smithies, with crafting-spot support for the blowpipe |
| Industrial | Short whip / repeating needle gun / precision needle rifle | 18 / 8 per needle / 20 points | Industrial PNA Weapons, 1600 points; concentrated PNA; machining table |
| Spacer | Silk whip / pulse gun / projector gun | 20 / 10 per pulse / 25 points | PNA Shape Control, 3000 points; Industrial PNA Weapons + Fabrication; fabrication bench |

Crafting consumes the fluid; finished weapons require no separate ammunition, refilling or gas management. Potency displays show the baseline, before consciousness resistance and target rules. Weapons use normal quality scaling for physical damage and armor penetration; potency and spacer repair budgets do not scale with quality.

Medieval and industrial weapons require a fresh qualifying organic open wound. Armor blocks, blunt conversions and ordinary defensive strikes do not deliver PNA; wounds remain and can be fatal. Spacer main attacks can be absorbed without wounds or ordinary armor wear by valid Chain-bound targets, activating PNA and repairing one eligible injury immediately. Repair budgets are **1 / 0.5 / 1.25 injury-severity points** per effective hit; overflow is discarded. Shields and cover still intercept attacks. Current Masters are protected from spacer main attacks; ordinary gun-butt attacks retain normal physical rules.

PNA delivery is isolated from Master damage sharing: transferred physical damage does not carry PNA or create resistance records. Industrial/spacer whip working ends bypass the dodge of non-hostile valid Chain-bound targets without adding a new melee-threat entry; attackers can still miss.

### Art and feedback

All nine weapons have dedicated art. Industrial and spacer whips have item, idle, strike and recovery images with aligned grips and hit afterimages. Purple trails mark ranged projectiles; inverted hearts mark accepted PNA input; the short spear has its own contact glow. Industrial needle guns use pneumatic sounds; spacer guns retain the accepted vanilla charge-weapon sounds. Further whip sound work is deferred after the trial was reverted.

### Bestow Bliss dose adjustment

On 1.7, eligible Bestow Bliss completions derive PNA load from the Master's current RJW fluid-producing organs: effective output is clamped to **7.5–250**, giving **3–100 load points**; output **20 gives 8 points**. This uses body output capacity, rather than fluid actually transferred during the encounter. Ordinary producer supply remains 2 points. Need refill, addiction reinforcement, Corruption, development and feedback retain their existing rules.

### Upgrade and validation

**The old Basic PNA Launcher, its projectile, recipe and dedicated research are removed from 1.7. Saves referencing these definitions are not supported; no migration or replacement is provided.** Frozen 1.6 retains its original content. Replace the entire old mod folder when upgrading.

Batch acceptance and earlier automated evidence retain their original dates. The 1.7 DLL, BUILD-INFO, About and source product metadata are aligned to **2.4.3**, while 1.6 remains frozen at 2.4.1. Current build, full release gate and package audit results are recorded in [validation](2.4.3版本验证.md); earlier 40/3849 and targeted 4/1083 results remain historical evidence. The metadata-only rebuild was not launched again in game. See the [pending checks](../../Development/Verification/README.md) for remaining scope.

- 主从卡片显示经锁链验证的关系、头像定位和调教员指派；主人可批选当前地图的有效成员。普通查询直接读取现有关系、局部刷新，全局扫描仅供显式开发诊断使用。

### 三代九把 PNA 武器

| 阶段 | 武器 | 每次有效命中基础 PNA 负荷 | 研究与制作 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 中世纪 | 短矛／吹箭筒／轻弩 | 15／6／10 个百分点 | 原始纳米催情液武器，800 点；制造耗普通原液；锻造台，吹箭筒另支持手工制作点 |
| 工业 | 短鞭／速射针枪／精确针枪 | 18／每针 8／20 个百分点 | 工业纳米催情液武器，1600 点；制造耗浓缩液；机械加工台 |
| 太空 | 丝鞭／脉冲枪／投射枪 | 20／每发 10／25 个百分点 | 纳米催情液形态控制，3000 点；工业武器＋精密装配前置；精密装配台 |

成品无需独立弹药、补液或补气。效力栏显示修正前基线，实际输入仍受意识抗性与对象规则影响。武器伤害和穿甲采用原版品质规则；PNA 效力与太空修复预算不随品质增加。

中世纪／工业武器必须造成合格有机开放伤口才施药；完全挡甲、转钝及普通防身攻击不施药，真实伤口仍可能致死。太空主攻击对有效锁链目标无伤吸收、不磨损普通衣甲，并即时修复一处合格伤势；每次预算分别为 **1／0.5／1.25 点伤势严重度**，溢出舍弃。护盾、掩体仍会拦截。主人免受太空主攻击，普通枪托撞击仍按正常物理规则处理。

PNA 与主人伤害分摊隔离：转移伤害只保留物理作用，不携带药效或新抗性记录。两代鞭子的工作端对非敌对有效链目标免闪避、不新增近战威胁，攻击者仍可能失手。

### 正式资源与反馈

九把武器均有专用美术。工业短鞭与太空丝鞭具备物品、待机、挥击及回落图，统一握持点并带命中残影。远程紫色尾迹、受药倒置紫心与短矛专属命中泛光已接入。工业两枪使用气流声；太空两枪保留已认可的原版充能武器声音。两代鞭子的音效试验已撤销，后续优化暂缓。

### 欢愉恩赐剂量调整

1.7 的合法恩赐完成按主人当前身体的 RJW 产液器官计算负荷：有效产量限制 **7.5～250**，对应 **3～100 个负荷百分点**；参考产量 **20 对应 8 点**。采用器官产液能力，不统计本场实际转移量。普通产出者仍输入 2 点；需求补充、成瘾强化、恶堕、部位开发与独立反馈沿用原规则。
