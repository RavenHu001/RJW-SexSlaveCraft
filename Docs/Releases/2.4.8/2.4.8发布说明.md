## English

New work targets **RimWorld 1.7**; **1.6/Common remain frozen at SSC 2.4.1**. Download from [v2.4.8](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.8); the version-aligned build, full-gate and upload receipts are recorded separately.

### Broodmother specialization

- Adds a seventh specialization, **Broodmother**, with ordinary development and an **Ultimate Broodmother** form. New selection or reselection requires Chain stage 3 and completed uterine development. Lowering the stage does not add a new suspension rule for existing progress or achievements.
- Eligible Training, teaching, playing and live births contribute through their respective progression rules. Staged effects include fertility, pregnancy duration, education/play efficiency and Beauty; ordinary and final effects follow current eligibility. Gel processing and implantation preserve personality-based progress, while uterine settings stay with the body.
- Preconception settings choose offspring sex, species source and an initial-imprint preset. They unlock at 20%, 50% and ordinary completion respectively. Settings can be prepared with fertility off, but cannot be edited during pregnancy or labor. Species selection does not replace native gene/xenotype or litter-size rules.
- Actual offspring generation reads current eligibility and settings; existing pre-generated pawns are not retroactively changed. The initial imprint itself grants no SSC role or Chain. Live-birth progression is settled once per birth scope.
- The final **Conception** ability targets an eligible adult, non-hostile humanlike pawn on the same map. The caster approaches and completes a shared RJW scene before conception is committed to the caster. Only success starts the seven-day cooldown; interruption grants neither conception nor success cooldown. Unfinished scenes are canceled on load. This replaces the earlier separate cast/follow-up design.
- Names, four-language text, Beauty additions and ordinary/final label colors have scoped maintainer acceptance. Self-cloning/replicas are a separate deferred project, not an unfinished Broodmother feature.

### Personality imprints and ownership

Three mutually exclusive imprint forms govern initial and differentiated personality states. Seed use and the first successful new binding follow distinct qualification rules; differentiated forms lock the corresponding SSC role, with age-limiting, immunity and stat effects. Restoring an old Chain does not replay differentiation.

Imprints follow personality extraction, processing and implantation; preconception settings follow the uterine body. Current Masters cannot undergo personality extraction, and residual preparation is removed without introducing new unbinding behavior. Old gel compatibility retains its existing scope.

### Frequency attributes, Hollow needs and trainer assignment

- **Frequency sources:** both existing PNA effect families gain five factors, ×1.10/×1.25/×1.50/×1.75/×2.00. Five body-development paths contribute staged offsets, totaling +0.50 when all five are complete. These compose with the personal sensitivity level and existing imprint factor through the same general stat; they do not create a new timer or guarantee proportionally more actual events.
- **Hollow needs:** the completed Hollow state disables Joy, Comfort, Beauty, Outdoors, Indoors and RoomSize needs. Other needs, physical effects and personality rules remain unchanged; removal restores needs according to current eligibility, not their old satisfaction values.
- **Trainer convenience:** assign an eligible pawn and enable the Training work category in one explicit action, or enable it for the current assignee. New enabling uses priority 3; an already enabled priority is preserved. Closing the work category keeps the assignment and does not silently re-enable it. Both the Training tab and bond overview share these actions; multi-trainer assignment remains deferred.

### Validation and remaining scope

The above implementation batches have scoped maintainer in-game acceptance. The latest Broodmother/imprint maintenance ran 152 related cases plus a separate 4-case UI recheck; trainer changes ran 166 identity cases, 233 overview cases and 3 temporary native-interface checks. Resource-only changes used lightweight native/XML checks. These overlapping, dated results are **not a new full release gate**.

The registered runner still has 50 suites. The 2.4.7 release's 6333-case result remains historical; this registration does not infer a new full-case total from additions and deletions. Those registration-time results precede formal release preparation; actual rebuild, full gate and upload results are recorded separately.

Replicas/personality division, the unified stimulation/frequency framework, room/range effects and other future plans remain unimplemented or deferred. Traditional Chinese/English/Russian romance display, source-preference checks, unreported mod combinations and balance retain their independent boundaries.

## 中文

新增仅面向 **RimWorld 1.7**，**1.6／Common冻结SSC 2.4.1**。本版下载入口为[v2.4.8](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.4.8)，版本对齐、正式门禁与上传回执分别记录。

### 孕母特化

- 新增第七方向**孕母**及**终极孕母**。新选／切回要求锁链阶段3、完整子宫开发；阶段下降不额外停用既有培养与成果，其他当前资格仍按原规则判断。
- 合格调教、课堂、陪玩及活产沿各自规则培养，阶段提供生育、孕期、教育／陪玩效率及魅力效果。普通与终极效果按当前资格生效；人格凝胶保留人格进度，子宫设置留在身体。
- 孕前可设置性别、物种来源及后代初始印记，依次在20%、50%和普通完成开放。生育关闭时可预设，孕期／临产禁改；物种预设不取代原基因／异种遗传及胎数机制。
- 真正生成后代时读取当前资格与预设，不追补已存在的预生成角色。初始印记本身不授予SSC身份或锁链；活产培养按一次分娩范围结算一次。
- 终极技能**受孕**选取同图符合条件的非敌对成年类人对象，实际走近并正常完成同场RJW任务后，才提交施法者受孕；成功后起算7天冷却，中断不受孕、不扣成功冷却。未完成场景读档取消。此前独立读条与独立后续方案已撤除。
- 名称、四语、魅力及普通／终极配色均已获对应实机认可。自克隆／复制体独立立项并暂缓，不作为孕母未完成项。

### 人格印记与归属

三态人格印记互斥，初始态与分化态分开处理。种子使用与首次成功的新绑定各有资格规则；分化态锁定对应SSC身份，并提供年龄上限、免疫及属性效果。恢复旧链不重放分化。

印记随人格提取、加工及植入流转，孕前设置随子宫身体保存。当前主人身份禁止人格排泄，残留准备会清理，不因此新增解绑操作；旧凝胶兼容保留既有范围。

### 频率属性、空壳与调教员操作

- **频率来源补全：** PNA两种既有药效按五档提供×1.10／×1.25／×1.50／×1.75／×2.00；五部位开发提供阶段加值，全满合计+0.50。与个人敏感度和原印记来源沿通用属性合成，不新增计时器，也不保证真实事件数量同比增加。
- **空壳需求：** 完成态禁用娱乐、舒适、美观、户外、室内、空间六项需求，其他需求、身体效果与人格规则保留。状态移除后按当前资格重建需求，不恢复旧满足度。
- **调教员便捷操作：** 显式“指定并启用调教工作”或为当前指定者就地启用；新启用优先级3，原已启用优先级保留。关闭工作不清指派，也不后台重开。调教页与关系概览共用操作，多人指派继续暂缓。
