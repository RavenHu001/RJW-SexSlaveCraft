## 中文

### 猫狗培养与宠物共同规则

- 开放猫普通特化选择。SSC 性奴完成调教和猫研究、且没有猫／狗／兔终极成果后可选择；绑定主人和指定锁链阶段不作为选择门槛。当前普通猫成功亲昵实际主人时额外获得 **1 个百分点**；普通培养已完成、已有宠物终极或当前方向不是猫时不发放。
- 猫／狗／兔进入同一互斥组，奶牛独立。普通方向切换保留各方向历史进度，普通收益随当前方向生效；终极宠物的效果、技能与亲昵资格可以跨方向保留。有任意宠物终极成果后，不能重复选择或终极化另一个宠物方向。兔选择入口仍禁用。
- 凝胶加工同时检查候选原料与消耗前资格，阻止冲突或未完成培养的人格被错误消耗。沿用已有凝胶等级与配方映射，清理加工产物中的普通宠物标签，保留其他方向成果、人格数据和历史。
- 狗普通动物训练成长在确认真实 `TrainAttempt` 互动、原版动作正常返回后结算；训练随机失败仍算实际尝试，未发生互动则不发奖。已完成互动后取消收尾不会撤销奖励。现有基础增量与培养倍率保持。

### 终极猫：安抚共鸣

- 一个技能按钮、一套冷却。选择同地图其他**玩家阵营人形角色**；排除自身、动物、机械体和不能保持清醒的目标。睡眠者可唤醒，清醒但倒地的目标仍可接受。
- 释放者走至目标同格，以原版预热方式让目标短暂停留，完成 **两秒**读条后生效。支持征召与未征召施放，使用原版目标提示、选中圈，并显示进度与爱心。
- 生效时目标有当前 `MentalState`，直接恢复该状态，不限制类型或成因；没有当前精神状态时，给予 **+8 心情、全局工作速度 ×1.10，持续 30,000 tick（半天）**的鼓舞。重复鼓舞刷新持续时间，不叠加。紧张性昏迷不额外查找或删除 Hediff。
- 两种效果共用 **60,000 tick（一天）**冷却。技能不发亲昵培养经验，也不触发亲昵后续。冷却按人格保存，提取、加工和植入不会重置未到期冷却。

### 终极狗：定向驯导

- 同地图选择符合条件的无阵营或玩家阵营动物；排除死亡、无意识、精神状态异常及树精目标。走至目标同格，两秒预热后生效，显示原版目标提示、驯导图标、读条与互动特效。
- 对野生动物调用原版直接驯服流程，不需要驯服食物；对己方动物，按原版优先级**完整完成一个当前有效、已勾选的训练项目**，包含衰退后的补训，保留动物主人。
- 生效时重新检查目标与训练资格；取消、目标失效或无有效训练项目不新增冷却。技能各自冷却 **60,000 tick（一天）**，不额外领取普通动物工作成长或日常训练奖励。
- 新终极状态使用带组件的 Hediff；旧存档的普通终极狗 Hediff 保留，通过读档与维护入口补齐技能，不要求替换旧状态。人格迁移保留未到期技能冷却。

### 可见亲昵与自愿后续

- 普通及有效终极宠物在实际绑定主人附近约 **2.9 格**、双方空闲清醒且满足行为条件时发起自动亲昵。接近主人后停留两秒，显示双方朝向、读条和爱心，保护双方已有命令、队列与新指令。
- 成功亲昵给予主人 **+5 心情，持续六小时，最多一份记忆**；完成后冷却一天。中断不发奖；猫的特色经验只按普通猫资格发放。
- 成功后，成年普通及有效终极猫狗在 SSC 许可与 RJW 身体、自愿、欲望及冷却条件允许时，由**宠物向实际主人**进行一次 **20%**后续判定。双方生理年龄至少 18 岁且符合 RJW 成年条件。
- 判定保存且仅执行一次，不换目标、不交换主动角色，也不提供强制替代。未命中、拒绝或启动失败均保留亲昵结果；准备中新命令取消后续并保留新任务。后续沿用 RJW Quickie 流程，不增加配对专属冷却或独立亲昵经验。**该后续已实现并提交，实机验收待完成。**

### 界面、说明与继承内容

- 猫狗进度统一一位小数，达到共同 **0.999** 完成门槛后显示“培养完成，待终极化”，与终极成果分开显示。悬停可查看人格凝胶提取、雕刻台加工与植入步骤；普通完成不会直接授予终极技能。
- 更新简中、繁中、英文、俄文研究与终极化引导，同步语言镜像、四份玩家指南、模组介绍、规划与发布文档。
- 统一日常调教／完整绑定仪式基础特化经验已随 **2.3.6** 发布；自我调教与任务交接修复已随 **2.3.5** 发布。这些内容继续包含在本版，未重复计为 2.3.7 新增功能。
- 支持版本仍为 RimWorld **1.6**，必需依赖仍为 Harmony 与 RJW。绑定仪式使用 Ideology 仪式系统。旧 RimTalk 继续暂停；兔重设计、手动亲昵、阶段强化和独立技能图标留待后续。

## English

### Training and shared pet rules

- Ordinary Cat selection is enabled for SSC Sex Slaves with Training and Cat research completed and no final Cat, Dog or Rabbit specialization. Selection does not require a bound master or a specific Chain stage. Successful affection with the actual master gives a current ordinary Cat **1 percentage point**; completed or finalized pets and other current directions receive no such reward.
- Cat, Dog and Rabbit form one exclusive group; Cow remains separate. Switching ordinary paths retains historical progress and activates only the current ordinary path. Final pet effects, abilities and affection eligibility persist across direction changes. Any final pet blocks another pet selection or finalization. Rabbit selection remains disabled.
- Gel processing checks both candidate materials and eligibility before consumption, preserving unrelated outcomes, personality data and history while removing ordinary pet tags from the final product.
- Ordinary Dog training progress settles only after an actual native `TrainAttempt` interaction returns normally. A random training failure still counts as an attempt; no interaction means no reward. Canceling the finishing delay after an actual attempt preserves its reward. Existing progress amounts and multipliers remain.

### Final Cat: Soothing Resonance

- One button targets another **player-faction humanlike pawn** anywhere on the same map. The caster, animals, mechanoids and targets unable to stay awake are excluded. Sleeping targets can wake; conscious downed targets remain eligible.
- The caster moves onto the target cell for a **two-second** vanilla warmup that briefly pauses the target. Drafted and undrafted use are supported, with native targeting feedback, a progress bar and hearts.
- At completion, any current `MentalState` is recovered, regardless of type or cause. Otherwise, an eligible target receives **+8 mood and ×1.10 global work speed for 30,000 ticks (half a day)**. Reapplication refreshes duration without stacking. Catatonic Hediffs receive no special search or removal.
- Both outcomes share a **60,000-tick (one-day)** cooldown. The ability grants no affection progress or follow-up. Outstanding cooldowns travel with the personality through extraction, processing and implantation.

### Final Dog: Directed Taming

- Targets eligible factionless or player-owned animals on the same map, excluding dead, unconscious, mentally unstable animals and dryads. The caster moves onto the target cell and performs a two-second warmup with native targeting, a taming icon, progress and interaction effects.
- Wild animals use the native direct-taming path without food. On player-owned animals, the skill **fully completes one currently eligible checked training item** by native priority, including retraining after decay, while retaining the animal's master.
- Eligibility is checked again when the effect fires. Cancellation, invalid targets or no eligible training item add no cooldown. The ability uses its own **60,000-tick (one-day)** cooldown and grants no additional ordinary animal-work progress or daily-training reward.
- New final Dog states use a Hediff with components. Existing saved plain final Dog Hediffs are retained and gain the ability through load and maintenance paths. Personality transfer preserves outstanding cooldowns.

### Visible affection and consensual follow-up

- Eligible ordinary and final pets detect an idle, awake actual bound master within about **2.9 cells**, approach and perform two seconds of visible affection with facing, progress and hearts. Existing orders, queued tasks and new commands are protected.
- Successful affection grants the master **+5 mood for six hours, one memory maximum**, followed by a one-day affection cooldown. Interrupted affection grants nothing; Cat progress follows ordinary-Cat eligibility.
- After success, eligible adult ordinary and final Cats/Dogs make one **20%** roll to initiate a consensual follow-up **from the pet to its actual master**. Both must be at least 18 biological years old and meet RJW adulthood, body, desire, cooldown and mutual consent checks, plus SSC permissions.
- The decision is saved and made once. There is no alternate partner, role swap or forced fallback. A miss, refusal or startup failure preserves affection rewards; new commands during preparation cancel the follow-up and retain the new task. The scene uses the RJW Quickie flow without a custom pair cooldown or separate affection progress. **This follow-up is implemented and committed; in-game acceptance remains pending.**

### Interface, documentation and retained features

- Cat/Dog progress uses one decimal place and shows “Training complete; ready to finalize” at **0.999**, separately from final outcomes. A tooltip explains extraction, sculpting-table processing and implantation; ordinary completion does not directly grant a final ability.
- Research text and finalization guidance cover Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Russian, with synchronized language mirrors and updated player guides, overview, plans and release documentation.
- Shared daily Training/full Binding Ritual specialization progress was already released in **2.3.6**; self-training and handoff fixes were released in **2.3.5**. They are retained rather than counted again as new features.
- RimWorld **1.6**, Harmony and RJW requirements remain. Binding Rituals use Ideology's ritual system. Legacy RimTalk remains suspended. Rabbit redesign, manual affection, stage enhancements and dedicated ability icons remain future work.

## 安装 / Installation

- 支持 RimWorld 1.6，按既有顺序加载 Harmony、RJW 和本模组。退出游戏后，将 ZIP 内唯一的模组文件夹解压到 `RimWorld/Mods/`；更新时先移出旧模组文件夹，避免残留文件。
- Use RimWorld 1.6 with Harmony and RJW loaded before this mod. Close the game, move the previous mod folder out of `RimWorld/Mods/`, then extract the enclosed mod folder there.
- 校验附件为相邻 `.zip.sha256`；发布包内 `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` 记录源码提交、DLL 重编译状态、当次完整回归结果和有效载荷哈希。The adjacent `.zip.sha256` verifies the ZIP; its manifest records source commit, rebuilt-DLL status, current regression results and payload hashes.
