# SSC 2.5.3 · Unbound Slaves and Bond Release / 无主性奴与解绑

## English

**2.5.3 development milestone for RimWorld 1.7**, registered on 2026-11-21. It groups development after the 2.5.2 registration commit `7e5d17a9`. The public package is SSC 2.5.1, which includes SSC 2.4.1 for RimWorld 1.6. [Download 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **Formal unbound-slave progression.** Unbound and bound slaves share four progression stages. Unbound pawns have their own stage effects, moods, market-value multipliers, automatic sensitivity and behavior rules. Relevant specialization gates, body development, PNA addiction immunity and culture checks recognize the shared progression.
- **Submission Breakdown and binding.** Breakdown is enabled by default for new or missing settings, while saved choices are respected. Eligible pawns that remain at full Corruption can enter it after cooldown. Approved Training can establish an initial bound or unbound stage, bind an existing unbound pawn at their exact progress, or continue their progression. Daily Training grants ordinary rewards, with new bonds established through approved breakdown outcomes or Binding Rituals.
- **Unbound targets in rituals.** An eligible SSC Master can bind an unbound target while preserving the target’s exact progress. An eligible Training Officer can advance the target’s unbound progression. The shared vanilla-enslavement setting covers both ritual and relevant breakdown outcomes.
- **Release and loss of a Master.** Manual release, confirmed Master death and invalid former-owner recovery convert a bound pawn to unbound status at the same progress. Release applies a two-day effect with work speed ×0.75 and mood −8. New binding is blocked while that effect lasts.
- **Personality Gel and timers.** Gel preserves unbound progression and the remaining release-effect time. The timer pauses during gel storage and processing, then resumes after implantation. Implantation checks the former Master’s current eligibility. Breakdown duration, cooldown and release effects use vanilla disappearing-health components for their timers.
- **Fixes and four-language text.** Corrects stage market values and development-button progress transfer, recognizes formal unbound slaves in culture checks, prevents automatic Training from repeatedly selecting an already downed target, and fixes breakdown Training aborting when the receiver becomes downed. Unbound states, release effects, request options and shared sensitivity descriptions have finalized four-language text.

PR #48 passed a development-candidate All run of **52 suites and 6007/6007 cases**, with both resource mirror checks passing. In-game acceptance is recorded by batch on RimWorld 1.7.5014 rev1195. Formal 2.5.3 build, package validation and release results will be recorded separately. [Validation and remaining checks](2.5.3版本验证.md)

## 中文

**2.5.3 开发版，2026-11-21 登记，面向 RimWorld 1.7。** 纳入 2.5.2 登记提交 `7e5d17a9` 之后的开发。当前公开安装包为 SSC 2.5.1，包内 RimWorld 1.6 内容为 SSC 2.4.1。[下载 2.5.1](https://github.com/RavenHu001/RJW-SexSlaveCraft/releases/tag/v2.5.1)

- **正式无主性奴与阶段成长。** 有主和无主共用四阶性奴化进度。无主角色具有独立的阶段属性、持续心情、市值倍率、自动敏感度和行为规则。相关特化门槛、身体开发、PNA 成瘾免疫及文化判定接入共用阶段。
- **奴性爆发与绑定。** 新建或缺失设置时默认开启奴性爆发，已有保存选择继续生效。符合条件的持续满恶堕角色可在冷却到期后发作。特批调教可首次形成有主或无主阶段、按精确进度绑定已有无主角色，或继续培养。日常调教结算普通收益，新绑定通过获批爆发分支或绑定仪式建立。
- **无主目标的仪式。** 合格 SSC 主人可将无主目标按原始进度绑定，合格训导官可推进其无主进度。原版转奴设置统一覆盖仪式及符合条件的爆发结局。
- **解绑与主人失效。** 主动解绑、确认主人死亡或恢复时发现原主人失效，会按原阶段和进度转为无主。转换后给予两天后效，工作速度 ×0.75、持续心情 −8，期间禁止新绑定。
- **人格凝胶与计时。** 凝胶保存无主进度和解绑后效剩余时间，存放及加工时暂停，植入后续计；恢复有主关系时核对原主人的当前资格。爆发时长、冷却和解绑后效改用原版健康组件倒计时。
- **修复与四语定稿。** 修正阶段市值与开发绑定按钮的进度交接，文化戒律识别正式无主性奴，阻止自动调教反复选择已倒地目标，并修复爆发调教中受方倒地导致中断。无主状态、解绑后效、请求选项和共用敏感度描述均落实四语定稿。

PR #48 的开发候选通过整仓 **52 套、6007／6007 项**及两项资源镜像检查。实机验收按 RimWorld 1.7.5014 rev1195 的各批候选登记，2.5.3 正式构建、安装包和发布结果另行记录。[验证与保留事项](2.5.3版本验证.md)
